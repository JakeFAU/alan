## 1. Quantified claims and scope

The original question remains:

```text
forall X in A_op:
  (exists Rw. W_N^sigma(X,Rw)) and Progress_N(X)
    => exists Rg subset States(X) x States(K1).
         ARG1Compatible_N(X,Rg), under E_N.
```

For P_boundary this unrestricted implication is **open**, including when choices
are F_event-visible. Matching complete histories does not by itself make their
account a function of the original X state. The corrected sufficient result is:

```text
forall original X in A_op and fixed supplied state/edge maps alpha, lambda:
  Account_N(X; alpha, lambda)
  and (exists Rw. W_N^sigma(X,Rw)) and Progress_N(X)
    => exists Rg subset States(X) x States(K1).
         ARG1Compatible_N(X,Rg), under E_N,
         with X's fixed original demand read through alpha.
```

The certificate below is an explicit additional **representation hypothesis**
for this theorem. Its ticket transition laws come from unchanged K1; existence
of a faithful state/edge representation for every X has not been derived from
those laws. It is sufficient, not shown necessary. It does not change X, add an
input, or assume ARG-1 guard stability. Rg may replace Rw's pairs/paths; every
included `(x,k)` must still handle every actual enabled X move from that pair,
with immediate bilateral declared-environment service and related successors.

D_N is the depth2/cap3/residual I=R3/q restriction of D_full. Full W, Progress and,

corollary for the sufficient class; a profile certificate says nothing about

original S as sole q, stable authority/bindings, open eligible callers, no

## 2. Bridge A: guards, representation and a conditional normalizer

### Fixed reference paths and current guards

Every [reference transition](../../../research/substitution/model.go) at lines
181–426 except `choose_local`/`choose_helper` carries a mandatory ordered fact:
original commitment, exact admission/debit/request creation, request consumption,
reply commitment/disposition, terminal/freeze, publication, delivery, selection,
pending receive, q debit or closure. Preserve original identities, multiplicity,
payload, presence and exact reasons; successor Freeze supplies terminal facts
when coarse Event.Status is empty. F_event therefore permits the single matching
fixed event or empty private stutter. F_fact can additionally hide at most one
choice: both choices leave `choose`, with no return rule. No extra helper round
trip, selected consumption or charge can be erased by endpoint equality.

Current J/protocol guards use commitment/disposition predicates, source terminal/
closure, ledger/depth, request/reply/publication/delivery/selection facts and
fixed J/S/caller control. See [model.go](../../../research/substitution/model.go)
lines 138–177, 222–259, 331–423 and
[invocation_join.go](../../../research/substitution/invocation_join.go) lines
124–145, 174–225. Neither choice changes these. A possible or excluded
uncommitted helper has no intent to authorize/admit. At the post-input choice
region only q/closure can independently act in the local environment, and both
commute with either choice. Thus extra choices preserve the relevant current
guards; intermediates need not be related endpoints.

Exact future demand is different: local choice lowers U. The frozen
[weak-path definition](01-weak-contract.md) §7 and
[N R1](../phase1n/evidence/reviews/reference-r1.md) lines 148–155 require equal
demand at related endpoints and preservation of current guard dependencies at
weak intermediates. Actual semantic exclusion is not a demand-preserving private
stutter, even when its label is hidden. Accepted admission/debit/member/request
creation and terminal/freeze retain their original atomic groups. Adjacent
bookkeeping in a matching path creates no durable checkpoint or permitted
service inside an original transaction.

### Representation attempt and the precise failed quotient

The ticket laws have direct support in
[K1](../phase1i/01-substitution-contract.md) lines 44–49, 94–102, 153–176: fixed
initial e/h/q, possible-to-committed commitment, once-only spend or exact refusal,
local exclusion after input/before no-helper mode, helper selection before its
mode continuation, and no resurrection/refund. W item4 imports these joint
semantic laws, not merely a scalar budget inequality. But K:218–225 and frozen
W:244–247 quantify over **original candidate states**. Recording lawful accounts
on histories does not automatically make them projections of those states.

Two distinct quotient obligations apply. First, an accounted extension with
projection pi to original X preserves operational simulation when every original
edge lifts from every state over its source, all extension edges project to
original edges with the same atomic facts, and environment steps remain
immediate. From a projected related pair, choose its accounted witness once,
lift the actual edge, match it and project the successor. This proves transfer,
but not that exact demand is an observation on original X.

Second, an original-state observation D_X preserving every accounted demand
D_hat exists **if and only if** D_hat is constant on every reachable fiber of
pi: `pi(u)=pi(v) => D_hat(u)=D_hat(v)`. Necessity follows by evaluating D_X at
the common state; sufficiency defines D_X(x) from any witness over x. Only demand
must be constant for this factorization lemma, not the whole history. A fixed
full accounting record on each original state is a stronger sufficient route
used by the simple normalizer below; it is not claimed necessary. Together
with fixed edge meanings, its initial/update laws descend directly.

Alternatively one needs a proved state-respecting selection preserving original
accounting, initial coverage and every successor. Selecting maximum/minimum
demand or only a favorable history does not preserve all original values. The
unchanged weak clauses have not supplied factorization or that selection for
arbitrary P_boundary X. The independent quotient follow-up agrees with R1-1 on
this exact distinction; the claim here is the elementary quotient lemma, not
agreement among authors as evidence of universal representation.

R1's separating schema explains the failed inference. One lawful prefix excludes
h; another leaves h possible. They merge into the same raw state with only a
no-helper mode action left, performing exclusion with that action on the second
history. Neither history resurrects h and there is no helper edge to prune.
Yet their demands differ at the merge. Checking every outgoing edge on both
histories preserves transition behavior but does not give that raw state one
original demand. A relation on `(x,history-account)` is not yet the requested
relation on x. This is a representation diagnostic, not a fully qualified
candidate, impossibility theorem, counterexample or demonstrated specification
gap. Another valid state-respecting abstraction might exist; it is not supplied
by that history argument. Authorization-history extension in C cannot repair
this: C is expressly an added retained protocol history on a supplied coarse
relation, whereas the missing account must first respect the coarse X domain.

### A locally checkable state accounting certificate

Fix X's original reachable states/edges and its named public projection P_K
from W. `Account_D(X; alpha, lambda)` consists of one supplied map alpha on
those **same states**, and one supplied annotation lambda on those **same
edges**, for every configuration in D. They interpret original accounting;
neither adds states/transitions nor changes their guards or labels. A map may
use all of X's state; no canonical map from its private PC alone is required.
They are fixed for the candidate, independently of a proposed Rw or Rg.

Alpha records the original e/h/q ticket states (absent q is excluded) and the
original undecided/local/helper branch annotation. U(alpha(x)) is precisely the
possible/committed original tickets, with K's original costs. If X already has a
declared accounting projection, alpha must agree with it; a stale private flag
cannot be relabeled as, or used to replace, that projection. The certificate
requires the following finite checks on the supplied reachable graph:

1. **Initial coverage and state consistency.** Every original initial state has
   exactly K's original tickets and undecided branch. Each state receives one
   accounting record, identical at every visit. Its commitment/admission facts,
   original identities and actual ledger agree with P_K and original spends.
   A local-only implementation still begins with h possible. No future-language
   analysis or retrospective reduction of entitlement defines U.
2. **Original edge accounting.** Lambda names that edge's original accounting
   update(s) in their fixed order: no update; local/helper semantic choice;
   original e/h commitment; accepted/rejected admission; or q spend. The source
   alpha and the original action's ordered P_K facts satisfy the corresponding
   K rules at each specified update, giving the target alpha. Pure private work
   changes no account. Input, reply, consume, terminal, publication/delivery and
   closure keep tickets except for
   an already specified associated K update; closure alone excludes nothing.
   All identities, ledger deltas and exact refusal/subtree exclusion remain tied
   to the original action. There is no excluded-to-possible/committed transition.
3. **Joint branch/mode legality.** Local choice is post-input with h uncommitted;
   it excludes h before a no-helper mode action. Original helper choice does not
   erase h demand and cannot roll back to local. Helper commitment requires its
   possible ticket and that branch, possibly its already specified adjacent
   semantic choice; e's helper-mode continuation requires original h selection.
   Refused-helper failure retains its commitment/refusal/selection history and
   is not local exclusion. These checks use P_K's consumed/committed facts and
   alpha, not a reference-state relation or computed response correctness.
4. **All edges and original groups.** Check every reachable original edge,
   including every outgoing edge at a merge, at private boundaries and after
   closure. The same lambda applies at every occurrence of an edge; no past
   account or update is selected per future trace. An original action containing
   a semantic choice may keep that existing correspondence, but lambda cannot
   fuse/split original events or put service inside an atomic group. Visible
   choices remain visible under F_event. The certificate does not grant a new
   private instruction, callback, input or choice to X.

These checks are on X's own accounting fragment. They require neither equal
reference demand, bilateral readiness, current-guard stability, a successful
ARG-1 relation, nor candidate progress. W still checks payload/protocol behavior
and environment readiness; Progress still checks nondivergence and maxima.
Account therefore states an independent sufficient representation condition,
not the desired theorem as a premise. Finite checking presumes the supplied
graph includes all original reachable edges; no cutoff or hidden edge is allowed.
No certificate has been executed or claimed for a new candidate in this task.

The R1 merge annotation with different original records at the same raw state
fails item1 even though every continuation may be lawful. Supplying two maps
conditioned on the history is not this certificate. If its mode edge needs a
history-dependent update it also fails item4. This does not prove no alternative
faithful certificate exists for a fully specified X. It accurately identifies
what the earlier history proof failed to establish.

### Conditional proof on original state pairs

Under P_stage, W's definition already requires demand/stage equality through
state projections on its original pairs. Retain Rw; the fixed guard argument
and B/C complete the source derivation for that strong reading. Do not infer
P_stage from the old erasure checker.

Under F_event/P_boundary plus Account, generate a subrelation of Rw from aligned
initial pairs by W-successor choices for every required move. Complete matched
visible words include choices; alpha/lambda make their accounting updates fixed
on original states/edges. Induction gives equal demand on each pair. Visible
history matching **without** the state certificate would give history alignment
only, and is not claimed to close unrestricted P_boundary representation.

Under F_fact/P_boundary plus Account, build a relation of triples `(x,k,g)` where
x is an original X state, `(x,k)` is the current Rw witness, and g is a reference
state with the same mandatory facts and U(alpha(x)). Advance k from its existing
pair by W. Maintain g separately from the original initial state as follows:

| Original x / edge as fixed by alpha, lambda and P_K | Maintained g |
| --- | --- |
| Before accepted input | Corresponding entry/receive stage and tickets; exact entry refusal excludes its original subtree. |
| Input consumed, h possible/uncommitted, no mode action | Keep `choose`, even if X's original helper choice has occurred; it changes neither h demand nor mandatory facts. |
| Original local exclusion | Take `choose_local` at that lambda boundary; retain local mode through later account-preserving private work. |
| Original h commitment | From choose take `choose_helper` then the same commitment; retain the original immutable action and zero debit. |
| h committed/admitted/refused/selected | Follow its exact mandatory facts and ticket updates; await original selection before mode/failure. |
| Original mode action and later protocol | Follow the fixed branch-dependent action and suffix, with any existing adjacent choice correspondence preserved atomically. |

Generate triples from every original initial configuration and close under every
required original edge and bilateral environment move. Each transfer is valid
from every included triple:

- No-update private work keeps g. Actual local exclusion is enabled at choose
  by Account. Every future h commitment after it contradicts that same state's
  fixed original accounting transitions; no favorable continuation is removed.
- Helper commitment retains possible h until its matching choice/commit. A
  no-helper mode action has the prescribed local exclusion already, or its
  fixed edge annotation includes it. W supplies the exact mode/payload/terminal
  fact. After h commitment/mode, mandatory history fixes the reference suffix.
- Before resolution, q/closure commute with the choices. Later environment
  guards depend on mandatory facts shared by k and g. W therefore supplies the
  same immediate X counterpart for each g environment event and conversely;
  Account synchronizes its exact ticket/debit/refusal updates. No private step
  is inserted before service. Scarce/refusal/closure cases remain included.
- A legal unresolved local/helper fork is retained at g's choose. A premature
  local reference pair unable to support the helper continuation fails as that
  pair, not as the candidate when another Rg exists. Extra reference alternatives
  do not add an X choice because refinement is asymmetric.

Now set `Rg = {(x,g) | some k has (x,k,g) in the generated triples}`. This is a
relation on **original X/reference states**, with no history coordinate in its
domain. For every `(x,g)`, select any generating k as a proof witness; closure
of that triple covers every actual next X edge and required environment event.
Its successors project back into Rg. At state merges alpha(x) is the same by
hypothesis, so exact demand is independent of the generating triple/history.
The auxiliary k is not a new input or state of X and is not reselected to erase
an inconvenient outgoing edge. This establishes all-future transfer and demand
equality, not merely existence of separately favorable completed traces.

Matched choices may change demand at their semantic boundaries while preserving
current constructor/protocol guards. Fixed admission/debit/create/request and
terminal/freeze groups remain intact; no history expansion is used to introduce
an interleaving. Delayed e selection is already after the branch/mode suffix,
so the construction does not require an early selection. Progress is not derived
from these safety triples.

## 3. Bridge B: caller framing and delayed selection

Owner framing is the independently declared A_op interface, not a consequence
of W's forward simulation. Original caller commitment/selection retain fixed
guards and updates; candidate-private instructions cannot modify them. For the
local caller, reachable original history determines the phase:

```text
closed                         -> cancelled; no commit/selection
open, e uncommitted             -> first original Invoke
open, e committed, unselected   -> original e Await
open, e selected               -> retained original envelope; ended
```

Initial construction and the sole commitment/selection/closure transitions
establish this invariant ([model.go](../../../research/substitution/model.go),
lines 106–143, 230–242, 375–423). Required commitment, delivery, selection and
closure facts therefore determine bilateral caller readiness under either P,
without requiring equality of scratch PCs. This applies on D_full and D_N.

W also forces the caller matches to be immediate. At entry commitment no member
exists; reaching a reference choice requires additional mandatory admission and
input consumption. Before original e selection, e is already refused or
frozen/published, and any chosen helper was consumed before its mode action.
No silent reference work remains at either caller position. The matching path
is the corresponding single caller transition, so W already relates its
immediate successors. By contrast, declared environment admission, reply,
publication, delivery, q and closure readiness is directly bilateral in W at
every private instruction boundary; a preparatory private step cannot postpone
it. `Environment=false` itself certifies neither ownership nor readiness.

J's common fixed phase adds its original input gate before e commitment and its
S-selection gate before e selection. For other events, monotone mandatory e
selected flags forbid an intermediate e selection on a path ending unselected.
For actual e selection, both sides already satisfy the common S gate, and its
match is immediate. Neither early reference selection nor substitution of
delivery for consumption is needed. The
[local projection](../../../research/substitution/callable_join_local.go) lines
10–38 intentionally has a scratch e Await while full J may still await S;
it does not claim the full e selection is already enabled.

### A_graph-only control: G_q

Without owner framing, define G_q as the original K1 graph with only
`outcome_consume(e)` removed whenever the original q debit is enabled. Identity
is a weak relation under every F/P reading: retained edges match identically,
and all declared environment edges remain immediate in both directions.

A reachable D_N answer/local prefix completes e through delivery before q.
The open caller awaits delivered unselected e, e has spent1 of residual3, and q
is still possible. Original K1 permits selection; G_q removes it. No alternative
ARG-1 relation can hide this: mandatory original facts force the same open Await
and delivered-unselected envelope in every related reference state.

Conditional on the reference's progress obligations, this deletion preserves
them on D_full and D_N. Whenever it removes selection, q remains an outgoing
edge; after q or exhaustion of its budget guard the deletion ceases permanently.
The graph introduces no cycle or new maximum, and eventual service does not
permit withholding continuously enabled q forever. With absent/ineligible q,
closure, or designated pending, there is no new blocked selection. This is an
analytic control, not a new executed qualification.

G_q is outside A_op because it changes the original caller guard, and no public
realization is claimed. It also does not break fixed J: by the time J may select
e, S has been selected and its accepted admission already spent q. Thus failure
of local ARG-1 readiness under A_graph is distinct from contextual failure.

## 4. Bridge C: all legal first-authorization histories

Under E_N, a coarse relation using the mandatory facts admits an authorization
extension over **history-distinguished augmented states**. For full J the lemma
is conditional on Bridges A/B supplying coarse constructor transfer. It is not
a theorem for all local closure/retirement histories or for graphs allowed to
inspect/mutate authorization records.

For every legal prefix h and original committed intent i, retain either no
first decision or its actual immutable first record A_h(i), tied to its original
commitment and source prefix. The normalized record preserves intent/operation,
kind/payload/source/target/obligation, generation-qualified source and committed
revision, original binding, revision/generation predicate results, historical
source-open/retired snapshot, disposition and exact reason. Equality is under
one fixed identity renaming; **each run retains its own raw immutable provenance**.
Raw source sequence/ordinal/epoch data is not rewritten to manufacture equal
SQL rows or step counts. See [Authority](../../../internal/executor/store.go)
lines 58–69, [Intent](../../../internal/executor/program.go) lines 80–88 and
[raw replay checks](../../../internal/executor/composition_replay.go) lines
494–526. A concrete runtime correspondence remains a separate obligation.

[AuthorizeIntent](../../../internal/executor/messages.go), lines 103–177, returns
an existing first row before consulting current source state. A new decision
requires its original commitment and no row, then checks revision and generation
before the source retirement/open tests. The original bound reply exception uses
[compositionReplyBinding](../../../internal/executor/invocation.go), lines
123–140; an invocation receives no such exception. Exact refusal reasons follow
the ordered rule, not an unordered Boolean formula.

Take all compatible **legal** history pairs whose coarse endpoints are related,
mandatory words/atomic groups align, first-authorization occurrences align, and
normalized retained records agree. Do not take a Cartesian product of arbitrary
pending/allowed/refused assignments on equal coarse states. The extension is
closed by induction from each already-related pair:

- A new first decision on either side has the same original commitment and row
  absence on the other. Equal source terminal facts and E_N's stable authority/
  binding give the same snapshot and ordered first result. Append it to both.
- A lookup returns the old corresponding record. A later End cannot recompute it.
- A coarse matched step uses the existing path from its current k. Extra choices
  preserve commitment/source facts. Actual commit or terminal changes occur in
  the same mandatory order, preserving captured prefixes and earlier decisions.
- Split admission requires the corresponding retained row on both sides and
  retains the existing current source/scope/depth/budget checks. Later service
  never resets the row; atomic transactions are not split for authorization.

This covers every legal candidate authorization schedule and every later
bilateral authorization from every included pair, including private instruction
boundaries. It selects neither a favorable order nor a new starting reference
state per event. Extra reference choices commute with authorization because
they preserve its particular predicates; this does not prove demand equality.

Let c/a/t/d denote bound reply commit/first authorize/End/admit. Both End statuses
permit `c;a;d;t`, `c;a;t;d` and `c;t;a;d`. Early authorization retains an open
snapshot; late first authorization retains a closed snapshot and uses the actual
binding exception. Prefixes `c;a;t`, `c;t;a` and unresolved `c;t` may share a
coarse state yet require different histories. J's failed End may retain its
present diagnostic; [freeze and publication](../../../internal/executor/invocation_outcome.go)
lines 220–288, 354–373, 426–440, 571–621 preserve that committed evidence.

First invocation authorization after source End instead refuses; an earlier
allowed row also does not waive later current admission guards. Such closed
source invocation schedules are not reachable for required original calls in a
W-conforming E_N composition: O stays open through J selection, J through e/S
selections, and e consumes its chosen helper before its mode action. Do not add
impossible refused dispositions to positive histories merely because the kernel
can represent them in another environment.

## 5. Progress and evidence limits

Progress_N is a separate all-mode/nondivergence premise. The bridge construction
adds at most one reference choice, and at most eight first decisions for the
fixed four possible invocations J/e/S/h and four replies. A decreasing count of
remaining first decisions excludes infinite **new authorization** steps; it is
not a private-program rank or runtime budget. Repeated lookups cannot justify
unfair withholding of required admission/service. Arbitrary private cycles
still need the candidate's own sound rank/SCC-equivalent certificate.

J's finite commitment prefix and original finite S path eventually open its
S-selection gate under E_N. Original entitlement prevents resource starvation;
S admission may occur before or after e admission without q priority. Delaying
e selection withholds no candidate input: e is frozen/refused before publication,
has no post-freeze component work, and has consumed a chosen helper before its
mode action. After the finite delay, the original service obligation resumes.
Answer/fail/absent follow their original envelopes and finite J/O suffixes.
Pending reaches the actual unmatched receive after required consumptions, with
S selected, J awaiting e and O awaiting J; no reply, freeze or release is invented.
Unfair service withholding, a private divergent cycle and designated pending are
distinct. Adequate concrete operational bounds remain a separate premise; the
historical rank or 512-step setting is not a uniform limit on arbitrary X.

## 6. Corrected conclusion, remaining obligation and validation gate

The source derivation supports ARG-1-compatible state relations for the explicit
Account class under either F/P reading, with separate W/Progress_N and E_N.
P_stage already includes its state-level endpoint demand equality, so its own
strong reading retains the direct Rw result. For unrestricted P_boundary A_op,
including F_event alone, **ARG-2 remains OPEN**. W4 supplies original ticket
laws; this work has not proved that every permitted finite representation
admits the required state-respecting account or another suitable relation.
Account is an additional sufficient, locally checkable representation condition,
not shown necessary and not claimed to follow universally from weak conformance.

The exact remaining obligation is to derive from unchanged W/A_op either a
faithful accounting abstraction on the original X states/edges, a conservative
quotient/selection theorem preserving exact demand and all atomic successors,
or a different Rg on that original domain. Legal history accounts and
all-successor coverage alone are insufficient for that derivation, as the R1
merge diagnostic shows. No specification-gap verdict, conforming negative,
fixed-J contextual failure or differing candidate acceptance sets are established.

B and C remain conditional on the supplied coarse relation; C's explicitly
permitted retained authorization histories do not justify silently augmenting
X's demand representation. Full local W/Progress/Account restrict to the profile;
profile-only evidence is not full-contract qualification. Reference/product
premises, A_pub correspondence, concrete operational bounds and manuscript
reconciliation remain separate. The original rejected universal conclusion is
not integrated as an accepted result.

Independent rereview must verify the certificate is independent of ARG-1, its
state/edge maps interpret original bookkeeping rather than invent it, the
triple-to-pair proof covers every successor and original atomic group, and the
P_stage result uses only that explicit stronger reading. Current guards, B/C
and all-mode progress remain supported conditional portions, not repairs for
an absent Account proof.

No new semantic tests, searches, public executions or qualification counts are
claimed. Only after argument review, small supplied-certificate validations may
check the accounted one-private-bit toy, an unresolved local/helper fork,
q/closure and exact all-mode/refusal boundaries. Preserve R1's local-only merge
as a demand-descent diagnostic rather than rejecting it as resurrection; also
include an actually resurrecting merge as a separate invalid-account control.
The six original bound-reply authorization/End orders and retained lookup remain
useful C instances. These are finite clause/transcription checks, not whole-D_N
conformance, A_pub witnesses or proof of the open representation theorem.

No generic solver/candidate campaign is justified by this correction. If a new
weak checker is proposed, the packet's five independent machinery fixtures
remain required. No finite implementation begins before rereview. Any public
execution first needs its own correspondence and common operational bounds;
controller-owned qualification, manuscript reconciliation and phase completion
remain unfinished.
