# A callable rule for one diagnostic relay

Fix one of the two original boundary readings and one of the sixteen requests. Write `a=(id,1−value)` and let `ℓ` identify the original candidate invocation, with its actual request and original links. All statements quantify over those choices. For `T₀[X]=J[X]` and `Tₙ₊₁[X]=R[Tₙ[X]]`, the original-premise implication remains open at positive depth. The rule uses the specified source transitions of R and the independent contract `K_report(b,h;ℓ)`. Public realization was gated on transport. ## Export at the original binding

Take P's complete original configuration, including O as root and immediate caller, original namespace, operations, generations, bindings, initial private data and sequences. Use its independent `J[K1]` and O policy premises and `Conf_N^σ(X)` on `D_N`. P supplies `K_join`, including safety on every legal finite prefix. J commits e then S and selects S then e by their original handles. S returns the original id and zero. On the helper branch e selects the original successful h with the correct present complement before its mode action. J's classifier reads both actual selections. Its answer report copies the singleton `[a]` after e's succeeded/present answer. The absent `failed/member_failed` envelope after e Ends Failure yields `candidate_failed`. The absent `failed/missing_reply` envelope after e Ends Success yields `candidate_missing_reply`. Both error reports have empty answers and J Ends Failure. J sends exactly one report with the request id before End on each completed path. [P proof](../phase1p/02-preservation.md)

Consider the prefix **at first J publication**, including Failure. Every required child selection already occurred before report commitment. Each such selection requires its own original delivery and publication, hence its accepted member ended and its captured replies have final dispositions. Either e selected h or its local branch irrevocably excluded h. This exhausts the only descendant calls, including their commitments and admissions. J's End froze its one outward report, whose accepted disposition is required for the specified present public report. Nothing in the footprint can still compute, admit or consume internally. J's own outward observation can still require service. This argument uses actual joins because the kernel can publish a failed member without joining its descendants. In pending, e consumed its request and any chosen h and has reached the designated unmatched receive. S has been consumed by J. Neither e nor J can publish, J awaits e, O awaits J, and all other productive work is exhausted. P's all-mode progress establishes these maxima. Entry and descendants cost at most four singleton admissions and reach relative depth at most three. Thus `J[X] ⊨ K_report(4,3;ℓ)` **at P's actual binding**, retaining the stronger inner `K_join`.

## Single-constructor rule

In `L(b+1,h+1;ρ,c,d,p)` let r be the fresh member of R. For **every legal entry prefix** of this parent instance that reaches r's child Invoke, assume C satisfies `K_report(b,h;ℓ)` at the resulting `L(b,h;ρ,r,d+1,p+1)`, with its actual nominal binding and initialization. Prefixes that stop earlier need no child premise. Then `R[C]` satisfies `K_report(b+1,h+1;ℓ)` in the former frame.

**Supplying the port.** c commits the original R invocation. Its acceptance atomically charges one `(1,1,0)`, creates r and admits its request. r receives it once. At r's sole child Invoke the same physical root has outside spend `p+1`, residual I=R=b and cap `d+1+h`. r commits the unchanged literal request, executes its finite Await setup and stays open at the original handle until selection, indefinitely in the absence of delivery. Early delivery is retained. The conjunction of the exact eligible Await and matching observation permits selection even on failure. Reaching the Await requires no child publication. Waiting ancestors perform no competing debit, mutation, input, or release. Thus the port supplies the child's rely without assuming which outcome occurs. The child certificate applies in that very execution, with all its private steps retained.

For both entries a first decision is eligible on an immutable undecided intent. Stable revision/generation, open source and unretired subject establish allowance. Current admission separately checks source and enclosing scopes, declared target/template and generation, root catalogue, capability, payload schema, depth and both headrooms. For a member invoker its own enclosing template must also list the child template. r's entry binding ties its own return to c, whereas C's entry and return bind to r. Root ρ and immediate caller are separate roles even when c=ρ at the outer port. Each caller's control precedes End by its original selection, keeping these scopes open independently of the child's progress. [Admission and reply binding](../../../internal/executor/invocation.go)

**Every finite prefix.** Follow r's instruction order. It examines only the outcome actually consumed under the right recipient, provenance, template/generation, obligation and operation. A report id comparison comes later. Presence guards field reads and list iteration guards element reads. With matching id, `succeeded/member_succeeded`, tag `answer` and exactly `[a]`, r copies the selected report and Ends Success. With `failed/member_failed`, either distinct diagnosis tag and `[]`, it copies that report and Ends Failure. The same checks preserve defensive `invalid_outcome`. Malformed, inconsistent or published absent evidence reaching the guard yields `(request.id,invalid_outcome,[])` and Failure. The relay never substitutes mode for evidence or complements the selected answer again. Outside conformance a normalized invalid report can lead O to `invalid_outcome` where direct selection would give `invalid_join_outcome`. A schema-valid authenticated lie may pass the guards. Within conformance the child contract links the selected value to actual evidence for ℓ. Its completed publication is already settled, so even a prefix ending there can be extended to a serviced port maximum by delivering and selecting it. The immutable envelope therefore has the specified mode-consistent fields on that prefix too.

Only after child selection can r commit the one report on its own bound reply and End/freeze. Report value equality preserves the leaf claim. The child's envelope remains in its own history. The relay's committed reply records r as its source, with the first decision's actual source snapshot. Its new public envelope and delivery concern c's original operation for r. A committed original bound reply may first authorize and admit before End, straddle End, or first authorize and admit after either End. Protocol, entry member/run, target caller/generation, obligation, schema and stable revision/generation make the exception applicable. Ordinary invocations still require open sources. A repeated lookup returns its immutable result and leaves pending admission outstanding. At r's first publication its captured reply is admitted, r is frozen, and child settlement already held at the child's first publication and its outward delivery and selection have occurred. This proves settlement on either terminal branch, with only r's outward observation outstanding. Preserve original once-only facts and atomic groups, including End/freeze, acceptance/debit/creation/request and implementation settlement with delivery in one transaction. Prefix reasoning grants no early disposition, consumption or scheduling cut. [Authorization](../../../internal/executor/messages.go) · [Settlement](../../../internal/executor/invocation_outcome.go)

**Service.** Until the child port opens, each next original commitment, first decision, admission and finite entry instruction remains enabled at its turn under the stated guards. A finite maximum cannot stop there and an infinite serviced run cannot withhold that prerequisite. During the child call no outside transition disables a continuously eligible child action. The port preserves all child steps and obligations, including finite caller setup and persistent delivery/selection. If the child projection is infinite it satisfies the child's own service condition. If finite with an infinite erased suffix, an outstanding step at its fixed state, or the next necessary port prerequisite, stays eligible until service. Hence its serviced maximum is one specified by the child. Completed child delivery and exact selection, finite validation/Send and End, first decision and admission of the bound reply, publication, delivery and caller selection follow as their respective prerequisites persist. In pending no matching delivery exists, r remains at its Await and no postselection instruction can run. One extra open wait extends the child's exhausted chain. One entry bounds the new footprint by `b+1` and increases each child depth by one.

**Root corollary.** For any certified C at its actual O port, the fixed O policy supplies that port's entry and wait under the declared root bindings. Its original selection precedes the guarded decision. On `answer` O Ends Success and publishes `succeeded/member_succeeded`. On either diagnosed failure it retains the distinct tag, Ends Failure and publishes `failed/member_failed`. Child settlement already holds and O has no outward reply. In pending O remains at the original Await without selection, decision, End or publication. This gives the corresponding `G_outer` without any access to ℓ by O. ## Conditional induction in the actual ambient frame

One sufficient **additional** discipline is a per-instruction relocation proof for the unchanged X and any owned helper. At creation construct a jointly legal original initialization and a coherent correspondence for private states and all internal operations across the whole run. Preserve literal program constants, request and selected application data. Prove for every reachable private instruction, including tests on stored operation values, that each translated choice/step has a corresponding original step with related successor, and that originally continuously enabled work remains realizable across translated and erased steps while its guards persist. The supplementary `Nom` fragment proves the private clauses for its sorted instructions, literal initialization and opaque handle operations. Programs exposing handle strings need a literal-preserving proof for their actual tests. For each kernel step preserve the original internal operation links and actual service order. Embed the old resource root at the new O and its coincident old J caller at the actual innermost relay. Discharge the catalogue, authority, open-scope and bound-port checks above with adequate limits at both bindings. Subtract k from inner depths and cap, k from both I/R totals relative to P, and k+1 relative to `D_N`. These obligations concern inputs, choices, updates and eligibility, before consulting W or Progress. They do not postulate diagnostic correctness. Alternatively, supply the base export with its stronger J join, slot/branch and progress facts independently at each required actual base frame.

For the relocation route keep **every** actual e/h private step. At each projected `D_N` prefix apply W only at its already-related original pair, with P's finite forward matches and immediate bilateral declared service. Original S acceptance projects to q. J's receive and e/S commitments precede either selection. S has a funded slot even after e/h, an open J, and a finite reply/End/publication chain to J's S selection. This opens the e-selection gate. Earlier e delivery persists and becomes selectable at that Await. A scratch caller's End never closes the actual root. On an infinite actual-X projection, the eligibility clause plus these finite prerequisites and split first-decision/admission service imply original weak service. `Progress_N` excludes infinite private execution before a permitted boundary. On a finite projection with an infinite erased suffix, each enabled original step at the fixed local state persists or has the persistent entry, S, selection or first-decision prerequisite just identified. Thus a serviced stop or tail can only project to a true original maximum. P's argument then yields the base export. No intermittent X choice needs stronger fairness.

Fix total k and its actual `E_k`. Induct on the remaining constructors n, quantifying over each reachable outer prefix with `j=k−n` admitted ancestors, actual caller c at depth j, outside spend j, and the corresponding original operation, namespace and bindings. Claim `T_n` satisfies `K_report(n+4,n+3;ℓ)` in this frame. At n=0 use the supplied export at j=k. For n>0 entry of the outer relay changes `(c,j,j)` to `(r,j+1,j+1)` with n−1 constructors remaining. The induction hypothesis applies there under the same ρ, total allowance and cap. The single-constructor rule gives the claim. At j=0 apply the O corollary. The completed base cannot be stranded beneath an unserved finite return chain by the service argument at each layer. At pending O awaits the outermost invocation, every relay awaits its child and the genuine inner wait persists.

In `E_k`, O is one provisioned root. I=R=k+4, E=0 and cap=k+3. Each relay must be created and receive its request before invoking inward. Thus all k admissions precede J at depth k+1. After J's debit the inner residual is three. e/S are at k+2 and optional h at k+3. Only S acceptance implements the concurrent q debit. At every prefix put `U={r₁,…,rₖ,J,e,S,h}`, `A={accepted original slots}`, `B=U\A`, `u=(1,1,0)`. Every debit is one original once-only acceptance, refusals cost zero and nothing refunds. Thus `spent=|A|u` and `spent+|B|u=(k+4)u`. For each lawful history all possible/committed remaining tickets inject into B by original identity. Exclusion is irreversible, so spare capacity cannot authorize h after local choice. This bounds demand on each history through a merge without equating exact future debits. Local/helper maxima spend k+3/k+4 respectively.

These natural-number bounds concern this finite unary grammar under stable identities, generation/revision, authority, grants, schemas and bindings, with no cancellation, retirement, root closure, extra input, pending release or cross-subtree interference. Concrete representable depth, positive physical activation capacity and adequate per-candidate/per-term step, byte and dispatch limits are separate requirements. The conditional induction supplies neither an all-depth certificate from original `Conf_N` nor a public correspondence or a uniform service bound.
