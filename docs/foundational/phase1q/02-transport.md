## Target and direction

For every original finite operational X, each independently fixed original W witness on all 16 D_N initials and the separate Progress_N premise, project each legal translated prefix back to an actual path of that same X in D_N, and project each serviced maximum back to its appropriate open maximum. Then apply W at the reached original pair, not at a new translated configuration. W checks every outgoing component action from each already related reachable pair through a finite K1 match under the chosen F, and bilateral declared service there immediately. P_boundary permits the established original demand distinctions. The local safety relation includes closure, although the translated positive environment excludes it. A one-sided actual-run projection with service preservation would suffice. An isomorphism of arbitrary component/context graphs, stronger simulation, state-respecting demand function and all-depth W are not original premises. P contract lines 9–25.

* The remaining question is intensional. Does X retain the same **private interpretation** of initial state, actual own/bound operation identity, selected observation and original peer binding after physical relocation? Even under the strict reading that X only receives its own local state, input, selected outcome and ordinary original handle, that last distinction matters. Merely adding fields to its private input for absolute depth, a spending register, caller-kind tag or a secretly selected off-profile initial bit would be a new semantics, not a witness.

## Kernel, lifetime and accounting

Fix k. Use O at 0; r1..rk at depths 1..k, from O inward; J at k+1, e/S at k+2, h (if used) at k+3. Give the actual root k+4 I and R, cap k+3, E0; fixed O separately, adequate per-instance operational limits. No closure, retirement, identity/revision/grant changes, unrelated expenditure, input or release. Each finite entrance follows parent accepted admission/creation, original entry Receive, sole child Invoke. Thus J cannot be admitted until all k relays were accepted. For k=0 this is the original P prefix. Before J, accepted slots are precisely a prefix of the chain, the next is funded. After J acceptance each inner legal prefix has translated spend k+1+i, i the count of accepted e, h, S, unit I/R; relative to E_N subtract k, and to D_N subtract k+1. Only original S acceptance realizes q. New ancestors add no concurrent debit. For an original slot of physical depth d, d+k > 3+k iff d>3; translated cap-headroom comparison of k+1+i against k+4 agrees with i against 3 for each singleton coordinate. All allowed original remaining tickets lie in the complement of actual **accepted** slots, which may include excluded h. This is an upper bound, not a right to commit h. No refund, resurrection or history-specific demand equalization. A private transition legal in original D_N must be established before invoking W's branch/ticket laws there. On those legal prefixes, maxima charge k+3 or k+4. These are symbolic identities of the fixed topology and guard, not finite measured qualification.

Use one fixed role-indexed map for all corresponding constituent identities, including association e→J, S→J, h→e, original run/generation, invocation membership, the actual commit and its operation/handle, selected source and delivery. Preserve original request, application id/value, selected terminal status/reason/presence/payload and causal order/multiplicity. Original commits, first authorizations with retained source snapshot, admissions, request consumption, reply commits and dispositions, End/freeze, publication, delivery and selection retain their actual atomic groups. The old external root-as-resource-anchor maps to the new O resource anchor. The old outside J caller-as-requester/reply-recipient maps separately to the immediate actual relay member, rk, or O if k=0. In particular the old J invoke is by the root with no parent invocation, in the new k>0 case by rk under rk's parent scope. The J entry/reply binds to that caller, and that caller waits on its own original J operation. An injective global map on old names cannot map one old root run to two distinct new run identities. Do not preserve global equality of roles which coincide only in the k=0 frame. A nominal map on internal links, plus the two external embeddings and per-rule port checks, is well-defined for the kernel. It is not a map on arbitrary private data by construction.

## Service and where the attempt stops

Provided the induction yields a real local execution of X, retain every e/h private instruction and every original public/selected fact (including h selection). Project only e/h first-authorization services out of the coarse local graph, and keep their actual later dispositions. For finite k, the next open ancestor's original entry commitment, first decision, admission/create/request and declared entry instructions remain eligible in sequence until serviced; creation admits each one in order. This rules out a finite early maximum or an infinite erased prefix before J/e entry. It uses stable declaration, scope, headroom and continuing eligible service, not a bound on delay. J's receive/commit e/commit S need no response. S admission is independently possible even after e/h accepted. S's request/finite answer/bound reply/End/publication/delivery/actual J selection follow in turn. Delaying already-delivered e selection until S selected withholds no input to e because e is refused or already frozen. Never place a priority on q or anticipate e's h selection.

For an infinite projected run one must show any continuously eligible original local e/h action (including private choices), not just each visible observation, can be serviced unless its eligibility actually ceases; outstanding first decision then admission discharge a coarse service obligation. For a finite projection with infinite erased tail, a still-eligible local step must remain executable across the erased tail or have a persistently serviceable finite prerequisite chain above. This includes the first local step, q and delayed e selection. If the actual-X interpretation reflects/enables the original owned actions for this purpose, Progress_N gives completed/pending boundaries and rules out infinite hidden private divergence. Upon J completion, selection in its actual caller, validation, bound report, End/settlement/publication/delivery repeat outward finitely to O, then O decision/End/root publication; each waiter is open and each outstanding prerequisite persistent. Pending leaves all such waits open with no publication, release, possible new work or remaining service, after S and any h completed. P proof lines 64–104 handles the original case and does not prove the translated local eligibility premise. One-way preservation of selected labels, or even occurrence-only projection, does not reflect maximality when an old enabled private step silently disappears.

**First missing clause.** When e (or h) is created, and at the first private instruction that observes a rekeyed original h-operation or other binding-dependent private value, what are X's permissible initialization and private-read interpretation across the two frames? Does unchanged X instantiate corresponding enabled choices/next-private states under the internal map, with enough eligibility reflection for service? The original profile specifies neither that cross-frame semantics nor another way to show every translated owned transition is a transition of this same X at a legally covered original initial. Equality of the original input and corresponding selected outcome is insufficient in the absence of this specification. The private-step question persists even granting identical fixed initial private defaults and disallowing absolute depth/cap/root/caller queries. Hence failure of the general derivation for k>0, no positive-gate evidence for induction, and no qualified original counterexample here.

A separately named sufficient calculus would prescribe uniform instantiation/operand rules for private states, original request and selected application fields, plus disjoint fresh handle sorts and identity-respecting primitives; prove naturality of initialization and instruction rules and persistence/reflection of enabled private steps, using only adequate operational limits. A concrete raw-string program may instead need an artifact-specific dataflow/equality/size and branch audit. Neither sufficiency route follows from W_N or changes it. A different route could exhibit a single X, establish its permitted original instructions, original W witness including closure/all q choices and Progress on all 16 and both readings, then exhibit a legal E_k failure under that same interpretation. An off-profile depth guard, an unquantified raw-literal example or shifting a graph by definition supplies none of the missing evidence.

## Independent contract for a generic child

A relative child rely can be given at its own entry interface: actual caller has consumed any required input, is open at its lawful original Invoke and then waits on its genuine child handle through selection; the child receives the one typed request, its own real bound reply and outcome route, and owns only its declared finite subtree/effect-free slots. From before its entry admission the residual headroom b and relative depth room h cover its declared joint slot demand including its own entry; no competing admission from outside that subtree after the boundary, no closure/new input or release; stable membership, parent/root catalogue/grants, actual source bindings and adequate instance-specific limits; eventually service persistently eligible local and protocol actions in the retained instance. An observer need not get an instant selection before delivery. Guarantees are all-prefix protocol legality and original links/exact ledger, all-mode exact result/selected cause, completed publication implying all owned obligations/descendants settled and no further owned progress/admissions, pending with a real open quiescent wait. Neither this local rely nor the semantic guarantee assumes relay closure or requires correctness in arbitrary contexts.

An admitted relay under parent headroom b+1/h+1 consumes one unit/depth, receives its one request, declares and invokes exactly the child with the original input, and cannot End/publish, add a sibling/effect, change limits/grants, or supply another input while awaiting its sole actual handle. It discharges all the numerical, ownership, binding and service rely clauses by the above local guard and prerequisite checks. If the supplied child has been proved correct at **this relative interface/instantiation** the relay's one-step conditional transfer is available; original P supplies actual K_join and exact outer facts at k=0 only, so it cannot instantiate that antecedent at all depths without the missing lemma. An authenticated false report does not establish the truth of the original X leaf. At k=0, P's guarantee plus its proof that J first selects e/S and required h and settles its own bound reply before publication gives the stated settlement boundary. Pending exhausts original S/optional h with no enabled continuation through e. Its settled footprint is at most 4 including entry and relative depth 3.

## Discriminator with explicitly hypothetical interpretation

At the instruction after e actually selects its one successful h, consider a finite equality test on the saved handle's string copy against an ordinary constant c. Leave every obligation and source service rule untouched. In a separately specified representation with a determinate finite set of baseline handle spellings and all of them unequal to c, the comparison is always false on the original domain. Route false to the unmodified B continuation. That one finite private branch stutters for B's witness, cannot veto q/closure/protocol service, creates no new cycle, and changes no original fact. It may therefore be analyzed by the old all-mode B witness on that explicitly represented domain. If a lawful re-instantiation of the same component makes its newly generated original h handle equal c, route true on answer to Failure (without an additional Send, after required h selection). The candidate would have the wrong answer there even with perfectly valid funding, authentication and eligible service. If instead private identity is opaque and only structural equality/Await is permitted, the test against the literal is not a source instruction at all. This separates the material missing interpretation from atom bookkeeping. Neither baseline spelling condition, raw-name coverage nor the legal translated realization in this conditional discriminator has been established for original A_op. In particular the raw namespace is generated in the concrete store (store.go:317–321); one sample and one unequal raw spelling cannot establish coverage over excluded or universally quantified names. No original candidate verdict or runtime witness follows.
