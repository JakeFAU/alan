# Phase 1Q — Diagnostic callable interface and qualification freeze

The research question is whether the fixed owning join can export a truthful diagnostic through any finite chain of ordinary, single-child callers. The new type is a local contract for one invocation tree. The separate relocation argument must justify using the *original* candidate premise at a different physical location.

## Premises and types

`Req = {id,value,mode}` ranges over `{1,2} × {0,1} × {answer,fail,absent,pending}`. The candidate response is `{id,value}`. The diagnostic is N's closed `{id,classification,answers}` schema. `classification` is the four-tag enum below (32 bytes maximum). `answers` has at most one original response record and at most 192 canonical list bytes. Integer domain restrictions and exact answer content/cardinality are semantic conditions. [N representation](../phase1n/01-representation.md)

For each `σ ∈ {(P_boundary,F_event),(P_boundary,F_fact)}`, use precisely `Conf_N^σ(X) = (∃Rw. W_N^σ(X,Rw)) ∧ Progress_N(X)` for original finite `X ∈ A_op`. One original-state relation covers all aligned initial states with consistent identity correspondence. From *each* related pair *each* actual component step has a finite ordered match from its related state and related successor. Enabled declared environment moves in either direction match immediately with the same event/ledger change and related successor. Original joint operation/branch/ticket laws hold at all prefixes. Both readings retain mandatory facts. `F_fact` permits the qualified silent reference branch choice. Progress on all four modes, including actual original input and any chosen helper consumption, is a separate premise. Local safety includes the closure option, while local progress and the positive composition exclude closure. The aligned local profile fixes e depth 2, cap 3, I=R3 residual, E0 and one possible q. The independent reference assertion for J[K1] and fixed public O policy retain P's scope. [Weak contract](../phase1o/01-weak-contract.md) · [P contract](../phase1p/01-preservation-contract.md)

`J : (Req → candidate response under K1) → (Req → diagnostic)` and `R : (Req → diagnostic) → (Req → diagnostic)` use original invocation outcomes, including optional response, status, reason and a possible open wait. `O` consumes a diagnostic callable as a fixed provisioned root. For `T₀[X]=J[X]`, `Tₖ₊₁[X]=R[Tₖ[X]]`, the proposed conclusion is `Tₖ[X] ⊨ K_report(k+4,k+3)` and `O[Tₖ[X]] ⊨ G_outer` in `Eₖ`. `J[J[X]]` is ill-typed. The owning instance additionally satisfies `K_join` on the original or successfully transported frame. A relay never acquires ownership of J's two selections.

## Local frame and owned observations

Write `L(b,h;ρ,c,d,p)` for the following open test environment. Parameters include the actual root ρ, fresh original entry operation, caller run c, its physical depth d, and the cumulative prior singleton count p (I and R). At the initial boundary the root is open, the single original caller Invoke is ready but uncommitted, the subtree is fresh, outside spending is p, root I=R=p+b, E=0, and cap=d+h. If c is itself invoked it has its own distinct original parent and outward reply binding. Its ancestry has already been admitted, and no such ancestor freezes, closes or admits anything else during this call. The only outside service up through the child's return is the caller's original commitment, entering its exact wait, delivery and selection, kernel service, and inert lookups or finite private wait setup. No competing debit, sibling, cancellation, retirement, extra input, pending release or other cross-tree mutation occurs. The caller stays open at its original Await once reached, including indefinitely if no outcome is published. After selection the caller may continue outside the child's footprint. `p` counts historical charges, not reservations. The root and immediate caller parameters may coincide at the outer entry.

At each boundary the template and input/output schemas, root and parent catalogues, capabilities/grants, protocol, namespace/route, entry member/port, source run/generation/revision and target/obligation are valid and stable. Descendant entry templates declared by the child have the required bindings and permissions. The external caller and its ancestry stay open while the original selection is outstanding. A conforming child must establish its *own* source lifetimes, internal open scopes and obligations. Finite operational limits are adequate for this particular component, frame and execution profile, including representation sizes; physical activation capacity is positive and separately specified. For progress, continuously eligible semantic work eventually receives service; a finite maximum has no enabled productive step. Repeated lookups cannot fulfill other outstanding service. Environment adequacy never grants a private computation step, branch choice or fabricated outcome.

The test environment has one caller interface, independent of any particular caller program. It supplies the declared request and original invocation. Under service the caller reaches its actual Await on that handle. An already delivered matching outcome stays available. Once both the Await and matching delivery are present, selection stays eligible until served, including for a delivered failed envelope. If the child remains pending there is no publication or matching delivery, and the caller remains open at that Await indefinitely. An invoked member receives its own request, uses its own retained control/data, makes only its declared subtree calls, awaits its matching original observations and replies through its original binding. Its internal computation and descendants belong to its footprint. Kernel decisions, root ledger and caller control stay with their actual owners. The public caller sees its own inputs, handle and delivered envelope and may inspect selected report fields under presence/cardinality guards. It sees no private evidence for leaf truth. This new ownership contract describes the diagnostic port; it makes no determination of which private tests unchanged `A_op` permits across different locations.

`C ⊨_L K_report(b,h;ℓ)` is satisfaction in that **particular** independently specified frame; `ℓ` is a proof index identifying the actual original candidate operation in the owned history. A leaf observation is recorded under its single assigned original identity, request, generation and links, including the distinction between local End and published status/reason. The index and internal evidence do not travel on the wire. An arbitrary child's certificate must establish the following from its actual histories. The relay rule requires the child instance at the frame it supplies. Export from `Conf_N` to that frame is a separate proposition.

* **All legal prefixes, irrespective of future service.** When the caller commits the fresh entry its literal request and original binding are fixed. While awaiting, it consumes only that original outcome, at most once. The component consumes only its own admitted request once when reached. Every produced diagnostic commitment is justified by the actual original leaf evidence in the table below; every published report equals that commitment. Track each layer's original commitment, immutable first decision, current admission, report Send, reply disposition, End/freeze, publication, delivery and actual selection, with the original ordered facts and transaction groups. All invocations have declared, once-only original commitments, exact parent/source/target/operation links, stable generation and original charge, delivery and selection evidence. Availability of a first decision does not imply allowance. The bound-reply exception after either End depends on actual entry binding and stable revision/generation; ordinary first invocation authorization and current admission still require their own guards. Frozen members cannot execute further instructions. The footprint is pure, contains only the entry and its declared owned singleton descendants and has no unaccounted work across the boundary. All actual accepted slots plus all lawful remaining admissions on a continuation fit b (including entry); no owned operation exceeds relative depth h, entry at 1. Every accepted admission atomically debits `(1,1,0)`, creates its member and admits its request. Refusal costs zero. No refund, repeated charge, grafted outcome or inferred authority is permitted.
* **Publication is a settlement boundary.** At the *first* completed public invocation publication, including a failed one, entry has ended and the committed, correctly bound outward diagnostic is admitted. Every owned committed call has a final disposition. Every accepted descendant has ended and published, required deliveries/selections have occurred and all captured reply intents have final dispositions. There is no remaining internal productive delivery, selection, computation, authority/admission or possible new admission in the owned footprint. The entry's own outstanding outward delivery/selection is served by the port; identical historical lookups are harmless. A published failure by itself has no implication about descendant settlement in the kernel. [Publication and freeze](../../../internal/executor/invocation_outcome.go)
* **All serviced maxima on each request.** Write `a=(id,1−value)`. Each completed row of the table requires the actual, correct original selection of the leaf evidence by its owner before diagnosis, exactly one bound diagnostic Send before matching End/freeze, actual allowed first authorization and accepted reply before public publication, then outward delivery and matching selection by the eligible caller. First reply authorization/admission may follow either End. `mode` indexes what conformance must produce; it never supplies selected evidence. The owning base separately establishes original S and any required original helper joins. A relay preserves the witnessed public report of its actual child. Neither authenticity nor equality to a prediction from mode constitutes leaf truth.

| Mode | Original leaf fact for ℓ | Entry report and own envelope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| answer | Success, `succeeded/member_succeeded`, present `a` | `(id,answer,[a])`, Success, `succeeded/member_succeeded`, present |
| fail | Failure, `failed/member_failed`, absent | `(id,candidate_failed,[])`, Failure, `failed/member_failed`, present |
| absent | Success, `failed/missing_reply`, absent | `(id,candidate_missing_reply,[])`, Failure, `failed/member_failed`, present |
| pending | Original input and any chosen helper consumed; actual designated unmatched receive, with no release | No entry report, End, publication, delivery or selection. The original caller remains at its exact await. |

At a pending maximum the owned wait chain to the designated leaf is genuine, all other required owned calls and reply obligations are settled, and no productive service or admissible new commitment/admission remains. There is no input or publication on which to advance the chain. In particular, an endlessly spinning child, an unserved authorization, and a completed child with delivery withheld fail the progress clause. Defensive `invalid_outcome` is outside positive maxima.

## Constructor and caller obligations

A relay consumes its one request and commits exactly one declared child with unchanged literal `id,value,mode`. It waits on that child’s original handle and, upon matching delivery under service, selects the outcome once while still open. Only after that selection does it inspect response presence; only within a present report does it inspect fields, and only within the bounded populated list does it inspect an answer record. Each valid present row requires original report id. It checks exact answer cardinality/content and envelope status/reason. It commits one bound reply before the indicated End. New envelopes belong to the actual sending/ending member. Only the application report value is copied. Rebuilding the bounded list from the actual selected elements is equivalent if its values and order are proved equal. [Original J/schema](../../../examples/callablejoin/join.go) · [O guards and original handle](../../../examples/callablejoin/outer.go)

| Selected immediate-child evidence | R report and End | O decision and End |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present `answer`, singleton `[a]`, `succeeded/member_succeeded` | Exact report, Success | `answer`, Success |
| Present `candidate_failed`, empty, `failed/member_failed` | Exact report, Failure | `candidate_failed`, Failure |
| Present `candidate_missing_reply`, empty, `failed/member_failed` | Exact report, Failure | `candidate_missing_reply`, Failure |
| Present `invalid_outcome`, empty, `failed/member_failed` | Exact defensive report, Failure | `invalid_outcome`, Failure |
| Published absent, malformed or inconsistent evidence | `(request.id,invalid_outcome,[])`, Failure | `invalid_join_outcome`, Failure |
| No publication | No selection or report; open await | No selection or decision; open await |

The malformed row applies to evidence the guard can actually receive; an invalid schema may already be refused upstream. Application-id equality cannot authorize selection. A published absent response after successful End (`failed/missing_reply`) differs from one after failed End (`failed/member_failed`); both use the defensive row. For invalid immediate evidence an intervening relay can turn O's final decision from `invalid_join_outcome` into `invalid_outcome`. On a conforming child O's successful root publishes `succeeded/member_succeeded` and its failed root `failed/member_failed`, retaining each distinct decision. A pending root has no decision, End or publication. Rebinding the unchanged `NewOuter` builder changes its child template declaration and transitive catalogue/compiled identity as needed.

**Base obligation.** At k=0 the export uses P’s same complete nominal binding and initialization for the configuration under consideration, including its actual root/caller O, namespace, run/operation links and initial sequence. Matching only d,p,b,h for a different binding or initialization supplies no such export. In a completed execution of that original frame, J selected original S (present id/zero) then original e; the mode action for a chosen h required its original consumption by e. Each descendant thus ended, captured replies received decisions and its envelope was published, delivered and selected before J's diagnostic. No branch can admit another helper after this point. J's original reply disposition is known at publication even on Failure. At pending, S and any h have already been selected and J awaits unpublished e. This exports the stronger settlement and wait facts using actual control and P, in addition to its `K_join`. Applying this export with a changed caller, depth and absolute spend requires the independently reviewed transport disposition.

**One-step obligation.** In `L(b+1,h+1;ρ,c,d,p)` the relay's accepted singleton entry accounts for exactly one irrevocable charge before its child can be committed. At the child's Invoke the root is unchanged, caller is now the open relay at depth `d+1`, outside spend is `p+1`, residual I/R is b and remaining depth is h. Stable declarations include this immediate-parent catalogue and the child's reply route to the relay. Ancestors only wait. The relay commits the child's literal input and waits on that handle. A matching delivered outcome remains selectable until consumed; pending keeps the relay waiting. This supplies exactly `L(b,h;ρ,relay,d+1,p+1)`. For a child satisfying the new contract *at this frame*, selection of a published outcome already implies its owned settlement. After the relay's own guarded Send, End/freeze and admitted original reply, settlement at relay publication follows on Failure as well as Success. Pending extends the child's exhausted wait by one open await. Its private steps and the child's actual service cannot be erased to create progress. The fixed, finite entry and return prerequisites remain enabled in order, including first decision of the immutable intent and subsequent actual admission. No service premise forces an intermittently enabled X choice. These obligations concern any term admitted by the diagnostic interface at the indicated frame; they alone do not transport `Conf_N`.

In `Eₖ` one root O is provisioned at depth zero, and the common dynamic I=R=k+4, E=0, cap=k+3. Relays enter before J, J at k+1, e and S at k+2, optional h at k+3. After k relay admissions and J admission the inner I/R residual is three. Only actual original S acceptance implements q at arbitrary legal timing. The generated slots are `{r₁,…,rₖ,J,e,S,h}`. For the set A of original accepted slots in any history prefix, `spent=|A|u` and `spent+|slots\A|u=(k+4)u`, `u=(1,1,0)`. Lawful remaining admissions must inject into unaccepted slots by original commitment, once-only acceptance and irreversible exclusion of h after choosing local. This bounds each history without determining the exact future demand of a merged state. Actual terminal or pending charges are k+3 (local) or k+4 (helper). Spare capacity confers no permission. The conditional induction needs a base export in the frame of J for every k, then k one-step instances and O. Source-level natural-number depth and concrete representability/adequate limits require separate treatment.

## Frozen qualification matrix and stopping rule

This is a plan fixed before new construction or nested execution. The policy above is the expected observation from *selected evidence* against which to test the authored programs. A finite qualification of its correspondence does not discharge source-level induction.

| Boundary | Evidence required if the transport gate succeeds |
| --- | --- |
| Independent policy/model | k=0 use the archived N reference for all 16 inputs and both choices. At k=1,2 use an independently authored policy/reference argument or finite model for each of the same 16 inputs and both local/helper branches in each mode. Inspect two distinct original relay selections and the returned unchanged value at k=2. Preserve all actually enumerated states/edges/maxima, failed/stuck cases, revisits and cutoffs with exact input coverage. |
| Public correspondence | Compile/build the public relay, rebinding of O, and external public-import consumer. Check artifact/template/declaration/port and transitive catalogue identities, correlate compiled instructions to selected input/presence/guard/Send/End, check finite per-artifact operational bounds. Exercise A and B × four modes × one/two relays at capacity one where supported. Use original Store controls to delay a real failed relay's *first* reply authorization until after its own End, then verify its actual selected child preceded Send and upstream selection followed publication. |
| Semantics versus validity | Drop a failure report; distinguish truthful present `candidate_missing_reply` from absent response. Exercise real successful and failed members ending without reply and a genuinely open pending receive. Check wrong id, valid-but-wrong answer, wrong handle/provenance, repeated selection/delivery/Send, and inconsistent status/reason/tag/list length. Reject double complement, requested-mode oracle and relabelled diagnosed failure. Include an authenticated schema-valid lie that a public consumer accepts and reject its underlying semantic truth by independent leaf evidence. On malformed child evidence compare direct O with R then O using the defensive table. |
| Resources and prefixes | On a chosen-helper path independently lower I or R by one and cap by one, preserving the actual refused operation/reason and zero refusal charge, with sufficient other bounds. A local-only path must remain feasible at I=R=k+3, cap=k+2. Probe cuts at commitment, first decision, reply admission, End/freeze, publication, delivery and selected consumption; include both orders of late reply service around End. Read each real transaction as a unit, including grouped publication/delivery where applicable. Distinguish detached decoder mutations from reachable executions. |
| Raw audit and gates | Decode each original operation/parent/source and actual matching receive, input/report value, immutable first decision/disposition, charge, End and publication from retained records independently of reducer/classifier. Check O's actual decision instruction. Preserve focused exact normal/race invocations, test discovery and per-leaf outcomes, inherited boundary checks, pinned local offline readonly toolchain/Go/OS/arch/CGO, actual argv, formatting, vet, module verification, whole-diff and protected-source audits. Retain interruptions, rejected arguments and corrected reruns under distinct identities. |

Review target and transport before creating a family. If unchanged `A_op` cannot be instantiated at the relocated boundary from the original premises, preserve the exact missing semantic definition or fully qualified failing witness and its evidence. Keep the k=0 export, frozen one-step rule/obligations and independently supported analysis distinctly scoped. In that case the k>0 construction, induction and public-qualification matrix above remain conditional work, not successful experiments. An unavailable public API after a positive transport gate instead calls for a minimal complete attempt and exact diagnostic, preserving independently supported source results.
