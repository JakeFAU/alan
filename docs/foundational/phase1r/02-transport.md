## One interpretation for each history

Pair a translated execution with an execution of the same bytes in a **covered original configuration**, starting before e's commitment. At the J boundary retain the original outer harness for the P proof. Map the original resource-root role to the new O, and the original requester and return-address role for J to its actual caller, the innermost relay or O at k=0. The old root may occupy both roles. At k>0 mapping that single run to two distinct runs is impossible for any bijection on bare run names. Each guard below uses its own role. The internal edges e→J, S→J and h→e preserve their actual parents, response routes and selection history.

Maintain a single partial bijection π between structural operations in the two paired histories, sorted by role and declaration. A pair records **commit occurrences**, including source run/generation, original operation and ordinal, with its binding and subsequent request, disposition of a reply, publication and delivery attached to that same occurrence. Application integers, booleans, strings, records/lists and constants have the identity action. The diagnosis is application data. Structural caller, root, member, source, template and observation fields have their own role-indexed correspondences. In particular a rebound enclosing caller artifact can have a different immutable ID. Never edit a program literal or an arbitrary string in a projection by searching for the spelling of a key.

For each paired e/h member require equal PC, status, actual register domains and cursor domains. Each defined data register has the identical literal value, including record fields and list order. Each defined Handle[d] register contains the actual key of a past commit to d, and its partner contains the key of the π-paired commit. Cursor indices and literal snapshots agree. Inputs and authenticated observations consumed or still available to these members correspond by the original subscription and port, actual recipient, occurrence and consumption state, with identical application fields. This relation is checkable on two histories without running any future branch.

At entry the stored template fixes an open projection at PC 0 with declared literal inputs, no handle and no cursor. Constants and default collections inserted by the author compiler execute as ordinary instructions. Neither frame evaluates a user callback to initialize a handle. On the first paired durable Emit the reducer constructs JSON of (actual subject,generation,next per-run sequence,0). Two different commits in one history have distinct valid source run/generation or sequence/ordinal. Same-descriptor lookup and failed attempts create no commit. Valid UTF-8 and unambiguous tuple encoding preserve distinctness; legal bindings require fresh actual derived members and conflict-free original identities. This is no collision-freedom theorem for the hash functions used for derived IDs. Leave extra outer relay operations outside π's image. Extend π by the pair of fresh corresponding occurrences only on commitment, preserving all old pairs even if the last register alias disappears. Reusing a declaration or a destination creates a new pair. Byte-equality of two same-sort live handles is thus equivalent to their referencing the same occurrence, in each direction. A data literal equal to one serialization remains data on both sides. Thus a successful scan defines one partial register typing Γ whose restrictions meet every instruction constraint simultaneously. This is a finite, conservative sufficient condition. Its existence says nothing about whether the referenced register is defined on an execution path. The dynamic invariant tracks its actual domain instead.

CheckTemplate performs this scan on all stored programs, canonicalizes the bundle and pins alan/template/v1 and rechecks each descriptor with the executor's RetainTemplate. This order precludes assuming the new binding is already conformant. For the fixed J/S/relay instructions use their actual port rules and literal predicates on selected data. In particular J cannot select e before S. The abstract local caller tracks its e commitment and eventual selection. Its synthetic postselection terminal has no power to close the actual root. Respect the original semantic transaction boundaries and the ordered observations within them. Erase an e/h first-decision sidecar only in the projection to the coarser local graph, retaining both that first decision in the full history and the later original disposition.

* **Numerical guard.** For k relays, a child can only be invoked by its created, admitted parent. All k admissions precede J's. ## Service, settlement and the relay induction

Fix a full maximal execution under weak service of continuously eligible actions. For finite k the chain to J has finitely many prerequisite actions. An open parent's entry instruction, original Invoke, first authorization and current funded admission become persistently eligible in order. They cannot be withheld forever, and a finite maximum cannot stop before the next eligible action. J receives and commits e then S without waiting for either outcome. S's unspent slot exists even if e and h were accepted first. S admission services q. S consumes its one request, sends (id,0), Ends and publishes after its bound reply is admitted. Its delivery and J's original S selection persist in turn. Then any already delivered e is selectable at J's subsequent Await. Delaying e selection cannot alter e's private state after e publication. None of these finite delays is a scheduler priority or uniform time bound.

Retain **every** original e/h computation step, including private branches, helper selection and any private cycle, in the projection. On an infinite projected run, a continuously eligible original private action has a corresponding actual action, because its observations, PC, data and handle tests agree, and erased outside steps cannot consume its inputs. Entry, q and caller selection have the finite prerequisites above. For a continuously due coarse admission service the first decision, if missing, and then the still-undecided admission must each be serviced. Repeating the first lookup cannot fulfill the second duty. The other local delivery/publication guards persist under the frame. Thus the projection respects original weak service. An intermittently enabled choice creates no fairness obligation. Progress_N excludes an infinite projection before the required boundary. In completed modes e and any chosen h have frozen and been selected, and q is spent. At pending the original e is at its designated unmatched receive, q is spent, and any chosen h is finished and selected. There is no further productive projected step in either case.

For a **finite projection**, an infinite erased suffix would leave its last local state fixed. If any original action there were eligible, either the actual action stays eligible across the suffix or the first outstanding prerequisite for it stays eligible and advances a finite chain. The only new delays are entry, S before e selection, and split authorization/admission as above. A finite maximal stop likewise cannot leave the first such action enabled. Hence the endpoint is an original open maximum and Progress_N supplies the mode there. This also covers an erased prefix before e's first instruction. Counting protocol events alone cannot exclude divergent private control.

## Scope of the evidence

The private lemma is a source-level argument under the checked grammar and separately stated frame assumptions. The authoring type system admits Q's string-list discriminator. An acceptance by the operand checker of a spelling-dependent private transition under adequate limits would refute its soundness. A failure of the full restricted implication would additionally require original all-input W and Progress for that same X, a legal translated context and a violated guarantee. Conversely, a wrong literal answer can certify. Independent testing of the unchanged probe, same-sort aliases and distinct commits challenges executor correspondence on actual records. A pair of public placements can establish finite evidence for those instances only. The abstract proof has no distributed execution, crash, scheduler or compiler correctness conclusion.
