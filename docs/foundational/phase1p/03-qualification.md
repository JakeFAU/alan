# Phase 1P — Behavioral qualification

## Result and scope

Under the fixed E_N context, each original finite operational candidate X satisfying the profile's weak relation **and separate all-mode Progress** preserves K_join in J and G_outer in O[J]. This is a conditional source-level argument for all such X in A_op on D_N under P_boundary, for F_event and F_fact separately. Each request has original id in {1,2}, value in {0,1}, and mode in {answer, fail, absent, pending}. Full-local W and Progress specialize to this same conclusion because those configurations retain the same local transitions, closure options and service conditions. The [frozen contract](01-preservation-contract.md) states the exact premises. The [proof](02-preservation.md) supplies the prefix and maximal-execution arguments.

The arithmetic uses only once-only admission of the original slots, and the proof of permission uses the actual transition and original ticket laws. Progress applies to the projected execution of X, retaining its private steps. This separates three questions which previously shared a proof strategy. The existing stronger ARG-1 lifting rule remains conditional. For P_boundary under either F reading, ARG-2 (existence of an original-state relation satisfying that rule) remains open. Original-state Account remains sufficient under Phase 1O's hypotheses, and P_stage already includes the stronger stage comparison. The proof here gives a behavioral conclusion without settling necessity or existence of either certificate. [Prior classification](../phase1o/04-classification.md)

## Evidence boundary

The historical N **K1-first** archived normal-reference record covers all 16 requests with full I=R4 and cap 3. It records 6,376 model/monitor states, 13,232 transitions, 24 terminal and 8 pending maxima, with zero state, rank, prefix, component, projection, contract and outer failures, rejections, stuck states, cutoffs and cycles. The archived normal-correspondence-r2 record covers 283 mapped instructions and 73,984 J / 6,784 O policy inputs per A/B binding. The two record payloads were read from integration commit `d0def139632c73936a3e821d93271a62267fa46d` and checked against both hashes in their respective historical command manifests. These records support the inherited reference and policy premises at the stated finite scope. Their executions predate Phase 1P. [N qualification](../phase1n/03-qualification.md) · [historical records](../phase1n/evidence/model/qualification/README.md)

The challenges produced no fully qualified failing X and no pair of interpretations with conflicting behavioral outcomes. For instance, the fork rejects a particular path strategy while the resource argument survives the lawful merge. No new checker, product or public execution was needed for those questions. For a particular new candidate, its W and Progress obligations remain to be discharged. Transfer to a public program additionally requires its correspondence, original selected and bound observations, and adequate operational limits. The original A/B measurements supply no uniform private-step or runtime bound. Arbitrary contexts, symmetric exact-debit equivalence and general compiler, scheduler and recovery correctness remain outside this result.
