Proposition P below is the target submitted for semantic review. Its statement supplies neither a proof nor a candidate verdict.

Keep distinct original Send commitment, first authorization, reply admission, End/freeze, publication, delivery and selected consumption. N does not require reply admission before End. O sees the original application input and its public handle/envelope. It cannot attest inner report truth.

Here a legal prefix is any prefix the original transitions and premises permit. The conclusion cannot filter out a failing continuation.
