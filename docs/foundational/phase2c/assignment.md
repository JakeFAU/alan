ALAN Phase 2C — Authorized reconciliation of original effects
Assignment and intended result
Goal: Determine whether a separately authorized ordinary computation can recover trustworthy evidence about an original external operation after that operation's dispatch authority has ended, use the evidence to discharge a genuinely unresolved original obligation, and enable useful ordinary continuation without repeating the business mutation.

Architecture: One retained ALAN Store under its existing single-host ownership discipline; an independent controlled endpoint with its own durable application ledger; an ordinary resolver with explicit read and evidence-adoption authority; a narrow runtime-owned target-binding and evidence-retention path; and a live ordinary consumer. Preserve the distinction between the original interaction's immutable outcome and later knowledge about world application.

Tech stack: The repository's existing Go runtime, public author/runtime/effects/schema APIs, SQLite fixtures, subprocess/barrier machinery, independent finite-model and raw-evidence checks, and synchronized Markdown/LaTeX manuscript. Discover actual tools and versions before running commands. No new dependencies, live providers, paid services, distributed ownership system, or infrastructure project.

Specification: This brief, the frozen Phase 2C contract produced in Task 1, and the evidence hierarchy in AGENTS.md. Execute the whole research assignment through reviewed draft PR. Do not stop after a plan, a host-only lookup, or the first passing example. Do not merge or release.

This is a substantial experiment spanning capability design, endpoint semantics, retained evidence, program behavior, and process recovery. A well-supported negative result is acceptable. An implementation shortcut that changes the question is not.

2. Research question and claim discipline
The principal question is:
Under an explicit endpoint contract and separately granted authority, can authentic read evidence for an exact original operation advance its retained application knowledge and missing obligation, after original R/Q, while preserving immutable history and allowing an ordinary consumer to act on what was actually established?

Separate five outcomes: evidence acquisition; original-result adoption; local settlement; captured aggregate publication; and actual ordinary selection. A sixth, business application, belongs to the independent world. None of these names can stand in for the others.

The main positive profile requires original effective revocation and a successful correctly targeted Q before adoption. This is a simplifying domain restriction that excludes a surviving original local response writer. It is not a theorem that every safe read needs prior Q. If a successor owner is involved, revalidate the durable revocation and applicable drain conditions there. An old host acknowledgment is neither a portable capability nor permission to ignore current ownership.

Use two information components: immutable historical interaction observations, and accumulated evidence about original application. A genuine found receipt can justify applied under the frozen endpoint contract. not_found means no matching committed application was visible at that particular read; application knowledge remains unresolved. Query failure, missing response, conflict, and lack of authority are separate cases. Do not collapse them into a definitive negative.

This phase adds an evidence channel that the earlier no-reconciliation counterexample explicitly lacked. Success would not refute that counterexample. No universal reconciliation theorem, symmetric crash bisimulation, exactly-once guarantee for arbitrary endpoints, generic adapter retry safety, compensation policy, or final nonexecution from absence is authorized as a conclusion.
3. Fixed design requirements
3.1 An ordinary resolver and explicit authority
Use an ordinary authored resolver as the principal actor, with its own legitimate request, observation, authority, and accounting. Its live scope and the final consumer must lie outside any original owning scope that has been revoked. A different subject within the same revoked scope is insufficient.

Define permission to read the particular endpoint/target separately from permission to adopt evidence into the original obligation. One narrowly scoped capability may explicitly bundle those permissions; neither may be inferred from possession of a target string, a mutation grant, lifecycle authority, or Q. Include a read-authorized but adoption-unauthorized control.

Every physical read passes the resolver's normal current dispatch gate. Preserve Phase 2B's R/A/L/H/Q ordering, short lock, owner pinning, and error retention. Specify which already-authorized late evidence may still be recorded/adopted after resolver closure; a fresh read requires fresh eligibility. Resolver Q must cover its query recording and any adoption accounting belonging to that dispatch lifetime. Original Q does not automatically cover another actor's later reads.
3.2 Exact target binding without hidden Store access
Authenticate a reference to one committed original operation. Bind the namespace, original run/generation and operation, retained original authorization/attempt, endpoint/protocol, key, request bytes, and relevant schema/configuration. Separate the new lookup's own operation ID/key from the old mutation's target ID/key.

The compiler already permits a request identity to cross a typed string payload boundary. That serialization loses its static declaration proof. A forwarded string does not become an ordinary adapter Await handle. Authenticate it through the declared runtime path.

The existing effects.Adapter contract keeps Prepare pure and gives Execute no Store. Do not close over a Store or consult its SQL from a nominal adapter to evade this boundary. Add the smallest explicit runtime-owned target-resolution/binding mechanism needed. Derive trusted descriptors from retained original facts; validate any program-supplied metadata against them. Do not accept a caller's invented descriptor as authoritative.

Distinguish the complete Prepared.Digest from the digest of the exact HTTP body. The current counter's wire request omits several fields in fixture.Request, and the current endpoint's re-marshaled decoded request is not proof of original wire equality. Establish exact bounded wire retention or a precisely specified canonicalization/preimage contract. Bind actual bytes independently at application and lookup. A digest is a correlation device under the trusted endpoint assumption, not a new authentication theorem.

Bind continuity to an unchanged durable endpoint ledger, independently of URL, executor epoch, endpoint PID, and process-start token. Prefer a stable ledger identity for the new controlled profile; alternatively freeze a clearly enforced unchanged-ledger restriction. Test normal endpoint process restart on the same ledger and substitution of a different ledger at the same address. Historical records lacking newly required binding must remain explicitly unsupported or receive only the weaker justified interpretation.
3.3 Read semantics and a bounded recovery rule
Implement a controlled read-only receipt query that distinguishes an exact found application, no matching committed application at the read, explicit conflict/ambiguity, and unsuccessful query. State its linearization/snapshot rule. Return the original immutable application receipt, not merely the present counter total or an echo of submitted target fields.

The query must never call the mutation route, invoke Apply, reserve or seal a business key, cancel an accepted packet, install a tombstone, retry the original mutation, or perform compensation. Audit writes and read accounting are allowed and must be identified. Prove noninterference on the endpoint's business projection under a coupled schedule of independently serviced original packets; concurrent application means total application count need not stay constant during a read.

Use this explicit default for a lost query response: a narrow trusted opt-in recoverable-read protocol, limited to this frozen capability. Its logical query denotes the first durable terminal query outcome during that query's service interval. A successful observation contains an authentic identified snapshot; transport failure or local refusal contains no invented world evidence. A retained snapshot need not be the first response, the newest snapshot, or the world state at consumption. If a process dies before any result is retained, a fresh owner may perform a newly identifiable physical read under current resolver authority and a remaining durable allowance. That read may observe a later world. It is not replay of the lost snapshot.

Retain a distinct read ordinal/reservation before each physical exposure. Bound total opportunities across owner crashes; a crash before transmission still spends its opportunity. Distinguish logical effect debit, physical-read allowance, endpoint read contacts, and business applications. Freeze small limits, ordinarily two physical reads per logical query and at most two logical queries in the principal histories. Include an exhaustion case that supplies only the declared local refusal/unknown behavior, never fabricated world evidence.

Once any query result is durably retained, including not_found or a transport error, do not issue another read for that logical query. Complete retained accounting locally. A subsequent refresh uses a new logical query identity. Preserve both snapshots. Concurrent candidate responses must obey the frozen first-result/conflict rule; never relabel the last response as the first or allow delayed absence to erase known application evidence. HTTP method, adapter name, or a program payload saying “read only” cannot grant recoverability. All existing mutation adapters retain their no-repeat policy.
3.4 Original evidence adoption and immutable outcomes
Implement a narrow provenance-checked adoption path. A retained authorized query observation, its exact target binding, endpoint evidence, and declared import permission must justify every original-evidence transition. Validate these relationships on reopening and replay. An unrestricted host ImportReceipt accepting arbitrary bytes is not an acceptable substitute.

For a missing original result, retain an explicit reconciled outcome and provenance sufficient for existing original-obligation handling to progress. Use a separately specified result-reconstruction rule for the controlled protocol. In particular, the principal adapter positive uses the frozen counter's success policy, exact schema, and wire. An application receipt is not the result of an arbitrary adapter; the same receipt under selected-error or unknown policy does not automatically become a validated success. Preserve those policies or reject adoption where reconstruction has not been justified.

A successful lookup response is not an original POST response. Preserve the original attempt's identity, request, authorization, epoch, reservation, and debit. Represent actual query transport metadata and reconciliation provenance honestly; do not invent an original receive, HTTP status, response timestamp, or candidate history. A new tagged retained representation is permitted if necessary and explicitly versioned/validated.

If an original result already exists, leave it byte-for-byte intact. A genuine later receipt can be additional evidence about application while a previously selected transport_error remains the original interaction outcome. Keep that evidence separately correlated and available to the resolver. Do not rewrite a failure, frozen reply, publication, selected observation, or terminal projection into success.

Freeze duplicate and conflicting import semantics, including two authorized resolvers. Equivalent receipts may provide additional provenance without a second original settlement/debit. Genuine conflicts must remain visible and cannot be resolved by overwriting the first record. Declare transaction boundaries: a program-visible adopted claim must follow durable adoption. If result retention, proof retention, adoption, and settlement are separate commits, every interrupted intermediate state must be valid, recoverable, and truthfully classified.

5. Definition of done and convergence decision
The successful bounded result requires all of the following: an ordinary authenticated identity handoff; a separately authorized read; correct interpretation of its exact snapshot; durable provenance; a genuinely missing original obligation advanced by justified adoption; a live parent selecting and using the resulting public evidence; genuine process recovery; preserved original authority and history; and no additional business mutation from reconciliation.

The absence and immutable-failure counterexamples are equally required. If a found receipt can only be printed by a host, the intended positive has not been established. If an implementation cannot meet the positive within this scope, retain the smallest reproducible obstruction, complete the independent analysis and qualification that remain possible, and explain the next missing semantic capability. Do not expand into compensation, global workflow orchestration, or distributed consensus to force completion.

At phase end give a decision, not a generic list of future work: did the declared evidence channel suffice for original-operation resolution and ordinary continuation under the chosen bounds? Which assumptions were necessary? Which new counterexample narrows the claim?

If this succeeds, the next convergence target is a restricted effectful composition and recovery relation connecting the established Series 1 fragment to this explicit reconciliation environment, with visible costs, authority, world interference, and progress premises. If the obstacle is definitive nonexecution, identify whether an endpoint-side seal/fence would be required; that would be a separately authorized world-changing protocol, not an enhancement to this read-only query. Phase 2 is not complete merely because one lookup works.
