# Reconciliation contract for an original controlled operation

## Actors, authority and ordinary handoff

An issuer commits exactly one counter Request with its own immutable source, authorization and reservation. A separate live resolver has a normal adapter grant plus a provisioned exact-operation read grant. A separate adopt bit grants authority to retain an original outcome justified by that read. There is no permission associated with knowing a string, owning a lifecycle edge, having sent a mutation, or receiving a host Q. The parent remains an ordinary live program. Its success branch must verify the target, found evidence and meaningful receipt values and consume the genuine result of its original invocation. The original issuance marker and that invocation's frozen reply remain truthful at their original times.

The old template's sole caller port captures a reply; that reply can be unavailable until the very effect being reconciled is known. A static root can send an ordinary ID early but its root publication has no neighboring authored AwaitInvocation. A live nested resolver can itself keep an original invocation pending on a descendant. The chosen construction permits one explicitly declared typed duplex identity handoff between the actual caller and entry of one bound invocation. Both sends go through ordinary authorization, admission and selection; the bound issuance reply uses its separate unchanged obligation. Authenticate the precise invocation edge, caller, generation, member, peer, direction, obligation and schema before rebinding a program endpoint. Other templates preserve their original closed peer domain. Only one handoff profile is provisioned, and its locator fixes the static caller RunID, invocation emission sequence/ordinal zero, template and entry. Resolution derives the actual entry RunID from the Store namespace and bound invocation. Grants made on that admission bind direction, revision and obligation. The declared port schema contains one bounded target string. The two message permissions themselves are reusable; the frozen artifacts each emit one ID/ACK in this run. Exact target authentication occurs later at the read, independently of any matching string. The caller may forward the genuine target to its independent resolver and acknowledge its selection; the issuer consumes an echoed target before End. Check source eligibility at real authorization for ordinary sends. No actor reads executor SQL inside an adapter.

A positive schedule can hold or trigger ordinary service until the issuer has successfully terminated, the exact source has reached Q, and its original invocation is pending on the captured missing result. Such scheduling provides no target or application value. A resolver that requests prematurely receives no promise of positive adoption. The read protocol rechecks current terminal source and local drain under the actual owner; it never treats a previous host answer as a transferable entitlement. The read, the original R/Q and the final consumer remain independently identified.

## Query identity, recovery and import

The scheme opts a composition deployment into schema 5. The executor resolves a program-supplied target against a fixed resolver generation, static or exact invocation/member origin, original sequence and ordinal zero, retained intent, grant/attempt, ledger-specific URL, key, exact request and protocol/schema/configuration. A bare operation string is an untrusted local selector. Two namespaces can emit an identical subject/generation/sequence/ordinal tuple. Its text alone cannot identify origin. The separately witnessed ordinary handoff establishes program provenance in that execution; authentication binds the local namespace, original retained facts and namespaced key. A foreign qualified key or mismatched proof must fail. The read has its own logical effect ID, key, authorization and debit. Input and output schema identities, complete preparation digest and exact body bytes/digest are separate bindings. The positive uses only the new controlled counter's exact adapter success policy. Raw keyed and non-idempotent originals supply read evidence only. Historic stores/attempts lacking the binding have no import or read retry privilege. Concrete public grant fields, version selection and retention checks are in the accompanying [runtime design](01-runtime-design.md).

A physical query is a new exposure under the live resolver's ordinary Phase 2B gate. Atomically check its current authority and reserve its next durable ordinal **before physical entry**. Each logical query gets at most two opportunities across all owners. An ordinal spent before transmission remains spent. One ordinary scheduler per Store and no direct adapter entry mean only one live ordinal per logical query on a conforming owner. An admitted opportunity can complete after resolver revocation. Its already-authorized return may import under its exact prior read/adopt authority; a fresh opportunity requires a fresh current gate. Its resolver-local H covers query handling, import and owed original/own settlement. Resolver Q surfaces genuine accounting failures, including error during local service after a crash. A completed policy refusal can drain successfully. Original Q alone does not cover work later undertaken by another actor.

The physical read has a strict ledger-named lookup route and a first-class snapshot ordinal issued by the independent endpoint. A stable, unmodified ledger survives process restart. Only its business writer applies a mutation. The read takes a serialized snapshot of committed applications, compares exact old wire bytes, returns the original immutable receipt for one match, and changes only the separate read audit. A same-address replacement has a different ledger identity. Under the controlled-world premise there is no third party submitting a copied original key/body, except in the explicit multiplicity attack. A matching receipt establishes the ledger’s recorded application of those bytes; it does not cryptographically identify the physical network sender. An absent key is an observation about one snapshot. A lost response leaves no locally retained snapshot; the next eligible ordinal may observe a later world. Retained absence, found, explicit conflict, malformed/unavailable transport, pre-entry refusal and exhaustion remain distinct. Any durable terminal result ends this logical query. Refresh requires a different logical identity. No statement about final nonexecution follows from a finite series of absences. [Endpoint protocol](01-endpoint-design.md)

The Store authenticates the query result and records its adoption decision, provenance and any newly reconstructed original adapter result in a single transaction. A missing success-policy result can gain exactly one tagged reconciled result, with its source query and independently stored original receipt. No original HTTP response, status or original callback is invented. An existing result including a transport failure stays byte-identical, with additional later application evidence linked to the query. Equivalent evidence may have more than one legitimate source; conflicting evidence remains visible without overwrite. A read-only grant can report a found application but cannot import. Raw ambiguity and unsupported reconstruction policies cannot fill a missing result. Freeze, publication, preexisting selected/defaulted outcomes and original debit remain immutable.

Original settlement, original aggregate publication, resolver result settlement and program selection are explicit subsequent transitions. Eligibility for the existing aggregate checks a captured result. Scheduling a cut between retention and original settlement is distinct from claiming that all publications universally wait for settlement. A program-visible adoption decision follows the durable import. The registered query lifetime finishes its owed local accounting before Q can succeed for that revoked resolver. After death the new owner performs available original/lookup settlement locally without another physical read. Reopen/replay validate source-bound proof and retention edges; offline replay neither queries nor mutates. Real original application remains an independent endpoint fact.

## Domain and falsification

Assume a trusted single-host owner and compatible successors, bounded correctly configured adapters, an honest endpoint with persistent ledger and identified snapshots, no malicious cloned or rolled-back database, and scheduling of the named ordinary actors. Conditional positive progress further requires an applied original, reachable responsive query service, a remaining physical allowance or retained evidence, sufficient ordinary budgets, and writable durable local storage. A slow read can consume a physical slot; unrelated ordinary work and revocation must remain serviceable. The reference bounds and concrete adverse histories are fixed in the [matrix](01-source-and-matrix.md).

Any found-based progression of an arbitrary adapter, rewritten prior failure, silently chosen receipt among multiple applications, replacement ledger accepted as the original, missing response presented as a durable snapshot, positive adoption observation ahead of its transaction, reset ordinal on reopen, repeat business POST from the reconciliation path, or invocation result supplied by host inspection would falsify this contract. The earlier no-channel counterexample still applies in its domain. Compensation, final rejection seals, symmetric simulation, arbitrary effectful substitution and distributed fencing are open.
