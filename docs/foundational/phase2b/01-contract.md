# Frozen local dispatch authority contract

## Domain and identity

One trusted single-host Store owner holds its existing exclusive nonblocking flock and pinned SQLite connection. The routing domain is raw external dispatch (Run and the public non-composition DispatchExternal) and registered adapter Execute (Run only). Prepare remains trusted pure bounded preparation. The adapter must avoid Store reentry and respect context cancellation. The controlled adapter issues one POST within Execute. Other adapters can perform different actions internally. An endpoint can accept a packet and apply later without any surviving client. The owner cannot fence that endpoint.

Keep the immutable intent, first authorization and its reason/epoch, logical source generation and revision, request or preparation, operation key, original reservation and any member effect debit. One logical key may generate multiple distinct physical opportunities on the raw keyed route. One owner-local opportunity is one registration object, consumed exactly once by that owner's handling path. An old permission, the attempted map in Run, a root slot and an operation ID alone each fail to identify such a lifetime. A new owner starts with no volatile tokens.

## Events and predicates

* **R** is the durable commit that first makes the specified authority ineligible. A gate re-reads current authority for an allowed reservation. The original intent's **source RunID** selects the subject and generation. Compare its retained source revision to the current subject revision. Require the subject's current generation to match, the subject to be unretired, and the source projection to be open. For an actual registered member additionally require the existing effective owning-scope predicate to permit work. Under the current provision/Retry APIs revisions are provisioned and immutable. Revision mismatch is guarded in source. Forged revision or future generation does not witness revocation.
* For an originating source, successful End, private Failure, CancelRun, AbandonRun, retirement, successor-generation creation and effective enclosing closure can exclude a new admission. A successful nested frame's **own** terminal source stops initiating its own new calls. Its successful unpublished freeze alone leaves eligible open descendants able to dispatch. Top-level resource-root status, non-success freezes, retirement closure and published invocation results follow `compositionScopeClosed`, including the binding/caller chain. A successful root can retain a permanently pending captured unresolved effect if it wins before A. An `already_terminal` lifecycle no-op confers no new closure.
* **A(w)** is the critical section which reads current eligibility for this exposure and adds its unique registration to the owner's live set under the same exclusion used by durable revocation. The opportunity also requires an open owner, an uncancelled worker at the gate, and its original allowed transport route. Gate refusal produces no call or invented operation-level result. It leaves a historical debit spent, contributes no second semantic debit and does not replace first permission. For source failures use the distinct reasons `source_generation_changed`, `source_revision_changed`, `source_retired`, `source_closed` and `scope_closed`, checking generation, revision, retirement, then openness and scope. Closing/cancelled owner work withdraws with its owner/context error. Genuine state-reading errors propagate.
* **L(w)** is entry to raw `http.Client.Do` or the registered adapter's `Execute`. A successful local gate, source-line placement before the call, transmission, ingress, independent application and response acquisition are distinct facts. Hooks can bracket physical entry with source and endpoint corroboration. They do not execute the call. The outcome of the ordered race is fixed: R before A excludes L; A before R permits R before L. A prior receipt may subsequently return and settle on a closed source.
* **H(w)** finishes handling a single admitted opportunity. It covers the return/withdrawal, completion queue and detached bounded recording and settlement of available authentic evidence, or explicit accounting failure. A genuine unknown/no-response can finish without any result, with its original obligation still unresolved. Returned local status, normalized defaults and endpoint-world knowledge have separate meanings. Once H has accounted for the opportunity its physical lifetime cannot be reused. A released transport slot or finished goroutine may precede H.
* **Q** is a successful return from the host wait below on an already-revoked exact target. All matching registrations have finished H, no matching accounting failure is hidden, and genuine already-retained evidence has completed its required settlement. No covered worker can thereafter enter L for that revoked authority. It may have sent a packet already. Neither R nor Q states what happened remotely, authorizes another grant, marks an operation suppressed, or determines a parent's next instruction.

## Public and internal interfaces

```go
type DispatchAuthority struct {
    Namespace   string
    Run         RunID
    OwningScope bool
}
func (s *Store) AwaitDispatchQuiescence(ctx context.Context, target DispatchAuthority) error
```

Expose the type and method through the public runtime Store alias. Use the observed Store namespace and a genuine retained RunID, including its generation. The receiver validates both. With OwningScope false, the target covers opportunities originating in that exact run, including its effective enclosing revocation. With OwningScope true, resolve the run's immediate genuine owning scope from immutable membership/binding, then cover registrations whose owning-scope chain includes it. A static member names its resource root, an invocation member its immediate invocation. Scope eligibility uses scope/ancestor closure facts independently of the alias member's own terminal status. Old-generation Q has no claim over a successor. Epoch and program sequence do not select authority. A bare subject, wrong namespace, missing/invalid run and unscoped scope alias fail target validation. `ErrDispatchAuthorityOpen` distinguishes a valid currently eligible target. Malformed, foreign-namespace or unscoped scope target returns ErrStale. Absent original run returns sql.ErrNoRows. A closing/closed owner returns the closed-connection error. A cancelled/deadline wait returns the context error, including on an otherwise idle target.

The Q path checks durable eligibility and the volatile registry under Store exclusion, then waits on a change signal outside locks/transactions. Recheck after wakeups. It can settle a matching already-recorded external receipt or adapter result with the existing settlement path, without a new mutation, active Run, ordinary Commit or selection. It never waits for a missing world receipt. It may itself spend time doing local I/O. Retain the first accounting failure per originating generation for the life of this owner. Source and covering scope waits return the matching error, even if a later independent action made progress. Preserve underlying accounting errors separately from mere caller cancellation or transport loss. The guarantee after executor death is relative to surviving records plus new owner-local work. Missing volatile bytes and errors cannot be reconstructed. Reopen validates historical evidence as before.

The internal gate takes the original allowed work descriptor/intent, derives exact identity from retained facts and returns a unique lifetime token, an ephemeral refusal, or a genuine error. The completion carries that token to a single release at H. Run performs A synchronously after preparation and before scheduling its worker or making its next ordinary commit. Direct dispatch uses the same gate. A fresh `dispatch.consider/pre-admission` hook is unlocked before A; a separately authorized public operation can win there. The inherited `external.authorized` / `adapter.authorized` pre-transport hooks occur in the worker after A. The additional `dispatch.admitted/pre-call` worker hook, `dispatch.refused/<reason>`, and `dispatch.waiting/in-flight` distinguish the causal order. A logical gate refusal on direct dispatch returns (false, nil). The model enumerates more owner interleavings than a single automatic scheduler invocation. All of these hold no Store locks. The durable R commit and gate eligibility/registration serialize via Store.mu. When also needed, order locks Store.mu then ownerMu, matching commitOwnedTx. Never wait for an endpoint, notification, worker, adapter, or Q while holding either. No production callback enters Store under its mutex.

A direct legacy method registers with the owner before preparation and stays covered until all handling returns, including paths with no admitted transport. Multiple direct calls and mixed direct/Run are supported on a plain store. Each eligible opportunity gets its own A, including same-key duplicate exposures. Run capacity limits its scheduler, not independent legacy direct callers. Composition direct dispatch stays refused before reservation. Close prohibits new owner admissions, cancels/run-drains or waits for covered work, including direct calls and Q's local settlement, then releases the connection/flock. A waiting Q must be able to exit on shutdown. Closing an open source's owner is independent of logical R. A fresh legitimate Open may admit that still-eligible source.

## Compatibility, service and falsifiers

The future dispatch policy applies on opening any already-supported store under this executable. No new durable schema, semantic/compiler version, historical grant, or request encoding is needed. Validate a real Phase 2A SQLite image, including replay and exact immutable bytes. Old history remains a historical account, even if a counterfactual re-execution under the new policy would make another decision. Deployed binaries predating this contract can still act under their original policy. The local Q guarantee assumes subsequent owners use this contract.

R requires normal owner-lock and local database service and cannot be blocked by a transport-duration lock. Q can await conforming work, bounded detached accounting, and scheduling/I/O service. A stuck or nonconforming adapter can prevent drain, and one occupied physical slot can delay another effect. A timed-out waiter has no affirmative observation. A fresh owner is qualified only after OS flock release or process death. General remote fencing, fairness, symmetric recovery and compensation are outside the quantified claim.

Falsifiers include any accepted A after exact R, any covered L after Q, Q between eligibility and worker registration, Q while a duplicate is live or available evidence is queued/unsettled, loss of a completed receipt on caller cancellation, new debit/key on same-operation recovery, rejection of an otherwise eligible open descendant solely for successful unpublished nested freeze, and a claim of remote nonapplication from refusal or Q. The separate persistent packet-after-Q schedule attacks the stronger remote proposition. The [matrix](01-source-and-matrix.md) fixes case families and model bounds before implementation.
