Model bounds precede exploration. An immutable allowed reservation has fixed original key/revision/debit. Up to two physical opportunities can share the key, one peer can take an independent private step, one relevant authority can revoke and at most one crash/reopen occurs. Distinct small profiles cover each route, source/scope/owner, known/unknown/error, nested freeze/publication, generation, prospective/held packet, and eligible/closed recovery. The external environment may decline to revoke or delay an accepted packet; internally enabled service and optional external choices have distinct owners. Conditional progress additionally assumes enough resources, fair local service, responsive or cooperatively withdrawing transport and successful local accounting. Exact per-profile manifests accompany the graph.
