The declared environment owns admission, reply disposition, publication and delivery, q and root closure. No service event depends on a member finishing an unrelated private instruction. ### First decisions and the closure limitation

For each committed invocation or bound reply, first decision `a` becomes available at commit `c`. Later admission `d` uses the retained decision. A lookup of the same immutable descriptor returns that decision and supplies no new progress. A local q or root closure may precede c, occur between c and a, occur between a and d, or follow d when its own guard still holds. Other members and independent intents may interleave at each real cut. No claim requires q/closure to intervene inside a commit. For a Send, End may precede a, intervene between a and d, or follow d. A stable valid binding allows first service after End. Generation/revision checks still precede the bound-reply exception. An invocation has no such exception.
