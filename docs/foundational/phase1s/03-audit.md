## Reconstructing the private boundary

The auditor reads the immutable B bundle with SHA-256 `8693f675a4ec4be6d14a859157fbbf9d21b3608e4485d32b900421c0d7d7b5aa`, checks the two program byte hashes, and decodes them. For the representative binding it substitutes the actual caller in the public port and bound Send/Receive, and the original operation and runtime-request provenance in the entry Receive. Invoke and Await retain their declaration, template and exact operation operand. Both initial maps are empty. There is no cursor-producing instruction. Distinct names root, e and h stand for the three subjects; generation is one. B's first Invoke occurs at execution sequence two. Each later helper selection must match that same occurrence. Other intermediate registers contain only literal data. The names may vary under R's freshness, binding and adequacy premises. The checker does not quantify over hash collisions, byte budgets or numerical exhaustion. At each state the checker reconstructs the actual PC, open/succeeded/failed status and private data for each admitted member. The request stage distinguishes undefined input registers from a received integer zero. H's complement destination is defined at PCs 3, 5 and 6. In B the helper key is present after B1 committed. After B2 consumed that exact outcome the status, reason and presence destinations exist. The two response destinations exist exactly for a present response. Branch guards at B3 and B4 therefore protect B6's reads on the entire reachable domain. B14 would require a succeeded but absent envelope. This kernel never publishes one. Without a second request, B13 is unreachable. The fixed empty success-output schema is validated at the actual successful Ends. These properties follow from the initializer and are preserved by checking actual successor data on *every* private edge.

The auditor evaluates the actual retained instruction with the only eligible original input, or with nil when none exists. The admitted request and original delivered helper envelope are the only two supplies. A consumed one is unavailable. Mismatched recipient, kind, provenance, generation, obligation or operation cannot supply another input. The runner skips consumed or ineligible observations and propagates other errors. A Receive with no matching observation can wait. A missing operand from a ready reduction would instead fail the audit. The checks compare the reducer's full result projection and the encoded private registers, including the successful sequence, and validate each emitted intent's target, generation, obligation and payload. A successful End increments the sequence and keeps its PC. The branch reaching B12 increments it once. No failed attempt advances it. The register array represents these defined values; an immutable request or selected envelope supplies the unchanging literal string fields. There is no dependence on a hidden future branch. ## Independent progress and adverse cases

On the open graph, count the two members' successful instruction sequences, e commitment, caller consumption of e, spent q, and each operation's accepted admission, accepted reply disposition, publication and delivery. Each reachable edge increases this count exactly once. Helper commitment and selection are counted as e instructions. The checked count is bounded by 64. Thus the finite original graph has no infinite productive run. Every sink independently meets the exact mode boundary, including spent q, both original inputs and B's selection of the correct helper envelope. In pending, e is genuinely open at B12 with no consumed second request, no reply, no freeze or publication, and its caller still awaiting. The exact private-step audit rules out an additional loop or precommit failure at an omitted PC. The P/R prerequisite argument still handles weak continuous service of first decisions and delayed caller actions in legal compositions. Saved first-decision lookups do not satisfy current admission service. [Open progress audit](../../../research/substitution/retained_b_audit_test.go) · [R service argument](../phase1r/02-transport.md)

The controls remove q and closure after the actual first Receive, remove each reference branch and a reference admission, change an environmental owner, and remove an immediate bilateral match. One mutation drops the helper commitment from its supplied path. Another appends the silent **local** choice after the matched request consumption. The latter preserves the fact word but reaches a different reference endpoint. An altered pending predicate placing B at PC10 fails the maximum test. Detached replacements change the helper instruction or an initializer while retaining the old evidence. The stale bundle fails the identity check. Separately, the original open prefix reaching B3 after h selection with q spent has exactly one due action. Finite-machine controls replace that private branch by a self-loop or a stop. O accepts their supplied finite-prefix witnesses. The first cycle services all independently due original work before spinning; the second has a nonboundary maximum. These are abstract suffix mutations, not public B executions.
