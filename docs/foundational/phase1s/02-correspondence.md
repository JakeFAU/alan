# The retained entry and helper on the original local interface

Take R's X at the original binding. The component supplies the actual private instructions of B and its one declared helper. The caller, protocol service, q and closure use O's fixed operational ownership and the original I/N local rules. R asks for this machine before introducing the J constructor restrictions. Interpret its sixteen initial configurations separately. Each starts with the actual request, e possible at depth two, h possible at depth three, q possible, the root open, residual I=R3, E0 and cap three. No instruction has executed. No observation can arrive from outside this interface. The stronger question of replacing this kernel with the Store on arbitrary cancellation schedules has a different answer below. ## Open progress and the concrete boundary

Independently remove closure, keep q, and generate the complete X account again. The intrinsic `graphRank` increases on every productive edge, including edges revisiting a known destination. Its observed maximum is 6,821. The finite state space follows from the fixed finite input and once-only tickets/stages, two finite forward-only instruction tables, bounded per-run execution counts and single occurrences at original roles. An increasing rank on this set excludes an infinite projected execution while retaining every actual private computation. All 784 open states and 1,184 edges were checked. Each of the sixteen inputs has one maximum. In every maximum q is spent, both e and h were charged, and the correct original h response was consumed by e. Answer reaches a selected present (id,1−value) with succeeded/member_succeeded. Fail has failed End then absent failed/member_failed. Absent has successful End then absent failed/missing_reply. Pending is open at B12, the caller awaits e, and no e reply, End, publication, delivery or selection has occurred.
