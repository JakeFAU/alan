# Finite owning-tree substitution in the open Store

**Claim.** Fix a finite, representable tree shape, a legal generation-one deployment of O_T, a literal vector in D_N to the power of its number of leaves, and *any independent* assignment of the exact A or B to each leaf. Compare these assignments on the same requests. With dynamic I and R each at least b(T), depth at least h(T), E=0, capacity one, and the representation, environment and service conditions below, each assignment satisfies the same [K_tree contract](01-contract.md) in [K_store_tree](02-store-correspondence.md). Safety holds on every legal prefix. At every serviced maximal execution the completed leaves contribute their authentic role-indexed diagnostics, or the tree reaches precisely the pending frontier below. If the whole tree completes, O_T selects its own original operation and decides answer/success iff all leaves answered, otherwise diagnosed/failure. The outcome is conditional on the stated domain. It does not equate implementation steps, private choices, exact demand or exact debit. The proof concerns the retained source fragment. Finite qualification and public Store histories are separate evidence.

## Disjoint slots, common ledger and actual authority

Allocate names in the proof only. Each grammar occurrence has one entry slot. J has two more for e and S and a possible h slot under e. For A discard h from actual lawful demand, but keep it unused in the common upper bound. Relay and Fork add the disjoint sets of their child occurrences. A proper child member can exist only after the unique commitment at its parent's Invoke was accepted. The programs visit each Invoke at most once on every path (their foreach bodies emit none). Original retry lookups do not introduce a new commitment. Induction from O_T's one committed entry therefore maps every accepted occurrence to a different slot. Distinct source runs or different committed sequences distinguish left/right even for two calls to identical templates with identical payloads. Every future lawful admission still needs its unique slot as yet uncharged. No retirement, retry generation or extraneous commitment enters this domain.

Let S_T be that finite common set, C its actually charged subset at a prefix, U the set of uncharged original slots whose first acceptance is still possible on a lawful continuation, including reachable uncommitted descendants and undecided original commitments. Then U ⊆ S_T ∖ C; root spend is exactly (|C|,|C|,0). Completed/refused work never returns an earlier debit. Hence actual spend plus remaining possible lawful demand is at most (b,b,0). Here b(Leaf)=4, b(Relay U)=1+b(U), b(Fork L R)=1+b(L)+b(R). Physical depth from the immediate caller to the deepest possible new member is 3, 1+h(U), or 1+max(h(L),h(R)), respectively. The provisioned O_T contributes one fixed run, zero dynamic slots. On serviced positive boundaries the charged count is the number of grammar nodes + two per leaf + one per B leaf, even for pending. A bare J[A] spends three and J[B] four.

For any subtree V maintain C_in=C∩S_V and **all** C_out=C∖S_V. At a sibling admission, C_out increases by exactly that original charge; the local uncommitted/committed tickets, run projections, observations, readiness tests and C_in remain unchanged. At internal acceptance C_in increases, C_out remains. Thus after arbitrarily many interleaved outside spends the current root remainder is at least |S_V∖C_in|, since C_out⊆S_T∖S_V. This bound at every prefix, also when two h decisions compete for the last slots, is stronger than treating a one-time starting balance as a reservation. In a B leaf's *original* local view only accepted e, h and **its own S** contribute to the three inner units; subtract all actual outside debits and the real J entry, normalize the depth of e/h to 2/3, and clip any unused slack to the original envelope. This may coalesce several physical ledgers in the projection. It preserves the truth of each due admission's positive numerical guards because each original remaining slot still fits both views. It equates neither exact physical residuals nor unknown future demand across implementations.

At every invocation keep the full resource root and distinct immediate parent. The original declaration, target ID/generation, parent's permitted-child catalogue (if dynamic), root's transitive catalogue, input schema and zero-effect capability set follow from provisioning and the same once-visited Invoke. At admission the fresh member and request are bound to that caller and root; at return the Send resolves to the actual caller. Each pending invoker remains open: O_T and Relay have no End before their one select, Fork none before both, J none before S then e, B none before h. A proper ancestor whose owned call could still admit a conforming child cannot already have selected that aggregate: its aggregate can publish only after its own End, and that End is downstream of all its own selections. Formally take the first alleged ancestor End with a required descendant admission still outstanding. Its preceding required selection entails a publication and an earlier End descending the same finite path, until the immediate invoker contradicts its own awaiting control. A leaf helper has no further Invoke. Thus no open-environment closure, freeze or result on a relevant ancestry invalidates a live original admission. A frozen or failed sibling is outside this path. First permission, current admission, depth, budget and reply schema are independent checks. No grant is inferred from a spare slot.

## Correspondence of the generated Relay, Fork and O_T

Inspect compile/member/leafPaths and validate/validateLeaf, with the particular expand/step lowering and reduction clauses below. Induct on child schema construction. A leaf has exactly the historical request/report records. Relay copies them. Fork prefixes *every* child field with its own disjoint two-character role and preserves the field descriptor, including each bounded list element {id,value} and its one-item/192-byte limit. leafPaths strips an exact terminal id suffix, validates the complete prefix trie, and compares both entire retained schemas with ones reconstructed from all its paths. Sorted paths determine checking order. No relay marker or new input is introduced. All dynamically invoked constructors are singletons with a caller-bound public port and empty initial data; O_T alone starts with the validated request as input registers. NewTemplate retains the immediate children and provision collects the actual transitive catalogue. [Tree construction](../../../examples/owningtree/tree.go) · [catalogue formation](../../../author/invocations.go)

* **Commit before select.** For each immediate child the generator creates a declaration-specific handle destination, argument map from the corresponding input registers, and separate response bindings with full role prefixes. It collects Invokes in a separate ordered slice from the selections/validation slice. After the member's original Receive (O_T already has inputs), all Invokes are emitted left first. There is one distinct emit with that child template literal, generation 1 and declaration obligation, saving this commit's key. No later instruction overwrites the handle. Await lowers to receive of runtime-invocation, with the declared template source, generation 1, declaration obligation and that same handle as operation operand. Ineligible observations, even a right outcome addressed to this same parent while it awaits left, cannot advance it. The only output bindings live after a successful selection are those allowed by actual presence. Parent source code and retained handles fix the topology without consulting request mode.
* **Guard invariant.** tree_valid and tree_answer start true. A child's own answer flag starts true on every selected child. The first branch checks selected presence, and absence only clears tree_valid. On the present branch, each role checks its selected id against its input id, defines expected by its own input value, resets seen=false and executes a bounded foreach on its selected list. A next with a row binds both row fields before their first read. It checks seen=false, sets seen=true, checks row id and expected complement. Exhaustion exits without entering that body; stale row registers are never read there. The answer tag requires seen=true. Any other tag clears the child answer flag, requires seen=false and must equal one of the two diagnosis literals, else it clears tree_valid. Finally an all-answer child requires succeeded/member_succeeded; a child with a diagnosis requires failed/member_failed and clears tree_answer. tree_valid only decreases. An invalid left never bypasses the subsequent right Await. No optional use occurs on the absent branch and the final Send is dominated by tree_valid. Semantically false but internally consistent reports (for example, an authentic false diagnosis) need the child truth premise. Shape/guard checks alone cannot recover a hidden leaf's actual cause.
* **Return.** The successful validation arm forwards exactly the selected report registers mapped to their own prefixed fields, using the parent's *own* bound Send. There is precisely one such instruction and none on the invalid arm; the latter records invalid_tree_outcome and Ends Failure after all original selections. On the normal arm tree_answer chooses answer/Success or diagnosed/Failure. O_T has the same checks and decision, with no Send. Distinct register names separate role inputs from report fields, operation handles, envelope fields and the shared, freshly set scratch registers. Once a report is validated its selected fields persist through all later checks.
* **Lowered controls.** The compiler emits one set/branch/jump/emit/receive/end per corresponding primitive step or control edge. IfEqual patches the true entry, false entry and reachable-arm forward join; a terminal arm cannot fall through. A ForEach emits foreach, next, its body and loop-to-next, with Otherwise just after the body/back edge. Reduce snapshots the list, binds only on a live row, jumps on exhaustion and cleans out-of-scope cursors. A one-item schema permits at most one body iteration. Branch compares these scalar literals/data; no identity is serialized into a report. All emit payload operands, branch operands, next sources and the completed report are thus defined on all positive paths. A structural receive mismatch is skipped; a type/operand/schema failure is a fault outside the defensive *selected* case. These restricted templates instantiate the above code patterns at every path. A proof of a general-purpose author/compiler correspondence is unnecessary for this induction. ## Safety, strong settlement and progress

Induct over the term at its actual placement, allowing arbitrary prefix interleavings in the disjoint-slot frame. The base is the actual J analysis above. For a constructor, original child selections require real publication and delivery. Apply the induction hypothesis to **each selected** positive child at its own first publication. All its owned descendants have ended/frozen, captured bound replies have dispositions, and every required internal selection has occurred. For a completed parent every declared child must already have been selected by the return control. Consequently, before its first public publication the parent's entire interior is settled. Its own captured reply disposition is required by prepareCompositionScope even when its local End failed. Its outward publication/delivery makes a new envelope and its caller may select it later. A transport acknowledgment is distinct from the dispositive reply record. The argument uses children's strong induction hypotheses on Failure too: the Store alone may publish a failed nonconforming child with unresolved descendants.

For present valid children the role-wise checks and forwarding preserve each actual id, complement and diagnostic distinction. A mixed failure preserves successful siblings and both distinct causes. A valid parent publishes succeeded/member_succeeded exactly in the all-answer case, else failed/member_failed with its *present* product. The outer decision follows by one further original selection and the same validation. A selected invalid or absent child instead sets a persistent invalid flag; the defensive parent completes only after every declared select and emits no normal report. When it then Ends Failure, the Store publishes its own **absent failed/member_failed**, which the next positive wrapper treats as invalid. A successful child End with no reply produces absent failed/missing_reply, a different envelope. A pending other child keeps the defensive parent waiting. This latter policy gives no settlement guarantee for arbitrary nonconforming subtrees.

For liveness start at O_T's Invoke and follow finite prerequisites. An accepted parent's original Receive remains selectable, its following Invoke(s) have no dependency on child completion, and each newly committed operation's missing first decision then its funded current admission remains eligible by the frame/lifetime proof. Thus every occurrence's entry is eventually admitted under service, including a right sibling if the left is pending. Within J, S can finish independently; even if delivered e is gated by S, S's finite receive/send, first decision/record, End, settlement/delivery and J's select persist in the requisite order. The original q thus occurs. The B helper likewise reaches a recorded reply and e's actual select. A and B take their finite actual paths to a completed envelope or their designated unmatched Receive. There are no other senders of that receive's original operation.

More formally, project a purported infinite serviced execution at a leaf. If it has infinitely many projected productive actions, retain every private step. A continuously eligible local instruction or selection is physically enabled throughout the corresponding tail or has the finite prerequisites just established (original entry, S before J's e selection, first permission before disposition). The unchanged recipient and original binding prevent interference. Physical weak service transfers to the local run, contradicting S's open Progress for B or the finite forward PC and once-only stages for A. If the projection is finite, an infinite erased suffix fixes the local PCs, protocol stages and selected observations. If any productive local action is still owed, its actual step or first outstanding member of the same finite prerequisite chain stays eligible until performed. Root spend can change only finitely many times, never exhausting a promised slot. Neither saved-row lookups nor infinitely many scheduler checks can discharge that obligation. Thus a finite maximal stop or an infinite serviced suffix requires a local maximum. This argument also excludes an indefinitely erased prefix before a leaf starts. After all local maxima only finitely many positive guards, selections, decisions and settlements remain in the finite enclosing tree. The same persistent-prerequisite argument at each constructor rules out an infinite delay before the next available required select. Service time has no asserted uniform wall-clock bound.

With no pending child, both ordered outcome selections and the subsequent finite return path become continuously serviceable, including delayed reply service after End; the parent eventually publishes and delivers. Inductively if left is pending, left never publishes; at the serviced boundary the parent is at its *left* Await. Right independently reaches its own boundary, with an already delivered right completion unselected, or with its own pending frontier. If left completes and right is pending, the parent selects left, finishes its finite validation and remains at its *right* Await, even for a diagnosed failed left. Relay/O_T await their one unpublished child. Recursing these alternatives identifies the first unpublished required child at every blocked parent and settles independently runnable completed siblings. No blocked ancestor sends, Ends or publishes. A finite prefix still at the first Await while a matching delivery or validation is outstanding is not evidence of this maximal frontier.

## Checking a legal placement before execution

1. Let n be a subtree's leaf count. Each request/report has 3n top-level fields and the authenticated await schema 3+3n, with 2n additional element fields inside the n bounded lists. Therefore 3+3n ≤ 128 (n ≤ 41) is a necessary envelope field-count check for O_T, and each internal record must pass the same actual schema checks. The 4,096 total schema-field and nesting-8 ceilings are separate. All generated identifiers are fixed ASCII strings, with two extra characters per Fork edge. Scalar fields preserve the original enum bounds. Use the actual retained/list encodings, including empty and one-row lists; the 192 bytes are a list limit, not an entire projection limit. Compile every distinct node and O_T bottom up. Check success under 10,000 emitted instructions, depth-64 expansion and 100,000 expansion visits, the actual retained identities and the default template/program checks. A very deep relay chain can fail even when there is only one leaf. [Schema](../../../schema/schema.go) · [aggregate bounds](../../../schema/collections.go) · [author limits](../../../author/author.go)
2. For each occurrence program p (including O_T and J/e/S/h), put k(p)=len(Code)+number of next instructions. All jumps outside a structured loop advance or terminate. On a MaxItems=1 nonnested loop only next can commit twice. Thus per-run sequence is ≤k(p), and total commits are bounded by N=sum of k(p) over actual potential occurrence slots plus O_T. An assignment-independent substitute is (1+b(T)) times the maximum k over possible programs on that shape. There are ≤b calls, ≤b bound replies and ≤b invocation settlements. With the normal pure runner, each original invocation enters pendingDeliveries either before admission or when ready to settle; each Send is delivered and acknowledged in one dispatch. Root settlement is outside its dispatch counter. So MaxSteps=N+1 and MaxDispatch=3b+1 suffice for this runner with no retries, extra routes or interruptions. Provision MaxInFlight=1; retain E=0. Check b, h, each sequence and N, dispatch counts and integer arithmetic fit actual host/SQLite limits. At most N+6b+2 composition facts are needed (execution, call/reply permissions, call/reply dispositions, freezes and publications). For manually repeated lookups one cannot bound arbitrary driver call count by 3b.
3. A conservative per-term byte preflight can be computed without a schedule. For each first-class register/operand position of each program take the largest encoded value over the finite input/mode/tag/id/value choices, its possible original handle, or its empty/singleton list. Include the *union* of all defined register names, one live cursor snapshot with index 0/1 at any possible foreach PC, and the longest PC/status representation to bound each full projection. For each emit, observation and outcome record use the full prefixed map, and for a freeze up to two original invocation keys and one reply key per singleton. For source, binding and protocol records use the actual struct tags and original literal names; all generated hashed occurrences/subjects are 64 hex bytes, depth ≤h, and the longest source operation key is bounded by the JSON for [a 64-character hex subject,1,N,0] (take the larger of this and the separately provisioned root name). Derive quoted/nested authorization and transport keys by applying their actual JSON constructors to those bounds. In each object take braces/commas, key encodings and maximum field encodings, including enclosing string escaping. This gives computable upper sizes rather than equal-length renaming. Marshal the actual profile/catalogues, original and rebound programs (including port metadata and original request key), and bound template descriptors. Require MaxProgramBytes to cover those measured/synthesized program and profile lengths, MaxDataBytes the maximal projection and freeze, MaxObservationBytes the full observation, message descriptor and bound-reply record with authorization, MaxOutputBytes the largest complete intent array or admission descriptor. Also satisfy default-sized template/original-program reads in the public constructor and reply-recognition paths. These maxima can be conservative; the necessary byte tests still run at the actual operation. Use a sufficient runner lifetime for the measured platform or treat interruption as inconclusive. Fixed global production limits and arbitrary mathematical finiteness do not imply representability.
