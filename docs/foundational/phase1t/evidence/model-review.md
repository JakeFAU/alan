The serial selection continuation waits for children in commit order, except for J's reverse order S/e. ## What the executable checked

The Python checker uses that role grammar and a separate inventory for every shape. Its only shared conventions with the Go implementation are the declared model projection, field/role encoding and JSON format. Its expected literal products come from the independent per-leaf answer/diagnosis table, including S's scoped id and zero, h's forced answer mode, and the distinct End and public reason for a candidate with no reply. The test constructs the complete case set independently, removes each input once, rejects duplicate JSON keys, binds each whole compressed export to its digest, and checks the per-case raw-line SHA-256. It tests exact action alphabets, target bit, owner, ordered projected word, first-decision source, full transitive incoming cones, counts, maximum, per-role blocked position, delivered but unselected right siblings, charge and depth. At each completed publication all events of every descendant must lie in its strict past. For every Send, first permission and End must be incomparable. These conditions, plus the matching cone for record, admit all three relative positions of End along the two-stage reply service.

Every reachable prefix is an ideal of the independently fixed precedence relation. A topological ordering witnesses reachability for any ideal. The least ideal enabling an omitted event is precisely its strict cone, so comparing cones before layout caching checks outgoing equality at every such prefix, with successor equal to the previous ideal plus the named bit. Cyclic or missing predecessors fail reconstruction. Unique full ideal and decreasing number of missing events yield the one productive maximum, no productive infinite path and continuous eligibility of a minimal missing event. The maximum returned by each archive agrees with the frontier derived independently from selection order. In particular failed-left/pending-right has an actual selected left and the blocked right handle, while pending-left/completed-right has a delivered right with no right selection.

Count the allowed Boolean assignments by multiplying one factor per precedence edge that rejects child=1,parent=0. For each bit v, add a factor with unit multiplicity and a mark iff v=1 and all immediate successors are 0. In an ideal these marks are exactly its maximal elements, and deleting one gives one incoming cover. Conversely every cover adds one such maximal element. Sum the marks across ideals to obtain the total cover count. A partial local assignment contributes a pair (number of assignments, sum of marks). Combining independent assignments multiplies their counts and adds their marks weighted by the other factor's multiplicity. Marginalizing a bit adds both components over its two values. Eliminating all bits therefore computes the state and outgoing-cover counts exactly with Python integers. Extra transitive constraints, when present, do not change which selected elements are maximal. The largest intermediate factor used four binary variables. Each case had its typed letters, maximum and incoming cones checked before reusing counts for an identical indexed constraint layout.

The 540 distinct constraint layouts cover 2,176 declared configurations. The five shape totals and maxima in the [result](model-review.json) reproduce **5,998,514,528 states and 36,922,121,416 covers summed across cases**. The largest graph has 143 event bits. Maxima across cases divide into 88 answers, 992 diagnoses and 1,096 pending frontiers. These are exact symbolic cardinalities of this projection. The audit does not claim the corresponding number of Store executions or run an explicit multi-billion-state worklist.

## Quotient and attacks

Actual local PCs can differ at equal ideals. They can stutter only through finitely many defined local instructions before the next projected action. All erased branches/sets use defined immutable input or guarded selected fields. In the actual A/B/h instruction streams every branch moves forward, A never reads an absent result, B reads h results only after the positive-status/presence checks and h's actual answer, and the pending arm reaches a second receipt after the only original request was consumed. J checks S's original success and presence, then reads candidate answer values only on present success, otherwise classifying a failed absent envelope by its reason. The constructor checks presence before report fields, and the bounded loop body defines row fields before using them. Its member decision Set between its Send and End is also projected away (the outer decision is retained). The local finite prefix can be run just before a corresponding boundary without interfering with siblings. Resource spend can change at sibling admission, but every originally owed slot is independently funded and a failed sibling cannot close its ancestor's lifetime. A waiting caller takes no dispatch capacity. Thus the quotient preserves *weak* enabling after finite local preparation, not instantaneous equality of raw PCs or authorization sequence metadata. The inherited open B/h proof, actual positive programs, closure exclusions, representable bounds and weak service are necessary premises. An observed matching-envelope reduction error would invalidate that projection for its program; inability to execute it is never a pending maximum.

A first decision must belong to the originally bound issuing run and follows the projected local prefix at its own edge. An ended reply sender still qualifies on this path (stable generation/revision, recognized bound Send). Its current status is reconstructed at the decision on the ordered path. A saved lookup can be repeated with that earlier metadata after different subsequent history, but cannot make a new decision, consume a sibling observation or perform admission. Original reply disposition, End and first permission need not be simultaneous. Service of the original predecessor is continuous until it occurs. An infinite suffix of polls or lookups at a proper ideal with this work owed is unfair. The projection has no cutoff on sibling scheduling.

Within atomic Store words some physical rows are omitted. Admission writes binding/subject/member rows, original request and a protocol fact before the charge; abstract words identify admission, creation, request and spend. Invoke publication actually writes publication → outcome fact → accepted result row → authenticated observation and delivery mapping. The exported word projects out the redundant result row. Root settlement writes root publication and a same-transaction outcome fact; its abstract root_publish identifies this whole transaction. Reply disposition includes the protocol fact and transport admission record. Subsequent original-key acknowledgments, further transport identities reusing the same reply disposition, unsuccessful preparation, saved authorizations, and wrong-correlation selection attempts can stutter. These omissions preserve the chosen contract observation and service of other continuously owed actions, given adequate operational headroom and healthy original storage. They do not preserve byte-exact histories, arbitrary transport queries, raw phase counts or all driver attempt outcomes. Acknowledgment is outside the choice of what it means for a captured reply to be settled here.

All 17 adverse histories have legal prefixes in the independent full-budget model. At each cut its next action or its absence has the expected guard/owner/word, including the authentic right-only publication while a left handle is required, failed-left/pending-right, two distinct diagnoses preserved in order, correct helper complement, and absence on an authentic missing-reply selection. The first reply ordering mutation literally adds an End prerequisite to permission (pre-End permission disappears), the second adds permission as a prerequisite to End (post-End permission disappears). The control identifiers name those cuts ambiguously; the archived histories and mutations give the precise directions. The three scarce resource prefixes really demand the ninth I or R slot or the fourth depth on BB, each violating exactly one bound. The fault-as-pending mutation inserts a fictitious wait into the graph. Evidence of a genuine absent-operand reducer error belongs to the separate runtime/retained-machine campaign. These attacks do not falsify the guarded positive model.
