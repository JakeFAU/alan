# Independent review of the retained Phase 1T Store runs

**APPROVE the finite runtime evidence.** All 80 automatic cases, the 14 named manual cases and the separate consumer matched the audited transitions and outcomes. The verdict covers this archive and its executable fixtures. The all-terms proof and exhaustive abstract-model claim require their separate reviews.

## Population and method

The checker independently enumerates the frozen 64 two-leaf cases and 16 nested/relay cases. It joins all 174 exported files to a passing named test and its logged JSON-value digest. Starting at the single fixed root, it derives a path from each original invocation intent, checks its per-caller declaration, the admitting authority's first decision and charge, and independently computes the invocation, binding, member, template and original request/outcome observation identities. Program IDs hash the actual retained bytes. The installed template catalogues, declared schemas, member initializers and concrete per-occurrence programs must agree. Reusing the same template or application ID cannot alias two roles. Outer program and top template identities for every placement are listed in the [machine-readable results](runtime-audit-results.json).

For each run the checker starts at the retained initializer and interprets the exercised instructions in Python, tracking defined registers, captured cursors, guards, original handles, entry and return bindings, and Send payloads. It compares every committed successor, selected observation and emitted intent, and hashes ordered execution, authorization, lifecycle, admission, freeze, reply and publication evidence against the Store's recorded fact digests. It checks captured replies and actual child selections precede a completed parent's first publication. The oracle computes the expected report for each occurrence from its input, independently of both this instruction interpreter and the generated classifier. For an incomplete tree it checks the actual blocked Await, earlier selections and the later sibling's own progress. The direct candidate check includes the consumed-first/blocked-second request at PC 11 in A and PC 12 in B. Automatic reports must be quiescent with all original entry requests consumed at the predicted balances and limits. Each replay's projected runs, count and root summaries agree with the separate inspection.

The 80 automatic stores contained 688 accepted dynamic invocations, 12,315 committed steps, 479 bound reply dispositions and 559 original child publications. Four outer roots answered, 38 diagnosed and 38 reached a serviced pending boundary. The separate consumer's nine templates and ten retained programs match the manifest, provision, run and replay. Its ordered failure/missing/answer product used 14 debits, 374 steps and 40 dispatches.

- In the open late-reply control, helper Send, End, first permission and record occurred at facts 34, 35, 37 and 38. In the cancellation control they occurred at 34, 37, 39 and 40. These prefixes give no post-cancellation completion result.
- The three nonconforming child programs delivered a wrong present value, an absent failed result, or a successful End with `missing_reply`. Each bad result was genuinely selected. The owning Fork either waited for its pending sibling or selected both original children and propagated absence to a failed outer decision. In the separate fault witness, the actual absent selection removed a previously defined `selected_id=73`. PC 4 then attempted to read it. The runner returned `missing or invalid data "selected_id"` and `Quiescent=false`.
- Seven detached audit mutations exercised swapped sibling delivery, substituted diagnosis, publication before captured reply disposition, a fault relabelled quiescent, a denied authorization relabelled accepted, ancestor closure moved past reply and a fabricated product behind a pending left child. Each failed the indicated semantic check. These inputs were modified in memory after decoding.
