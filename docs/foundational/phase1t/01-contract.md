# Owning-tree execution contract and qualification freeze

A term is a finite Leaf(A/B), Relay(U) or Fork(L,R). Leaf is the historical J around the exact original candidate and S. Relay and Fork are new singleton templates. One O_T is a separately provisioned root. The term and assignments are fixed independently of its inputs. Each leaf receives any of the sixteen D_N requests independently. Every numeric application value and every id remains literal.

**Type and API.** Leaf request and report are the historical closed schemas. Fork prefixes each immediate field of its left and right requests and reports with `l_` and `r_`, recursively. Relay preserves field names. Each leaf role thus owns id/value/mode on input and id/classification/answers on output. Retain the original list element {id,value}, MaxItems one and original byte limit. No dynamic recursive wire type or extra runtime adapter. Equal ids, values, template IDs or identical child implementations cannot identify an occurrence: the original call, authenticated envelope and static role must agree. Public Go construction in examples/owningtree exposes Leaf(candidate), Relay(child), Fork(left,right) returning (author.Template,error), and Outer(child) returning (author.Artifact,error). Provision(child, request map[string]schema.Value, limits runtime.ResourceLimits) returns (runtime.Provision,error), a sole generation-one root named root. A/B leaves are the positive domain; a schema-compatible argument can be substituted for adversarial testing. Outer input fields are the tree request fields. Its decision register is answer, diagnosed, or invalid_tree_outcome. Await declaration names are tree at O_T, child at Relay, left/right at Fork.

**Retained policy.** A generated parent receives the original request, then commits each declared child (left then right at Fork) before any child selection. It waits on the exact saved original operation in that order. Validation depends on literal id/value and selected presence, envelope status/reason, classification and answer data, never on requested mode. Check each report id against its own path. Each answer requires exactly one matching id with value 1−input.value; each candidate_failed/candidate_missing_reply requires an empty list. Reject invalid_outcome, malformed cardinality/values and inconsistent envelope. A valid whole child is succeeded/member_succeeded iff every role answered, otherwise failed/member_failed; both have a present report. Validate before reading optional fields, and iterate only populated lists. Status/presence semantics and real selection determine eligibility. A mismatched operation/provenance/source/generation/obligation is ineligible. It is not permission to skip a required select.

Once both original outcomes have been selected, send the correctly prefixed selected fields exactly once on the parent's own binding and then End success iff all answered, else Failure. Relay applies the same rule to one child. An invalid selection sets a local invalid condition; continue to await remaining children. After all selections End Failure without sending a normal report. An authentic absent failed/member_failed from this path is invalid evidence one level up. Successful End without a bound reply gives a distinct failed/missing_reply envelope. Positive settlement requires that at a term's first public publication all owned work and captured replies are settled (its own caller selection may remain). Defensive controls only guarantee guarded consumption, no invented normal report, and the required waits for actually selected child envelopes; an arbitrary failed child need not have settled descendants.

**Serviced frontier.** With no closure, extra input or pending release, independently runnable siblings continue even while a parent waits. At each blocked parent the first unpublished child in selection order is awaited; any earlier completed child is already selected. A later completed child's genuine delivered outcome may remain unconsumed. No blocked ancestor sends or ends. A finite prefix, operand fault, withheld service, capacity refusal, exhausted watchdog or failed checker cannot certify this frontier.

**Resources and bridge.** Leaf b=4, h=3. Relay adds one to b and h. Fork b=1+bL+bR and h=1+max(hL,hR). Both I/R budgets fund b including term entry, root excluded, E0. Require actual representable schema/instruction/template/identifier/sequence and byte limits, stable grants/revisions/generations and lawful bindings. Private member creation, authenticity and selection remain original even with budget slack. Track actual shared spend and remaining slots separately from a subtree's local view. Capacity one; a blocked Await holds no transport capacity. K_store_tree is the positive open Store account, including first authorization, later current admission, saved dispositions, and private faults. Weak service applies to continuously eligible original instructions, admissions, bound reply dispositions, publications, delivery/selection, and outer service. The actual generator must establish parent lifetime.

**Evidence freeze.** Qualify the abstract two-leaf product on 256 ordered request pairs × four assignments. For both three-leaf associations exercise 64 mode vectors × eight assignments, and relays above a fork and in a branch. Numeric partitioning must preserve every local comparison and include duplicate ids. Public two-leaf runs cover all 16 ordered mode pairs × four placements. Enumerate assignment AA,AB,BA,BB and modes answer/fail/absent/pending. Use left (id=1,value=0), right (id=1 or 2,value=1), exercising repeated and distinct identifiers. Include nested mixed diagnoses and answers, pending on both sides, relay above/within and actual aggregate consumption. Exercise an all-B fork at b=9, h=4 with I8/R9, I9/R8, and depth3 in isolation (expect invocation_limit, created_run_limit, depth_limit). Freeze the concrete last-slot schedule before the relevant Store run. Include delayed right completion with left pending, failed left with pending right, sibling spending and post-End first bound-reply permission. Cancel only O in the branching control after a valid committed bound Send but before first authorization, verify ordering and disposition rather than progress. Keep targeted attacks on invalid selected reports, wrong correlation, optional-field faults, and detached graph mutations separately identified. Pin numeric limits in fixtures before execution. Archive actual outputs/identities separately for each gate.

**Outer and settlement cut.** Provision validates each leaf's literal request domain (id 1 or 2, value 0 or 1, one of the four modes) and the complete closed input record. O_T has one child and no outward reply port. It records answer and Ends Success for an all-answer product, diagnosed and Ends Failure for a valid mixed/failed product, invalid_tree_outcome and Ends Failure on a selected invalid or absent envelope. No decision or End occurs without selection. A successful or diagnosed tree entry has committed exactly one original bound reply with its captured disposition accepted before its publication. At that cut all required child calls are already selected and their owned footprints settled, including failed children in the positive family. A Store invocation publication and insertion of the caller's outcome delivery share a transaction; later selection is separate. Administrative acknowledgement, where separate from disposition, is not another semantic acceptance.

**Interfaces between workers.** The executable Go handoff is the public Template and its actual Bytes()/Schemas(); role catalogues are derived from actual declarations, never from application id. Public tests record a gzipped JSON object per case with shape, leaf assignment, ordered requests, declared bounds, report and full runtime.Inspection (original field names), plus read-only replay where used. The independent model exports a deterministic machine-readable per-domain census with exact shape/assignment/request or an explicitly expanded equivalence class, states, outgoing edges, maximum/frontier and cutoff/coverage status. Its reference and eligibility auditor use no constructor outputs to predict a leaf report. Actual guarantees quantify over finite terms that pass recursive type/canonical-schema/identifier/compiler checks with adequate current root catalogues, depth, I/R allowance, and concrete bounds on program, projections, observations, intent arrays, steps and dispatch. Record a pre-run bound from the emitted PC graph per occurrence and the fixed singleton slot inventory. A legal D_N input never implies a valid deployment by itself.
