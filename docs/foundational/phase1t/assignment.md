3. Freeze the target before constructing its evidence
3.1 The finite typed grammar
Use the family
\[
T ::= \operatorname{Leaf}(X)\mid\operatorname{Relay}(T)\mid\operatorname{Fork}(T_L,T_R),\qquad X\in\{A,B\}.
\]
Leaf(X) is the existing callable owning J[X]. Preserve its original candidate and S interactions. A Fork is one ordinary owning invocation that commits both child invocations before awaiting either. Use left-then-right selection as the frozen policy. Completion order remains unconstrained.

Types follow the term. A leaf uses the existing request and J diagnostic. A relay preserves its child's request/report types. A Fork accepts the product of its children's requests and returns their diagnostic product. Implement these finite products with statically prefixed flat schema fields, preserving each leaf's existing bounded answer list. Do not assume recursive wire types. Review the actual schema encoding and the defensive no-report failure path before freezing them. Any necessary adapter is a real run whose cost and depth must be counted.

Each leaf has its own (id, value, mode) from D_N: id in {1,2}, value in {0,1}, and mode in {answer,fail,absent,pending}. Distinct leaves may have identical application IDs and values. Their static tree roles, original invocation handles, and protocol provenance distinguish them. Literal application data must not be renamed as operation identities.

Build a new ordinary outer consumer O_T for the term's product type. The unchanged single-leaf O remains historical. O_T is one separately provisioned root; its fixed run is outside the dynamic tree allowance. It consumes the tree through its original handle and validates the selected public envelope and product report.
3.2 The behavioral contract K_tree
The specification must distinguish these cases:
Situation	Required result
All leaves answer	Each leaf contributes its authentic id-preserving complement; the tree has a present product report and successful outcome.
All leaves complete, at least one fails or omits its reply	The tree has a present diagnostic product and a failed outcome. Preserve every leaf's diagnosis and every successful leaf's answer. candidate_failed and candidate_missing_reply remain distinct, including when both occur in the same tree.
Some leaf is legitimately pending	The appropriate ancestors remain unpublished at their actual Await instructions. No completed aggregate is invented. Independently runnable sibling work must still reach its specified boundary under service.
An authentic selected child outcome has an absent, inconsistent, or semantically invalid report	Apply the defensive no-report failure policy below. Do not read absent fields or fabricate valid leaf diagnostics. These adversarial components are outside the positive leaf premise.
An observation has the wrong operation, provenance, generation, or other protocol selector	It must not satisfy the original Await. Reject or skip it according to the actual runtime rules, without an unauthorized consumption. This observation alone does not establish legitimate pending.


Each normal parent report must be justified by actual selected original child outcomes. The parent may pass modes to children, but must not manufacture the answer or diagnosis by consulting the requested mode. Validate presence before optional reads, status/reason consistency, role and request correlation, bounded cardinality, and the actual report values. Public envelope validation alone does not establish semantic truth; the child proof supplies that premise.

A completed conforming Fork selects both original child outcomes, commits its own bounded report, and then Ends. It succeeds iff all constituent leaf reports are valid answers. A diagnosed failure remains a failed invocation with a present report. A failed child paired with a pending child does not authorize early completion under this owning contract.

For invalid selected evidence, use a concrete defensive policy: record the invalid condition locally, continue the required original child selections, and then End with Failure without committing a normal product reply. A Fork still waits for both child outcomes; if either remains pending, it does not complete. Its eventual absent failed outcome propagates as invalid immediate-child evidence through new relays/forks, and O_T records invalid_tree_outcome and fails. Preserve the inherited J schema and distinguish an absent failed outcome from successful End with missing_reply. Normal product reports contain only valid selected child reports; invent no leaf diagnosis for an invalid subtree. Prove positive settlement for conforming children and state separately what the defensive path guarantees.

Define the pending frontier as a serviced maximal or eventual boundary, not a classification of every finite scheduling prefix. With Await(left); Await(right), a pending left child can prevent selection of an already completed right child. The right outcome may remain published and delivered but unconsumed. At the serviced boundary, earlier completed children have been selected and the blocked parent waits on its first unpublished child in its fixed order. A finite prefix may still await a published child whose delivery or selection is outstanding. No complete product is published until all required selections occur. A reducer fault, withheld continuously eligible service, limit exhaustion, or search cutoff is not legitimate pending.

For completed publication, retain the strong settlement property: all owned descendants and captured replies are settled by first public publication, including on diagnosed failure. Distinguish local End, invocation publication, delivery, and outer aggregate publication throughout.
3.3 Shared resources and legal placements
For direct J leaves and one-member relay/fork constructors, use these common upper bounds on both dynamic invocations and created runs, including each term's entry:
Term	b(T)	Maximum depth from caller h(T)
Leaf	4	3
Relay(U)	1+b(U)	1+h(U)
Fork(L,R)	1+b(L)+b(R)	1+max(h(L),h(R))


Provision I and R at least b(T), depth at least h(T), effects zero, and a separately counted fixed outer root. Qualify physical capacity one. Waiting parents must not reserve transport capacity. Account for any extra instrumentation or adapters separately.

Prove a global finite-slot invariant: each accepted original occurrence charges once; every lawful future admission maps injectively to a remaining generated slot. At every prefix, actual spend plus remaining lawful demand fits the common allowance. Ghost allocations used in the proof introduce no runtime reservation. A has three actual J/e/S slots; B has four including h. Actual charges can differ across substitutions. This is preservation of the behavioral guarantee under a common bound, not exact-debit or trace equivalence.

Do not represent all sibling interference by S's original single q. Prove how multiple external-to-a-subtree debits project while retaining actual root charges, sufficient headroom, and eligibility. Authorization, current source eligibility, parent catalogues, original bindings, scope and depth need separate arguments; spare budget grants no authority.

Every quantified term must be finite and legal at its actual placement: stable bindings and authority, fresh original occurrences, adequate actual identifier/data sizes, numerical bounds and operational limits. Derive usable per-term adequacy bounds. Mathematical finiteness alone does not promise deployment within fixed finite production limits.
3.4 Runtime scope
Define a separately named K_store_tree account. Its positive environment performs no external cancellation or closure, supplies no extraneous requests/debits, performs no retirement or generation/revision changes, and never releases the designated unmatched receive. Prove from the generated parents' code that they themselves remain open until their required selections; do not assume this central ownership property as an environmental premise. Assume eventual service only for continuously eligible work; state the exact service obligations.

Retain original commitment, immutable first authorization, later current admission, bound reply disposition, actual private reductions and faults, End/freeze, publication, delivery, and selected consumption. Respect actual atomic transactions. A valid captured reply may first authorize after sender End. A saved authorization lookup is not a new admission or receiver consumption.

Provide a source-backed cancellation disposition table separately. Distinguish ancestor/root closure from cancellation of the actual source or caller, and distinguish invocation, ordinary message, and original bound reply paths.
