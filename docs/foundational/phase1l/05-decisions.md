# Phase 1L decisions and scope

C_inv owns both required invocations under one parent and joins their original
outcomes. This deliberately changes the context examined in Phases 1I/J/K;
their static-peer failures and authoring obstruction remain historical evidence.
No ordinary report/ACK protocol or root result-message obligation is added.

The shared allowance is the K1 worst case, I=R3/depth2/E0. A and B may spend
different amounts within it. Dynamic charges, fixed provisioning and physical
capacity are separate observations; this experiment does not claim exact-debit
equivalence. The positive profile fixes four answer payloads, stable identities
and grants, no cancellation, and eventual eligible service.
