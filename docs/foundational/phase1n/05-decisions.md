# Phase 1N decisions and preserved limitations

## Semantic choices

The new J is directly callable through its public port. O is one fixed root
consuming one original J handle; J owns original e/S handles and consumes both
before normal classification. The former C_reason root is not relabeled callable
or hidden behind a forwarding wrapper. Report data contain application id, tag
and zero-or-one original answers, never serialized inner handles.

Diagnosed errors remain actual failed invocations with present diagnostics.
Candidate absence, J missing report after Success, and J missing report after
Failure have different public evidence. O guards presence/cardinality/correlation
and commits an observed decision; status alone cannot invent candidate_failed.
Neither J nor O branches on requested mode to supply a diagnosis.

Public authenticity proves provenance, not truth. A genuine false-report control
passes binding authentication and O's faithful public policy while failing the
constructor truth obligation. Observation-versus-request disagreement instead
preserves truthful policy and rejects component mode conformance. These controls
are not substitution counterexamples with both premises satisfied.

J's prior admission is a fixed debit; original S is the sole arbitrary-time q
debit. The depth shift and residual allowance are explicit. No outer sibling is
added: it would require another environment-interference argument. A/B spending
differs under a common envelope, preserving the original exact-debit definition
without claiming that the candidates satisfy it.

ARG-1 is the reviewed conditional constructor rule with a supplied eventwise or
guard-stable relation, bilateral readiness/authorization/resource premises and
separate progress. ARG-2, deriving those premises from arbitrary weak unchanged
K1 refinement, remains open and not disproved. Finite A/B acceptance instantiates
the former, not the latter. No new theorem number or general congruence status is
introduced. Historical I–M counterexamples and recovery results retain their scope.

The model, finite instruction evaluator, genuine executions, raw audit and replay
remain separate evidence categories. Ghost classification/decision and atomic
publication/delivery expansions do not create durable checkpoints. The supported
late-reply trace demonstrates the actual post-End first authorization boundary;
it supplies no universal scheduler or recovery claim.
