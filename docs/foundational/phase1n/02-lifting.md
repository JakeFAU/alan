## Obligations and public environment

Keep separate:

1. X refines K1 under the aligned residual local environment, with bilateral
   environment readiness and separate all-mode progress/nondivergence.
2. J[K1] satisfies K_join under the declared public caller environment E.
3. The fixed constructor lifts the supplied relation: J[X] refines J[K1] under E.
4. The fixed O policy preserves G_outer for a conforming K_join component.

E supplies one original valid request/binding and stable generation, revision,
grants, schemas and routes; sufficient residual allowance/depth and operational
limits; no cancellation/retirement; an open eligible caller through original J
selection; and eventual eligible local/protocol service. It supplies no pending
release. J owns and remains open through both original child consumptions. The
constructor rule is parameterized by these public environment/ownership facts,
not by O's private PCs or registers. O is the concrete fixed caller realization
whose own control is checked separately in the outer corollary.

K_join requires truthful classification of the actual original e/S selected
envelopes, original once-only joins, one diagnostic and correct J publication,
payload/resource safety and mode progress. Its checker projects away O's private
decision, End and root-publication events. G_outer additionally requires original
J delivery/selection, O's actual committed guarded decision/End and matching root
publication. A public-policy monitor sees no e/S evidence; an authenticated lie
can satisfy that policy while failing K_join truth. Neither public validity nor
a safe prefix certifies conformance or eventual progress.

## Exact residual/depth mapping and reuse boundary

admission contributes prior offset p=(1,1,0), leaving I=R3. e and S are at depth2;
the optional helper is at depth3. J is never treated as another arbitrary-time

The new full state stores physical depths. Its inner reducer adapter uses the
explicit bijection `d_relative=d_physical-1`, `L_relative=L_full-1`, while inner
spent excludes J. For any inner admission:

    d_physical > D_full  iff  d_relative > D_full-1
    1 + spent_inner >= L_full  iff  spent_inner >= L_full-1

The inverse adds one to e/h/S depths only. Original payloads, flags, identities,
authorization dispositions, refusals, charges, publications and consumptions are
unchanged. This is an algebraic guard correspondence, not inferred equivalence
from matching totals. The new state checker applies the old inner invariant only
after this explicit inverse normalization. `CheckCallableJoinProjectionEdge`
also checks directed/full-search edges against the actual physical local graph
with EntryDepth2/Depth3, residual I=R3 and q, rather than invoking the old M
depth1/cap2 qualifier. Its reference instance is exercised on directed paths;
exhaustive projection and A/B local checks remain Task3b.

The physical local projection begins after J admission. It copies e/h, their

## Explicit lifted relation

Let R be an asymmetric local relation supplied for X versus K1. Its domain is
the declared event semantics, not arbitrary hidden host behavior. The original
K1 requirements include equal public requests, ledgers, original obligation
links/protocol stages/outcomes, event transfer and bilateral declared environment
readiness; pure stutters change none of those facts. Progress/nondivergence is a
separate premise. Consistent identity renaming is fixed once for root, subjects,
generation-qualified runs, operations, intents, observations and original handles.
Application id/value remain literal data, not renameable protocol identities.

Define L_R^E between full constructor states by conjunction of:

- Related projected e/helper states in R and the same original S state.
- Matching J semantic phase, original request/handles, public caller identity
  and binding, and public E/ownership/lifetime obligations.
- Matching original commitments, admissions/refusals, selected consumptions and
  retained envelopes, including status, reason and explicit presence. Delivery
  never substitutes for selection; helper outcome never substitutes for e.
- Equal full ledgers with the same prior J offset, residual allowance, physical
  depths, future demand and sole q/S accepted-admission mapping.
- Equal first authorization dispositions with original source identity,
  revision/generation and binding conditions; pending reply obligations remain
  retained after local End.
- Matching classification, diagnostic, diagnostic Send/authorization/admission,
  J End/publication/delivery and original public caller selection, when present.

The pre-admission prefix is the same fixed public entry protocol with no child
state. Accepted J admission initializes R at the aligned residual boundary.
After e completion R retains its selected envelope while fixed J proceeds.
L_R^E does not compare O's private decision/terminal state. For the O corollary,
add matching O control, retained original J envelope, committed public decision,
End and root/aggregate publication. `CallableJoinRelated(..., outer)` encodes
these two finite relation instances; the A/B witness erases only their private
candidate PCs/registers. The generic relation is supplied R, not that erasure.

## Transfer cases and the two bridges

The proposed transfer argument is induction over constructor transitions,
maintaining the preceding relation and immutable identities.

| Transition | Matching case |
| --- | --- |
| Public J commitment/authorization/admission/request | Same fixed entry guards, original binding and open caller give the same protocol fact and prior debit; request consumption precedes all inner work. |
| X private instruction | R gives a finite pure stutter/matching path without changed public protocol, payload, obligations or spending. Its absence of infinite stuttering is a separate premise. |
| Candidate/helper protocol | Transfer the exact original event under R; helper work remains one original invocation and its selected outcome precedes mode action. |
| S accepted admission | Identical S/J readiness gives the same accepted singleton event; bilateral q readiness preserves R after its sole debit. |
| Other S event | Execute identical original S protocol on both sides; projected candidate state stutters. Preserve any refusal/accounting distinction. |
| J e-then-S commitment | Same J phase commits the same original request/handle and zero debit; no candidate-private branch can create this owner action. |
| J S-then-e selection | Same original delivered envelope and J gate; both original consumes occur once before any normal classification, even if S is invalid. |
| Classification/decision/report | Equality of actual selected envelopes and original input gives the same guarded tag, zero-or-one answer record and terminal decision. No mode branch supplies the diagnosis. |
| J reply service | Equal immutable reply/source/binding and retained disposition give the same authorization/admission, including first authorization after End as detailed below. |
| J End/publication | Same Send precedes End; publication waits for reply disposition and retains an accepted diagnostic on Failure as well as Success. Original already-selected descendants satisfy successful publication's joins. |
| Public delivery/selection | Retain and select the original J envelope under E's open eligible caller, without changing child/J reasons. |
| O corollary | Equal public envelope and input give the same guarded committed decision, End and root publication; missing/inconsistent J evidence gives invalid_join_outcome. |

**Delayed e-selection bridge.** The local harness allows delivered e selection
before S completes; the fixed constructor gates that caller-owned action until
S is consumed. Both sides have the same S state and gate. Since selected flags
are monotone and are part of R's public protocol projection, a reference matching
path for an event whose endpoint still has e unconsumed cannot consume e as an
intermediate step and later restore it. For a path matching actual e selection,
the common S gate is already true. Pure stutters change none of this gate's
dependencies. Once e publishes, K1 has frozen it, so delaying caller selection
cannot withhold an input needed for further candidate work. S is independently
finite and eventually serviced, making the gate eventually available whenever
e has delivered. These facts justify restricting the local caller selection on
both sides; ordinary trace inclusion without the monotone selected-state and
readiness premises would not.

**Split-authorization bridge.** Extend R with the same retained authorization
sidecars. Authorization is an explicit full-product event but a pure stutter in
the coarse local projection. Its guard depends on matching original commitment,
source open state, fixed revision/generation and binding. Later admission retains
the historical disposition while checking current source/scope/depth/budget
guards. Every dependency agrees; resource/depth guards commute with the mapping
above. For inner work, J stays open through both joins and e stays open until its
helper is selected, so no constructor-created closure interrupts a required
admission. A pure matching stutter cannot change these public guard dependencies.
Refused authorization and final source/scope refusal keep their own reasons and
zero debit. A relation that only matches final totals or ignores source identity
would not supply this bridge.

For a general weak event simulation, the proof needs its matching paths to
preserve these public dependencies at intermediate pure stutters and to contain
no additional nonmatching protocol events. This is the event refinement intended
by K1's explicit facts/pure-stutter clause, not schema compatibility. The new
finite checker uses the stronger single-event relation already witnessed by
historical A/B local checks. Independent review must assess the generic weak-path
reading and the progress transfer separately; if that reading cannot be derived
from K1 as written, it is the precise remaining generic lifting premise. Do not
quietly redefine K1 or treat successful finite instances as its proof.

## Progress, maxima and finite bounds

Under entitlement and eventual eligible service, original S and any chosen
helper have their finite answer paths; e's separate all-mode progress supplies
its exact terminal envelope or designated unmatched receive. J's finite owner
prefix cannot permanently withhold an enabled entry or original selection, and
its post-join classification/report/End suffix is finite. Equal reply binding
and eventual protocol service discharge the post-End reply obligation. E supplies
eligible caller service; fixed O then has a finite guarded suffix. Infinite
candidate-private stuttering is excluded by the separate local premise, not by
simulation safety. Infinite idle/retry/restart-only suffixes violate eventual
service. These assumptions do not make designated pending terminate.

Pending requires actual original request consumption, successful consumed helper
if chosen, actual unmatched candidate receive, and S published/delivered/selected.
J is open at original e await with no classification/report/End/publication;
O is open at original J await without decision/End/root publication. Neither S
delivery nor a safe pre-pending prefix is enough. A trace checker reports these
facts; only zero successors establish an explored maximum. The explorer retains
every maximum and checks every edge before deduplication without failure pruning
or cutoff. Its key includes pending/classification/public report/selection and
all future-relevant monitor memory. Component-monitor memory is derivable from
the keyed inner operation records, as in the reviewed M machinery.

Let O_factor, M and P be M's conservative per-operation, inner model and protocol
monitor Cartesian bounds, quoted here as formulas rather than measured counts:

    O_factor = 4*3*3*9*3*4*3*2^7*6^3*4*10*7
    M = 16*7*9*2*3^6*16*7*3^16 * O_factor^3

    full model <= 5M * 2^11 * D * Q * 5 * 6 * 3

The factors include ten J milestones plus root publication, O's five stages,
six decision values and three terminal values. These are loose finite storage

Rank is `ConsumerReasonRank(inner) + 100*(J milestone bits + O stage + root
