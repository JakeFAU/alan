## Scope decision

Retain the reviewed application and unchanged evidence helper for the heldout comparison; do not tune prompts, retriever ranking, questions or gold against these outcomes. This preserves a single explicitly reported implementation and makes the measured limitations visible. Query-contract presentation and more selective retrieval are candidate later application improvements, not evidence for a missing interpreter capability. The success claim is bounded authorability, genuine evidence-dependent decisions and tested recovery, with usefulness reported from independent grades rather than assumed. No claim that reviewed composition always improves an answer or reliably finds all relevant evidence follows from this run.

Independent grading uses frozen facts and exact source spans and is performed without the system mapping or application review judgments. Grades are separate artifacts; this finding record reports observable mechanics, not final quality scores. Heldout remains sealed at this decision.
