Saffron worker 9 readiness record, revision 1, defines production fast and bulk profiles; neither profile is the default.
Saffron worker 9 production queue limit: the fast profile allows 400 queued jobs; the bulk profile allows 900 queued jobs. A profile must be specified to select one limit.
Saffron worker 9 production rollback: for either fast or bulk, restore worker 8 if job failures exceed 4 percent for six consecutive minutes.
