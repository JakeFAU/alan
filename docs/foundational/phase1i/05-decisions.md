# Phase 1I decisions

## Initial decisions, before candidate authorship

- Treat the attached assignment as authorization to resolve routine design and
  review internally. No additional human design-approval round is required.
- Reuse the clean isolated managed worktree on a new branch from fetched main;
  preserve the merged H branch and its evidence unchanged.
- Choose contract-relative preservation under maximum remaining demand, rather
  than pairwise trace equality for implementations with permitted cost variation.
  Insufficient-budget cases remain real safety/distinguishing inputs.
- Use five named finite contexts, not an implicit universal context grammar.
  Separate pure conformance, weak-budget, equal-charge scheduling and effectful
  uncertainty profiles; any positive result must identify its profile.
- A source-run authorization after root closure is not automatically a bug:
  the applicable root admission/reservation check is distinct. Existing committed
  reservations and late evidence retain their original identity and uncertainty.
- No production/compiler change is currently justified. Existing public API and
  process barriers appear sufficient; this is a hypothesis subject to witnesses.
- Scope qualification to changed research/application boundaries and focused
  relevant regressions. Prior H broad suites keep their original source labels.

## Results and subsequent decisions

Revision 1 was subsequently approved at its frozen digest before implementation.
The frozen file remains unchanged, including its historical pending-review header;
03-review records the later verdict. Do not confuse this specification approval
with implementation/model approval.

1. **Retain the negative instead of repairing runtime policy.** Resource
   entitlement does not keep a root open for an independently provisioned peer.
   Ordinary root Success can reject the peer's S admission as `scope_closed`.
   Preserve all eight initially entitled parallel cases and their failures.
   The other 76 entitled cases complete in the finite boundary products.
   A future guarantee needs an explicit root-lifetime/join premise or a changed
   context/program; neither was retrofitted into K1 to erase this result.
2. **Name the correspondence repair.** The original model's root-close convention
   differs from runtime: successful root termination fences admission, and root
   cancellation does not cancel an independently provisioned peer. The separate
   `RuntimeBoundaryGraph` records both differences. It preserves `Closed=false`
   for success and initial entitlement; matching its outcome is not a passing Gc.
   Original graph/results stay available with their narrower claims.
3. **Justify the added bound.** A separately frozen static-peer fixture adds 32
   ordinary Set steps to realize the abstract root-before-peer schedule through
   unmodified `Store.Run`. This is a new concrete artifact/scope, not another
   scheduler or evidence that the original deterministic fixture chose it.
4. **Separate conformance from exact equivalence.** A/B may have different
   K1-permitted demand. W_A/W_B expose the weaker schema/result promise at two
   admissions, outside Definition4.6's exact-debit premise. P/Q have the same
   demand and differ by one pure step: finite language equality holds for the
   three tested capacities, while 16 concrete samples found no different winner.
   Neither fact proves general interference stability or confluence.
5. **Keep independent checks fallible and visible.** The v1 collapsed boundary
   and v2 leaked payload defects are retained with source and RED evidence.
   Direct contract checks and runtime samples corrected the model. Replay and
   reference/candidate graph self-agreement alone did not establish the claim.
   An extra-helper model negative was further corrected to return the expected
   complement so its intended charge violation is isolated; original output and
   source remain in the development snapshot.
   Independent review also exposed lost consumed observation identity/payload,
   omitted member freeze validation and incorrect closure classification in the
   runtime decoder. Correct these in the test-only observation boundary, retain
   the five original mutation inputs and 18 cancellation misclassifications, and
   require raw consistency for every profile. The scoped rereview approved the
   correction after 61 cases; no production integrity failure is inferred.
   Final review additionally required actual P/Q/W artifact transcription instead
   of B-derived surrogates. Preserve the surrogate results and source; mapped
   original-protocol language equality is separate from runtime-boundary membership.
6. **Label environmental authority.** Closure fixtures may step an explicitly
   provisioned operator's own ordinary cancellation intent/decision at the
   observed boundary. Candidate policy still runs automatically. Fixed operator
   provisioning cost and authority do not become dynamic candidate charges or
   demonstrated supervisory policy.
7. **Keep effect profiles distinct.** Unknown non-idempotent work keeps original
   identity and spending across reopening without recontact. Authentic late
   evidence uses a surviving live transport. No new reconciliation API,
   business-level idempotence, or effectful substitution result is inferred.
8. **Narrow the paper at the actual dependency.** Preserve exact-debit Definition
   4.6; withdraw the unsupported confluence/normal-form argument, mark general
   Theorem4.8 and dependent future-recovery simulations open, and keep replay and
   conservative uncertainty safety separate. No general loop-ranking repair or
   whole-dissertation consistency claim. New references were unnecessary.
9. **Focused gates are appropriate to this boundary.** Production sources and
   dependencies are unchanged. Run new model/application/process/race gates and
   relevant old semantic/invocation/closure regressions. Preserve H inputs/results
   under their original labels rather than claiming a new whole-H campaign.

These choices cost generality: the delivered result concerns the specified finite
graphs and sampled local-runtime schedules. Wrong assumptions require a new
counterexample and revised claim, not reinterpretation of these records.

## Remaining theoretical obligations

An interference-stable, separately checkable component relation closed under
invocation, shared resources, root lifetime, hiding, refusal and divergence;
the connection between its nondeterministic schedules and concrete Store.Run;
compiler correctness; future recovery/world-response coupling; general loop
rank; arbitrary recursion or multiple substitution positions. None is discharged
by review agreement or by the bounded corpus. In-scope decoder/harness defects
found during review must be corrected and requalified before delivery.
