# Decision and next research task

**Pillar IV.** The tested boundary is first source permission, then reservation and any member debit, then a worker that can launch later. Source closure wins before first permission under trusted dispatch. The former theorem's physical-launch clause is false on both routes. Existing raw idempotent work may also be re-exposed after closure. A valid late result still belongs to its original operation. A typed unsuccessful result and knowledge that the remote mutation did not happen are different propositions. These conclusions do not adopt a supervisor or endpoint policy.

**Pillar V.** Across the selected cuts, useful progress depends jointly on stored identity, endpoint semantics, dispatcher behavior and the live consumer. Reopen plus the raw route's permitted immutable duplicate resolves both sides of a matched idempotent pair. A result retained before settlement or selection also enables progress after reopen. The non-idempotent pair establishes the information obstruction without a decisive reconciliation capability. The adapter conservatively withholds already reserved work even at this capable endpoint. A parent may keep waiting, may select a local failure despite a missing effect result, or may select an authenticated successful child response. No general symmetric recovery relation, exact expenditure claim or effectful composition theorem follows.

**Decision.** Study a source-and-scope authority fence at the next mutating launch. This is the smallest change that could resolve a falsified safety premise on both dispatch paths without first choosing a universal policy for unknown outcomes. Define the exact linearization event and actor: worker admission, transport invocation and remote application have different observable orders. Choose whether cancellation returning must exclude a subsequent first Execute/HTTP dispatch, what happens to already issued work, and which actor can authorize a new resolving submission. Require one ordered history for each side of the proposed race, with an actually useful open-source completion and a closed-source unknown outcome that remains truthful.

A mere last-second database lookup would leave an interval between releasing the lock and the physical call. The proposed contract is falsified by any acknowledged closure followed by release of a previously checked worker that first enters the protected launch boundary afterward. A stronger requirement to prevent application of an already transmitted packet would require a cooperating endpoint or a different operation contract. Test the local fence independently of such a capability. Leave the adapter reconciliation policy and parent uncertainty encodings open until this authority has a specified meaning.
