# Prospective contract for F(T)

## Public operands and grant boundaries

One generation-one static parent P and two independently rooted resolvers operate in one Store namespace. P's first three instructions commit original invocation handles for L, R and T in that order. T is one representable closed Series 1 leaf, relay or fork, built with the original A/B candidates and literal requests. P gives the entry request to each original issuer and receives the frozen issuance reply on the invocation binding. A second bidirectional ordinary message edge per issuer carries the issuer's actual request ID and its echo while the issuer is open. The entry's first ordinary Request follows its bound entry receive. The caller forwards the ID to the designated resolver and echoes its acknowledgment to the issuer. The source selects that echo before End. Empty per-query service inputs can arrange a schedule. Neither source identity nor a world observation comes from a service input.

The proposed versioned pair binds two different emission locators of this static caller, two caller ports, two template entries and distinct identity obligations. Both directions use the same typed one-target record. A retained ID is data. Grants bind an existing resolver generation to a particular original effect emission of a particular admitted invocation member and a particular format-2 ledger endpoint. The usual current dispatch, exact wire/attempt, revocation, local drain and physical-read rules apply. Version-one and closed Series 1 templates preserve their prior identities and eligibility. Every actual grant, schema and permitted direction is checked independently of endpoint truth. [Phase 2C binding](../phase2c/01-runtime-design.md)

## Ordinary evidence and decisions

For each sequential resolver the report records the handed-off target, first compatible found witness with its actual logical query ID and its mutation/total, and the outcomes of the one or two queries actually selected. The relevant candidate fields are validity, disposition, exact target, status, adoption decision, per-query adopted flag and receipt values. Failure defaults carry no application evidence. Once set, a compatible witness survives a later duplicate, provisional absence or transport failure. Incompatible positives and an explicit conflict latch a conflict alongside historical evidence. Exact schemas and register names are bound by the emitted artifacts. The first logical query that supplies compatible evidence can itself observe an existing result. An adopted flag continues to refer to that particular query.

P awaits L's invocation, R's invocation, then the original T invocation, then the two ordinary reports in role order. Selections can consume an earlier delivery on a later turn. The correct report port/source/obligation and the role's original target are both necessary. Success requires the two actual original envelopes with the supported reasons, present truthful issuance for the original IDs, compatible authenticated positive evidence for both, and a legal shared-world pair of receipts. The positive controlled world starts empty and has exactly one application per role, deltas L=1 and R=2 (also L=R=1 as an identity control). If L applies first, its receipt is (1,dL) and R's is (2,dL+dR). Reverse these role assignments for the other order. A failed original interaction must retain its actual failed projection and recorded outcome even if the later report proves application. All selected tree leaves/diagnoses remain role-indexed. An invalid operand may prevent success; it cannot be used to bypass an outstanding join.

The answer, pure diagnosis and invalid joined report are private decisions. The final ordinary ACK to each designated resolver is a separate Send. Resolver termination requires its actual ACK selection. P's End, its root publication, the two original settlements, both resolver Ends and host Q are distinct observations. With a serviced intrinsically pending tree and two settled effects, P can already have selected the two original outcomes and be blocked on T. Both genuine resolver reports can be delivered and await consumption, with the resolvers awaiting their own ACKs. The pure tree's ordered pending frontier is inherited under its frame. With R unpublished, P cannot pass its R Await even when T and both reports are available.

## Prefix observations, relation and bounds

Relate a single retained Store prefix and its independently observed endpoint prefix to a reference history by their labelled projection. Direction is concrete to abstract. Name occurrences by namespace, original invocation and member/operation, logical query, and spent ordinal. Preserve immutable original interactions, ordered application records and snapshot identities, query retention, settlement, publication, message send/authority/admission/selection, actual parent branch and each root's state. Local pure steps can stutter between visible events only while their projection and costs agree. A retained refusal, lost opportunity, external mutation, conflict, fault, delivery refusal, or divergent pending frontier is visible. Volatile crash cuts require observed process/barrier evidence. The relation alone supplies no converse simulation or liveness theorem.

The cost coordinates count accepted invocation debits, created members, original mutation debits, logical lookup debits, spent physical read ordinals, original contacts, query contacts and applications, by root and by role where applicable. A debit is counted from its retained ledger, a contact from endpoint ingress and an application from its durable business row. Neither a buffered worker nor its lost reply refunds any coordinate. Preserve remaining budgets and maximum depth/capacity separately. The parent minimum common I/R is b(T)+2 and depth max(h(T),1); each issuer is a singleton. Leaf b=4, h=3; Relay adds one; Fork adds one to the sum of b and one to the maximum h. A two-leaf fork has I/R=11 and h=4. The two original mutations share their parent's effect allowance of two. The separate resolver roots have one or two logical effect debits and capacity one each. Positive original service uses global capacity two and parent capacity two. Each logical read has at most two physical spends. All compile, projection and Store bounds additionally have to fit the actual emitted programs. Use atomically consistent snapshots and genuine wire bytes. Reconciliation cannot generate an original contact.

Couple different recovery runs on the same original application order and receipts, original identities and accepted authority, fixed policies, tree assignment/request and explicitly declared response opportunities. The business comparison may forget logical query identity, ordinal, immediate adoption flag and differing costs only when separately retaining the full histories. A strict full-world-trace or exact-cost equivalence fails if recovery spends ordinal two. A new identified read may find an application a lost earlier absence did not observe.

## Completion premises and falsifiers

Require live exact participants, stable generations/permissions, admitted original work and service before source closure, an intact unchanged honest ledger, unique visible applications when the successful reads snapshot them, enough remaining debits and physical opportunities, writable storage, completed local accounting, and actual ordinary service. For the positive loss profile permit at most one pre-retention loss per logical query and a remaining eligible successful read. Independently deriving the parent's actual guard and final ACK ordering remains an obligation. For an already adopted sequential query ordinary Await follows settlement of original and query; a competing resolver's duplicate is insufficient to establish the first resolver's settlement.

The automatic all-roots argument must derive the sole pending internal delivery at each final ACK cut from exhausted prior internal sends and invocation deliveries, absence of eligible terminal subscriptions, and the retained PCs at the fully joined cut. The stock runner services an internal delivery and books its disposition before committing a ready instruction in that turn. A fresh owner with the same durable prefix must recover eligibility before End. Manual service can instead commit End before authorization. Capacity one can keep an original worker from admission until its source closes. Eventual application can come after every available negative read. Those schedules are compulsory attacks on a broader progress claim. A parent decision alone cannot discharge these obligations.
