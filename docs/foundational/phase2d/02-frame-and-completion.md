# The two-original frame

**Domain and claim.** Fix one fresh schema-5, single-owner Store namespace, generation one, an intact format-2 counter ledger initially empty, and the exact deployment emitted by `New`. Fix the literal Series 1 request at each leaf and one independently chosen original A or B there. Only finite terms constructed by Leaf, Relay and Fork are in the grammar. Require successful compilation, catalogue validation, schema/identity/SQLite representation and adequate encoded-state/sequence bounds for the *combined* term, including P's registers, its expanded validation and the resolver reports. A legal prefix admits ordinary public driver interleavings and the declared owner-loss and endpoint cuts. It neither edits records nor replaces a resolver program. Physical dispatch, progress and termination need additional premises below. This is a source-level argument. The finite graphs and experiments have their own smaller domains.

## Frame for the original Series 1 theorem

1. **Occurrence and routing.** P commits L, R, T at sequences 1, 2, 3 with ordinal zero before its first receive. T's request map passes its original literal fields unchanged. Invocation ID is a function of namespace and original operation (source, generation, sequence, ordinal). Each admitted member is named from its actual invocation and member role. Induction on the parent operation distinguishes each T descendant from the two original issuers, static resolvers and other occurrences, even for repeated template IDs and equal request values. Fresh noncolliding derived IDs, catalogue validation and distinct static IDs are placement conditions. Each pure receive/send still binds its own original caller/member/port. The two handoffs are installed only for the exact first and second admissions, with distinct obligations and caller ports. Static report/relay routes terminate at P and their designated resolver. Neither those grants nor the two read grants name a pure route or member. A pure template has no handoff, read or mutation instruction. Its retained bytes are the historical closed bytes. There is no new authority in an old deployment. [Tree constructors](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/tree.go) · [binding](01-binding.md) · [Store correspondence](../phase1t/02-store-correspondence.md)
2. **Shared ledger.** Label the Phase 1T proof's disjoint slots by placement. Add two fresh singleton issuer slots. Only P can emit their two original Invoke instructions. The remainder of its code emits no Invoke. Each issuer commits exactly one Request and no Invoke. Each resolver is static and outside the shared original root. Within T only a lawful first admission at the corresponding uncharged slot can spend it. Induction over commits therefore gives at most `b(T)+2` dynamic invocations and created runs on P, with depth at most `max(h(T),1)`. Causally, if k slots remain uncharged, spent plus k is at most the initial bound, independently of placement or service order. An A leaf leaves its helper slot unused. An all-B two-leaf fork actually uses eleven. Two original effect debits cover the two unique Requests even when dispatch later refuses. Each resolver's actual one or two logical requests debit its own budget, and each has at most two durable ordinal spends. Physical capacity is a separate gate. [Resource provision](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/deployment.go) · [Series 1 slot and representation argument](../phase1t/03-tree-theorem.md)
3. **Service and lifetime.** P remains open across T's creation, reply and all required selections in the legal unchanged-program positive prefix. A resolver echo is its actual received target, so P's apparent early echo-mismatch End cannot occur. Failed/closed siblings change neither another child's ownership chain nor its current scope, and there is no cancellation, retry, lifecycle directive or new sender inside the product. A stalled P waiting on L or R does not block an already admitted T descendant. Outside steps erase from the pure projection only when they leave local control, bindings, its owed service and available slot lower bound intact. An eligible private reduction, first admission, reply disposition, outcome delivery or select remains eligible until taken, or depends on the same finite prerequisites as in Series 1. Under service of those prerequisites, deleting outside steps cannot strand an enabled pure step at the end of a serviced execution. A finite outer source crash must reopen durably and service these same prerequisites. This does not hide a refused admission or an indefinitely unserviced suffix. Thus the Series 1 induction applies at this occurrence, with P as the actual live caller for T. It gives strong interior settlement on a completed T and the original ordered pending frontier on a genuinely pending T. It does not prove first dispatch of either original effect. [Pure proof and pending recursion](../phase1t/03-tree-theorem.md)
4. **Outer selection.** P awaits the saved L, R, T handles consecutively. Only afterwards does it select L and R reports. T's validator uses the disjoint path fields, matches original id and complement, checks zero or one row, and distinguishes candidate failure from missing reply. Its present/status/reason branch agrees with the original closed tree contract. A diagnosed T still needs all its original selections and the two business receipts. If T remains unpublished, a serviced state can instead contain both selected original outcomes, two delivered unconsumed reports, and two live resolvers at their ACK receives. If R remains unpublished, P stops sooner at R, even if T and both reports are available. No selection occurs just because its observation is present. ## Stable role evidence and useful completion

The issuing member receives its invocation request, then requests the controlled increment at sequence two. The target echoed and forwarded is that original request handle. The exact grant requires its original admission, source generation/member, prepared wire/key/endpoint and resolver identity. Eligible read admission checks actual original closure and local drain. Successful targeted Q is a separate host witness to those conditions. A Q acknowledgment does not enter the program or grant a read. An already retained original that still owes settlement prevents a fresh read under the original drain check. Reserving an ordinal makes an irreversible local charge even before transport. The endpoint's read path observes its ledger and has no business apply transition. A loss can therefore sacrifice an ordinal or a snapshot while preserving the original ordered application rows. No local step can refund a charged opportunity or synthesize an unseen application or final negative. The immutable import decision inserts a missing supported result only when its own eligible proof verifies; it returns existing or duplicate against a retained result. Ordinary transport failures remain historical results. [Reservation](../../../internal/executor/receipt_read.go) · [atomic result and settlement](../../../internal/executor/receipt_result.go)

Induct over each designated resolver's one or two ordered Await selections. Before the first, its witness and conflict flags are false. Only a valid found observation for its received target seeds its bounded one-row memory, saving the actual logical handle and the selected target, adoption, mutation and total. An equal later found compares the same three receipt fields and leaves the earlier query identity and adoption unchanged. A different positive, wrong target or explicit conflict latches conflict. A later unsuccessful transport or absence leaves the witness intact, with its own normalized result retained separately. The ordinary Send serializes the first witness together with each actual query outcome. The unused second slot in the one-query program has typed defaults and an empty id. This induction assumes the exact designated program, typed ordinary inputs and the grant checks. An altered program in an attack fixture does not grant it trusted witness authority. [Resolver](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/resolver.go)

For positive completion require pre-closure admission and service of **both** originals (including actual world ingress), exactly one application for each of their distinct keys, an intact honest ledger with those applications visible at the successful reads, original revocation and local drain, enough debits and actual physical service. Every positive role, including an existing direct or failed original, requires the exact read and import grant. The Store checks that grant before classifying existing evidence, and P rejects the denied adoption class. A missing original uses the unobserving issuer and the controlled success policy, with an eligible read retaining its unique found result. An observing issuer must receive its actual direct or transport-error result, select it, send its genuine issuance reply and End before its post-closure read. At most one pre-retention loss per relied-on logical read leaves the required next opportunity; service it successfully while current authority and useful snapshot still hold. Extra reads may productively refresh or return a retained transport error after an earlier positive. Require working storage including successful local accounting and ordinary message and selection service. Neither eventual application nor capacity two alone entails these timed conditions.

An importing query settles the original before settling itself. Its selectable observation follows its own settlement, so its sequential resolver cannot select a later duplicate first. The existing-result gate above covers the already-retained alternative. The actual scope can consequently settle and publish each original invocation, and the resolver can forward its evidence independent of P's consumption. This implication is specific to one sequential resolver per original. Concurrent pre-admitted readers can separate first import, duplicate-report selection, publication and original settlement. An outstanding sticky accounting failure breaks the progress premise. Publication is never the test for repayment.

With the supplied positive premises, the required L and R envelopes are present for their own handles. The unobserving source reports issued, the observing success reports validated with the original receipt, or the observing failure reports transport_error with its immutable failed envelope. The role verifier checks this specific combination, issued target, retained positive, nonempty originating query, matching fields of that selected query, admissible adoption and absence of conflict. It guards presence before reading optional fields. Exact route and target cannot exchange L and R even under equal deltas. On an initially empty counter with just these applications the first receipt is (1,first delta), the second (2,sum); either role may be first. These are precisely the two order branches of P. Thus a completed all-answer T gives applied, a legal diagnosis gives applied_tree_diagnosed with actual role classifications; the latter's parent terminal is failed. This derives the private decision without requiring the *last* read to adopt. In a hostile/replaced world the preconditions fail. Full result provenance and expenditure need not coincide across owner loss.

## Final ordinary ACKs under the stock runner

Fix the joined suffix of the exact programs in an ordinary successfully accounted Run, or reopen at an identified committed ACK cut after the preceding automatically serviced turns. No concurrent manual delivery, outstanding pre-acknowledgment split transaction from an earlier crash, unrelated actor or injected transport is part of this suffix. The following derives the turn predicate without relying on fairness between arbitrary queued messages.

* An already selected ordinary message must have had an admission. Under these runner calls DeliverMessage and AcknowledgeMessage both finish before selectReady and the following Commit. Earlier identity relay/echo messages and both reports have therefore been bookkept by the first ACK. Bound replies in this deployment are serviced by the same two calls. For a completed pure tree, the strong interior settlement argument above gives all necessary reply dispositions and invocation deliveries, including failed/absent leaves. Each accepted pure Send is a bound reply needed by its selected child outcome. The two issuers' captured bound issuance replies are likewise decided before P selects their outcomes. All three outer invocation outcomes have deliveries before their selections. There are no unused side-message instructions in the tree or issuer code. The exact constructors install message routes; they install no terminal subscription and have no lifecycle instructions. Terminal obligations with empty destination sets add no delivery. The designated resolvers each have sent their report and can now only receive, compare and End.
* Consider pendingDeliveries after P commits its first final Send. Among internal work only that new message lacks bookkeeping. The two read-worker accounting stages are external to this choice. Run chooses its one internal delivery before committing any ready instruction. The open P and waiting resolver pass first source authority and recipient admission on their exact route, and the runner acknowledges before another ordinary commit. P's second Send cannot precede that service. The exact argument now applies again: selecting the first ACK can generate only a compare or End at that resolver, with no new message or terminal destination. Neither outcome publication nor root settlement enqueues another internal Send. Both ACKs are therefore serviced while P is open. The recipients can select their matching targets, pass their comparison, End and publish their roots under actual continued service. P's End and root publication are separate facts, as is host Q.
* After an acknowledged owner loss exactly between committing the sole outstanding ACK and its first service, Open retains the intents, prior acknowledgments, eligibility and open projections. No new message is committed by acquiring an owner epoch. Its first serviced turn encounters the same single internal delivery before any ready End. A split cut *inside* earlier delivery/acknowledgment, a sticky I/O error or a manual caller which commits End first falls outside this positive suffix. In the latter case the first authorization records source_closed and the resolver can remain open despite P's valid decision.

The process fixtures also count the outstanding message, undelivered invocations, lifecycle intents and unsatisfied terminal destinations directly from both durable ACK cuts. They then distinguish delivery, acknowledged transport, ordinary ACK selection and each root publication. This checked predicate does not follow from arbitrary eventual service. ## Relation and proof category

Map a concrete prefix by its actual typed original operations, role grants, binding, read/attempt ordinals, exact endpoint applications/snapshots, retained decisions, receipts, settlements, delivered and selected observations, and root/control boundaries. Count invocation/member/effect/read debits from their owning rows, ordinal spends from the retained reservations, contacts from the endpoint and mutations from the business rows. Holds and owner death need separately witnessed process cuts. Administrative stutter is allowed only between consecutive visible changes; neither a lost observation nor a changed cost is a silent step. The source argument above covers the legal restricted frame. Embedding a concrete schedule into **an enumerated graph** additionally requires its named finite profile's starting conditions, event ordering and ablations. The graphs do not enumerate all interleavings of an arbitrary tree or synchronize two ledgers by their final total. Comparisons across recovery couple the original keys, wire, order, endpoint and actual per-role receipts. A business projection may omit the consumed physical ordinal and immediate adoption metadata only while the complete histories are retained. This is a one-directional restricted safety and conditional completion argument. A symmetric contextual equivalence, arbitrary caller nesting, hostile ledger fidelity and recovery from unlimited pre-retention losses are open.
