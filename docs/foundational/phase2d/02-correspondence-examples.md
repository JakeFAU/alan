# Relating a concrete observation to the reference

The reference protocol and its directed projection precede enumeration. Its finite products have explicit scheduling and initialization restrictions. The independent raw audit interprets SQL/program/world data; the independent graph checker interprets the reference export. The notes below identify where their resulting observations can be compared. An agreement at a sink does not establish that every event from a concrete run traverses that particular finite graph from row zero.

For the public programs, a resolver's query is named by its committed logical Request and actual selected effect observation. The model records its role and query slot and attaches each spent ordinal and independent world snapshot to that slot. A physical ordinal, a lookup snapshot number and the originating logical query ID are three different fields. Send, first permission, recipient admission and ACK bookkeeping come from their separate tables or process cuts; selection requires the recipient's committed history with that exact observation. The abstract role/port checks do not prove that the implementation bytes implement the grant. That check uses the independently recovered namespace, source, member and emitted-operation identities and the exact wire/endpoint in the raw audit.

The broader source-level relation is conditional on the stated identity, authority, representability, service and closed-tree premises. The finite products neither implement the reducer nor enumerate a simultaneous product of all pure reduction steps, tree placements, read retries, original losses and message interleavings. No full end-to-end trace inclusion into their finite union is claimed.
