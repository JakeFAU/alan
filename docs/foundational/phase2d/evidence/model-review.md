## Why the frame and ACK statements have these bounds

I checked the construction against the original Series 1 tree and the new parent and resolver programs. Distinct operation handles at original parent sequences 1, 2 and 3 establish the three outer occurrences. Recursive left/right paths and actual invocation/member bindings keep repeated literal IDs and reused templates distinct. The two issuer slots are disjoint from the pure tree's charged slots, the resolver roots are separate, and the pure constructors still call the original A/B, Leaf, Relay and Fork constructors. Thus the shared original root needs two additional I/R slots over the Series 1 inductive bound, with the same tree-depth bound or one for the issuers. Root headroom, representation, exact caller lifetime and actual service of continuously eligible pure prerequisites are necessary. A pending pure child can leave a buffered sibling or two reports that the waiting parent cannot select. The finite exports seed pure histories and costs at selected cuts. They do not implement or model-check arbitrary tree reductions. [Series 1 theorem](../../phase1t/03-tree-theorem.md) · [Construction](../../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/tree.go) · [Provision](../../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/deployment.go)

For the exact positive joined suffix, every earlier report and handoff consumed on a stock-runner turn already has its message bookkeeping, all selected invocation outcomes have their own deliveries, and the completed pure component's stronger interior-settlement property accounts for its remaining children. The actual programs have no other ordinary sends enabled in that suffix, no terminal subscriber and no lifecycle command. A new final ACK is then the sole pending internal delivery. Ordinary service retains first authority and books delivery while P is open, before any more ordinary work. This applies again to the second ACK. On reopening exactly after sole-ACK committal the same durable bookkeeping and open source remain. At a cut inside earlier disposition/acknowledgment, with unrelated traffic, with storage failure or under manual End-before-first-permission, this argument does not establish the same completion. The resolver still has to actually select and compare its matching ACK and End. Application order is shared across both originals; a matching final sum cannot identify the first role. Positive comparisons can preserve the two decisions under matched genuine world histories while a lost snapshot costs an extra ordinal and query contact. The full provenance/cost traces remain different. The inconsistent-guard-input profile changes a selected value synthetically and the wrong-role profile changes a report operand. Neither is an honest ledger history. Competing X is an ablation ending at X's selected query, with no claim about that extra root's termination. The directed frame argument is conditional on these identity, authority, lifetime, service and response premises. Arbitrary effectful contexts, bidirectional full-trace refinement, unlimited crash recovery and the adequacy of the symbolic wire encoding to an arbitrary deployment remain open.
