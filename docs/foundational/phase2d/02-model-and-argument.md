# Joint reference, cost and component argument

The reference has two original operation identities and one ordered ledger. Safety is an implication from a legal retained prefix and the corresponding external facts to the abstract observations. Completion is conditional on a particular service and lifetime domain. The [prospective domain](02-model-domain.md), [frozen contract](01-contract.md) and [program map](01-programs.md) define the operands. Binding bytes, actual scheduler choices, and replay/process evidence require the separate Store experiment. There is no converse trace theorem.

## Finite transition relation

`Profiles` supplies 158 bounded cases. The first four are launch products, the next twenty are read/join products, the next 128 are fork products (two literal-ID choices for every placement and ordered mode pair) and the last six contain Relay. Each JSONL line is `{Profile,Tree,Rows}`; row 0 contains the entire initial state and every row contains `{ID,State,Class,Edges}` with labelled `{Action,To}` successors. `Explore` enumerates labelled alternatives before successor deduplication. `Check` checks every reached edge and eliminates sinks in reverse to verify that the finite productive graph is acyclic. Its suffix rank is zero at a sink and one plus the maximum successor rank elsewhere. Optional operator choices and waiting without service do not constitute charged productive steps. A finite prefix or an arbitrary timeout cannot establish that the Store has serviced all eligible work. [Model and bounds](../../../research/effectfulcomposition/domain.go) · [Transition relation](../../../research/effectfulcomposition/transition.go) · [Join](../../../research/effectfulcomposition/join.go) · [Enumerator/checks](../../../research/effectfulcomposition/explore.go)

* **Identities and shared costs.** Side 0/1 means the exact L/R identity tuple, including namespace, issuer/operation, ledger/key/wire, schema/policy and grant. A logical query is its resolver and index. X's snapshot has a separate symbolic query tag 3 on target L. Spent physical ordinals are attached to their own query. `World[i]` contains role, application number and cumulative total. Snapshot `Serial` counts all committed lookup observations in order; `At` counts applications at that instant. `Log` survives local losses, including an unretained earlier response. No authored program accesses the ghost snapshot or physical ordinals. Root costs are decomposed into the two original lanes belonging to P, the pure slot set also belonging to P, and each independent reader's charged work. In `Cost[0/1]` I/R/Mut/Post/Apply refer to the respective original lane, Read/Ord/Get to that lane's resolver. `Cost[2]` holds T's actual accepted I/R, `Cost[3]` extra-reader expenditure. The integer tuple is `(I,R,Mut,Read,Ord,Post,Get,Apply)`. A contact requires actual ingress; loss before contact consumes Ord alone. Neither observation nor adoption may increase Apply or Post.
* **Launch slices.** The two original requests and the identity handshake are taken as genuine fixed prefixes. Both sources can finish on the remaining ordinary branch. The model conditions on independent service of the tree's actual slot admissions before this cut. With capacity two, each source may close after its own admission and state transitions distinguish entry, ingress, application and H. The gate and L are separate. Two service orders yield different role-indexed receipts; equal deltas still leave distinct occurrences. Capacity one starts with L holding the slot, while R has either an unexamined request or a *historical first permission only*. R may End before service. A refused first permission is followed by a local `source_denied` result and its settlement, with zero mutation debit. If first permission was granted earlier, a later allowed nested reservation consumes one semantic debit; current gate refusal on the closed source supplies no operation result. The launch slice does not run subsequent publication/query service. The `cap-one-granted` prefix uses the separately callable first-permission operation; it need not be the stock automatic ordering. `Q` here samples an already closed/drained original after the servicing of applicable refusals. An initial candidate incorrectly erased the distinction in durable outcome and seeded R's reservation before a worker slot was free. The rejected candidate is retained. [Rejected graph and source](evidence/model/rejected-first-denial)
* **Read slices.** Each initializes a single joint ledger. Except in the declared delayed-R cases, both authentic packets already applied in L/R or R/L order and their sources are closed/drained. `direct-existing` and `retained-failure` already carry a settled and published immutable original L interaction. Other original local results are missing. R's delayed packet has been transmitted and counted as a contact and can still apply despite source Q and owner loss. L’s designated reader is serviced through report bookkeeping before R’s for these cuts. An ordinary Request, allowed reservation, read-ordinal spend, lookup POST ingress, snapshot, atomic query retention/import, original and query settlement, ordinary selection, and report transactions have distinct steps. A fresh eligible spend checks the live owner, prior original drain, existing unsettled original results and remaining ordinals. A prior imported record can enable publication before its settlement. The adopting query cannot settle before its original settles. A duplicate never inherits an outstanding importer's settlement. The competing slice first admits X and L before either import, fixes L as importer, then permits the two reader completions to interleave. X's own selection is observed; termination/publication of that extra root is outside this ablation.
* **Durable cuts.** `absent-refresh` applies R after selection of the first absence and before the next named query contacts. `late-application` permits it only after both selected absences. `loss-refresh` kills after the first unretained absence and permits the accepted packet to apply before the second ordinal snapshots; either the dead-owner or new-owner interval may contain that application. `intact-found` and `lost-found` use identical application order, business receipts, original policy, placement, logical requests and intact endpoint. The latter alone loses R's first response before retention, requiring ordinal 2 and another lookup POST. `exhaust` kills after each of two spends and before either send. `import-kill`, `selection-kill` and `fresh-ack` use one loss at the named durable cut. The model never wipes the ledger or resets debits at Open. Positive results require an eventual remaining good read *while the receipt is visible*. Finite earlier absences do not become positive when an accepted packet later applies.
* **Joined suffix and negative controls.** Each selected query occupies its own slot. First compatible found evidence plus query ID persists through a later duplicate/error. The explicit `invalid-role` and `incompatible` profiles are synthetic guard-input ablations: one places the other genuine role's proof and target in a report; the other changes a later found value at consumption to test the consistency latch. They are excluded from honest world/provenance claims. Empty or unsuccessful observations cannot provide positive proof. We service both ordinary report Sends, first permissions, admissions and bookkeeping, and each currently possible original publication before entering the serial parent-join slice. This fixes a feasible order of *independent* work; it does not claim an automatic Run must delay all original selections to that moment. The single parent selection count identifies the exact consumed prefix `[InvL, InvR, T, ReportL, ReportR]`. After all five, `decision` checks the selected operands and legal role pair; it cannot drive any endpoint choice. Each final ACK is separately committed, first-authorized or denied, admitted/refused and bookkept. Selection, resolver End and root publication each have their own transition. Manual sends can outrun first authorization. The automatic and fresh-owner slices use the service argument below. [Report memory and role checks](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/parent.go) · [Sequential resolver](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/resolver.go)
* **Pure suffix.** The grid starts with actual slot costs, an already serviced pure component boundary, both original publications, and two sequentially sourced settled positive reports delivered but not yet consumed. The pure leaf projection contains original input ID/value, classification and optional complement under the same ID. Every placement gives the same role-indexed product for fixed literal requests. The actual fork frame costs (including both issuers) are 9/10/10/11 across AA/AB/BA/BB. At a pending left the fork is at its first child await; a nonpending right is buffered. If only the right is pending, left is selected. Completed rows present both fields, including diagnosed siblings; the typed product is not just a scalar status. Model slots and symbol bindings assume a legal deployment and do not test field-size limits or the byte-level decoder. The `slow-read` cut includes a pure private commit while a spent lookup is held, conditioned on a separately held purely local continuation. The model does not claim the cost-free step creates new invocation slots.

**Schedule manifest v3.** L and R refer to designated reader lanes unless prefixed by P. Fixed construction prefixes include all authentic handoff selections. In launch `cap-one` L is admitted and R has not been authorized; `cap-one-granted` differs only by R's historical grant. Each gate/preparation requires a free physical slot. Positive cap-two closes each individual source only after its own admission or later stage. In the read products L through report-booked precedes R's reader/report/publication actions. An original can publish as soon as its result exists (including before settlement) while its lane is enabled. Joining begins only after both reports are bookkept and every presently known original is published. X can request only when L q1 is spent; L q1 contact waits for X to spend; X's contact waits for L's import. After import their settlement, L's original publication and X's completion may interleave. Parent join waits for X's selection. The extra reader's slot belongs to its own root. `TreeStep` starts true except in `slow-read`, where the pure private commit is enabled when L q1 holds its spent opportunity, and lookup ingress waits for the commit. In `loss-refresh` the independently accepted original packet may apply after the first loss while the owner is dead or after Open. In `import-kill` the importer's settlement waits on one forced owner loss, but its original publication may precede that loss. The parent suffix stops for the forced `selection-kill` immediately after the first original selection or `fresh-ack` after the first ACK Send. Reports and original publications were already serviced by those suffix cuts. `manual-ack` frees P to send the second ACK once the first has been sent and to End once both have been sent. On a stock-run suffix, any pending final ACK (`sent`, `allowed` or `delivered`) forces its next delivery stage as the sole ordinary-service successor, before **any actor** can make another private Commit. Root publication is a separate post-Commit category. Immediately after an End in this fully joined suffix the ending root already has a ready outcome. The same turn visits all roots with `settleRoots`, so the model makes its publication the next step before the following ordinary turn. These two priorities are removed in the manual profile. Outside those forced stages the model allows a choice among eligible actors rather than reproducing the exact round-robin cursor. [Turn order](../../../internal/executor/runner.go) · [Root service](../../../internal/executor/composition_root.go) The last `late-application` packet remains independently enabled after both negative selections even at an already blocked parent.

These are complete outgoing relations for the **declared slices**, not for arbitrary public schedules. Early message transfer and exact binding are fixed authentic inputs in the model and checked by the public construction. The concrete projection follows each committed invocation to its admitted member's actual request, its committed identity Send, P’s matching Receive/relay, the resolver’s relay Receive/echo, P’s receive/issuer ACK, and that source’s matching ACK selection. A committed ordinary Send may fail at first authorization; this fixed prefix cannot be inferred from intent creation alone. The outside-world portion couples the actual application order and role receipt bytes, and query snapshots are joined by actual query and ordinal. Local private tests or representation steps may stutter when their projected facts and costs remain equal. Grouped prefix events and productive costs remain visible in concrete evidence; byte histories and read numbers need not agree across different executions. The small graphs do not establish a full runtime/compiler simulation. [Public construction](01-public-construction.md) · [Role bindings](01-binding.md)

## Conditional source argument

**Role and evidence safety.** Assume the declared immutable namespace, verified prepared wire, ledger/protocol, original effect emission and grants, trusted dispatch and validator, single local owner and intact transactional ledger. The paired binder grants only the named sender/receiver generations, fixed caller emission and the corresponding typed obligation. The message observation binds its real source/obligation; P also compares the target from the original identity handoff to its original's typed issuance reply, designated resolver report, witness and selected query. Equal payloads/programs therefore cannot identify an occurrence. The shared ledger's mutating application transaction yields an injective application number and running total in its own serialization order. `Lookup` reads matching wire/key evidence and appends a read observation in a distinct table, without updating business applications. `bindReceiptRead` binds one resolver and its original; a new physical read spends a persisted ordinal before contact. `decideRead` distinguishes existing original evidence, a supported import of a unique compatible receipt, an equivalent duplicate, conflict, denied adoption and unsupported policy. `recordReadResult` installs a supported missing original and the query evidence in the same transaction. Neither new evidence nor H overwrites the immutable failed original. These facts give prefix safety under the trusted domain independently of finite service. The local application inference additionally needs an authentic matching receipt. Read-only `found` is a knowledge fact; the original invocation can still remain unpublished. [Bound read](../../../internal/executor/receipt_read.go) · [Import and settlement](../../../internal/executor/receipt_result.go) · [Independent ledger transaction](../../../internal/fixture/ledger.go)

**Memory, settlement and the ordinary join.** Induct on the one or two selected queries: initial witness is empty. On a valid compatible first found, `remember` stores its observed values and originating query ID. An equivalent positive leaves them intact, another incompatible positive or explicit conflict latches conflict, and a failed later query leaves the earlier witness intact while occupying its own report slot. This induction assumes the Phase 2C atomic proof/wire binding. It does not equate an unsuccessful transport with nonapplication. In the designated sequential policy, an adopted query's own observation exists only after its original and query settlements are serviced; ordinary Await is later still. A subsequent duplicate from this resolver follows that Await. For existing direct/failure evidence the fresh spend gate rejects an unsettled original result or a still-live original handler; an actual completed positive read therefore has a drain prefix. With two readers already admitted, one can record `adopted` and be awaiting settlement when the second sees `duplicate` and settles its own query. Publication may use the original record in this interval. A duplicate plus publication alone cannot certify the first reader's settlement. Under the positive premises, both real parent invocation envelopes and T are available, both reports are delivered while their sources remain live, and P selects all five in the emitted order. An issued-only envelope has the original target and truthful issued marker; a directly observing source contributes its validated fields or genuine failed/zero interaction. P compares the reported witness to the referenced selected query slot, checks role, adoption class, conflicts and both ledger receipts against either legal order, and checks all pure leaves. These conditions derive `applied` for all answers or `applied_tree_diagnosed` for a present mixed/failure product. They do not prescribe which read adopts or assert success from a query flag. A pending tree leaves both selected originals with unconsumed reports; a missing R leaves P at InvR even if T and reports are available. [Exact program operands](01-programs.md)

**Frame for every legal T and independent A/B assignment.** Take the actual Series 1 Leaf/J/e/S/h, Relay and Fork bytes, literal request vector and bindable pure templates; require their typed sizes, fixed schemas, gas, catalogues, call depth, maximum projection, observations, query reports, Store output limits and generated identities all fit this *larger* construction. Use disjoint occurrence slots for the two issuer calls and all pure calls. P commits the three original handles at its fixed sequences before joining. The pure descendants have only their own bound parent/child ports, no business capability, handoff or resolver grant and no opportunity to consume issuer/query messages. Conversely the paired grants bind only the two issuer emission locators and the two provisioned readers. Each admitted original in this frame debits its own slot once; two singleton issuers add two slots and two potential mutation debits. The only other new semantic effects are the separately rooted readers' one or two requests. Thus a common remaining I/R of at least b(T)+2 and depth at least max(h(T),1) cannot exclude a lawful pure admission; an A placement can leave a helper slot unused. This induction also requires actual sufficient physical scheduling capacity/service for original calls: semantic headroom does not create a worker slot. T's owning caller remains open until its actual T selection: normal program control cannot reach P's decision/End before that instruction under the genuine handoff preconditions. Pure frames cannot change ledger data; blocking on R or T does not revoke the independently rooted resolvers. Each productive local step of the pure subtree has exactly the Series 1 enabling prerequisites under these hypotheses. Frame away unrelated steps at unchanged pure projections while retaining total expenditure separately. With eventual service of all productive pure and message prerequisites, the original theorem applies by induction to T, giving the role-indexed product or its specified ordered frontier, for every independent assignment. Without enough residual schedule/representation resources or with an external lifecycle revocation of this frame, this transport of the theorem does not apply. The old pure theorem itself does not supply a business evidence rule, issuer pre-A lifetime, two-edge communication behavior, ACK progress, or equality of exact costs. [Series 1 open theorem and representability conditions](../phase1t/03-tree-theorem.md) · [Frame constructor](../../../examples/effectfulcomposition/tree.go)

**ACK service and restarts.** Condition on the exact completed program suffix with one designated sequential resolver per original, no extra eligible lifecycle/terminal/ordinary input, genuine matching sender/receivers still open, productive transactions and no concurrent driver changing the turn. The necessary internal routes can be enumerated by kind. All captured invocation outcomes in the pure and original footprint are delivered before their actual selections. Earlier generic handoff and report messages were authorized, admitted and marked delivered by the stock runner before a subsequent ordinary Commit could consume them. The selected pure products include all required original bound replies with service completed; there are no unaccounted surplus sends in these source programs. There are no subscribed terminal destinations in this deployment and no authored lifecycle request. The two original records and all selected logical query outcomes have settled in the sequential positive domain. Therefore immediately after the next ordinary ACK Send commit its own delivery is the sole pending internal delivery. On its next iteration `Run` handles an eligible internal item using `deliverOrdinary` **before** it commits a ready private instruction. The source remains open at this authorization and the resolver blocks on this ACK; normal permission, admission and delivery bookkeeping can finish, independently of which ready run next commits. Apply the same reasoning to the second ACK. Durable retained commitments and missing `message_acknowledgments` give the same eligibility to a fresh owner after a loss immediately after either Send; actual active service, successful storage and a still-open resolver are necessary. Genuine selected ACK permits each resolver to End. P's final conditional and End can then publish its root separately. Manual supported commits can emit both ACKs, decide/terminate before first authorization, and leave even a correctly decided and published parent with a resolver waiting. Prior acceptance of another message is no permit for this one. ## Evidence and limits

The corrected export contains 8,456 states, 12,454 labelled edges and 167 sinks in 158 profiles. The 128 fork profiles split into eight successful, 64 diagnosed and 56 pending sinks; the latter divide into 32 awaiting left and 24 awaiting right. This doubles the 64 placement/mode grid by the equal/distinct ID comparator. No counts imply concrete runtime executions. Ten shortest within-profile adverse traces include the eight required overbroad claims, the extra competing-settlement claim, and both cap-one refusal layers. Independent review rejected the intermediate stock-turn projection. At state 75 of `intact-found`, L’s resolver could execute its ACK-selection Commit while R’s new ACK was awaiting first authorization. The unchanged stock runner services that sole internal delivery first. The new regression failed at that exact vertex. The corrected model enforces service priority over every actor’s ordinary commit and services each already-ready root publication in the ending turn. The manual negative remains. [Rejected graph, source and regression](evidence/model/rejected-ack-service) · [Regression output](evidence/model/rejected-ack-service/regression-red.txt)

The initial build RED demonstrated a missing harness only. The witnesses refute substantive claims with reached transitions. The candidate correction adds the durable first denial and labels only the designated three roots as terminated in the X boundary. Snapshot identities, state comparisons and costs are all available independently of aggregate counts.
