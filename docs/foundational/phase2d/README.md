# Phase 2D · Two original effects on a shared ledger

One ordinary caller owns two issuing children and an original Series 1 pure tree. Two independently rooted resolvers can recover qualified evidence for their own original operations. The research question is what that caller can truthfully decide, how much recovery costs, and what further service lets both resolvers terminate.
