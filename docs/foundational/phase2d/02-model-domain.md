# Prospective finite reference domain

This domain is written before enumeration and before the construction gate authorizes model implementation. The research question is whether the actual caller can join two *specific* original invocations, stable resolver evidence and the original pure outcome on a shared counter, including what survives packet delay and owner loss. Evidence for one role cannot discharge another role's original interaction. A successful business decision does not certify every resolver's termination. The direction of comparison is from a single retained Store and its independent ledger to the reference projection.

## Fixed identities and limits

One namespace, one generation of the static caller P and the two designated resolver roots; two disjoint issuer members, immutable original handles/keys L and R, one shared initially zero ledger, one irrevocable accepted packet and at most one increment per role in the positive domain. No mutable grant, transfer or Retry. Default deltas (1,2); the equal-value comparator uses (1,1). Dedicated resolver reads are sequential per role, one or two distinct logical queries per original and at most two durable opportunities per query. A third distinct reader occurs only in the pre-admitted competing-import slice. Permit at most two process losses in the two-ordinal exhaustion control, otherwise at most one. An optional read transport failure is a retained unsuccessful result. A thrown-away pre-retention observation may consume another ordinal, contact and snapshot. No fault injects an invented receipt in a genuine positive family.

For the pure grid use four independent A/B placements × all sixteen ordered pairs of answer/fail/absent/pending, request L=(id 1,value 0) and R=(id 1 or 2,value 1). Include equal and distinct literal IDs, retained per-slot handles and semantic spending. Add Relay/Fork profiles with answer, diagnosed and pending outcomes. A Leaf J has three dynamic members for A, four for B, Relay adds one and Fork adds one to the sum. Two singleton issuer admissions are additional. Positive common fork allowance is I=R=11, depth 4, E=2. Positive capacity is global/P=2 and one per resolver. Cap-one, one-less and representability controls do not inherit the sufficient-resource conclusion. Internal ports, representation headroom and branch/version identities need source and Store evidence.

## Bounded products and declared cuts

Use focused slices of **one** L/R/ledger protocol, never combine outcomes drawn from independent worlds. Each slice has a declared already-serviced common prefix or explicit barriers; no state-count, graph-depth or elapsed-time cutoff is allowed. Publish the initial facts and every eligible outgoing action at each reached vertex. A scheduler restriction imposed by a particular slice is a profile hypothesis, not an invariant of general `Store.Run`.

## Observable projection, costs and service

Correlate every actual observation through immutable namespace, original invocation/role-member and operation, resolver generation, logical query and physical ordinal. A pure occurrence is bound by its caller and committed operation, independently of its request data. Keep ordered ledger applications and snapshots (including historical absence), retained original/query outcomes and authenticated proof/import, original and query settlement, each Send/authorization/admission/selection, parent private decision, root publications, and resolver status. An opaque finite symbol for a byte binding requires an independently decoded full-byte equality in the Store experiment. Private instruction stretches may stutter only if they preserve every projected fact and cost; a changed ordinal, refused permission, new contact, received message or pending frontier is observable. A crash discards only the specified volatile cut, never a durable ordinal or accepted endpoint obligation. Equal business outcomes under coupled role receipt order, original envelope, request and placement are scoped projections; full-cost/full-provenance equivalence can fail.

Cost vector `(I,R,E_mut,E_read,Ord,Contact_mut,Contact_read,Applications)` counts actual accepted invocations/members, mutation/read semantic debits, spent ordinals, endpoint mutation/read contacts and applied original increments. Attribute them by role/root, retain remaining headroom and report the starting prefix costs. The reference checks cumulative monotonicity and derives costs from transition events and charged original identities, not from the parent's answer. Bound output includes all reached states/edges, labelled shortest witnesses for the eight assignment REDs, role/report/ACK eligibility, terminal versus serviced-pending distinctions and a rank for internal service. Productive automatic service, externally scheduled releases, claims from a finite model, concrete experiments and the conditional parametric source argument are distinct.
