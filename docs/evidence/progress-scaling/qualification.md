## Scope

The runner preserves mandatory cancellation, join and accounting before
releasing the owner. Task 2's retained tests compare static and fallback
execution, validate invalid and reached malformed evidence, exercise
limits and cancellation, and coordinate cross-method exclusion and Close.
Its unrelated corrupt-admission witness still produces the established
full-Run EOF diagnostic. Progress can advance other valid work. Full Run,
InspectExecution, Replay and fresh Open retain their corresponding
diagnostics. The original report tests and frozen report oracle remain
unchanged.

In the same-seed qualification, a real public weather executable creates
a closed Store, and each arm starts from a copy of that exact seed.
Both arms use the same endpoint and scripted wire behavior within each
scenario. Each arm advances four positions, submitting identical
protected approvals twice for positions two through four, then closes
and reopens twice after each position. The reopen calls use the opposite
method. The test compares the typed snapshot, paged history, Replay,
series view, three-field execution result, approval results and
independent provider journal at 16 matching cuts per scenario. A
third-position refusal retains its original selected rejection. A socket
close after acceptance leaves the third unresolved and without a
retained result. The separately approved fourth retains its own
authorization, contact and result. The fifth through eighth gain no
approval, effect or selection.

The crash campaign uses acknowledged boundaries and real SIGKILL plus
reap. The archive hook copies main, WAL and SHM and hashes the source
before and after copying, immediately after reap. It retains independent
weather consumer builds, bound input copies, reader output and process
witnesses. The same retained-result crash cut supplies a full resume
and a progress resume. Each consumes the original result and preserves
its original IDs with one provider contact across the original
execution and both resumes. Local dispatch accounting is tracked
separately from provider contact count.

These are local single-host observations. A bounded reported
opportunity and a recorded local admission give limits on possible
contacts. The controlled fixture directly records its own contacts.
Quiescence and a lost reply carry no remote-finality or exactly-once
assurance. Repeated global validation and requested evidence traversal
still have measurable cost. [Measurement and attribution](results.md)
